About Klarweg

German, made clear.

Most German courses ask Hindi-speaking learners to memorise rules in a language that already feels foreign. Klarweg does the opposite: it makes the structure of German visible, explains it in the language you think in, and treats you like the capable adult you are.

The problem

German isn't hard. It's hidden.

German word order moves in ways English and Hindi don't. The verb jumps to the end. Cases change the articles. To a beginner it looks random — so they memorise, forget, and conclude they're "bad at languages."

They're not. The structure was simply never shown to them. Every German sentence has a clear architecture — it's just invisible in black text on a white page.

The method

Colour carries the grammar.

Klarweg gives every grammatical role one fixed colour. Subject is blue, verb is red, object is green, time is purple, place is orange. The colour never changes — so the structure is visible before you learn a single rule.

What we believe

Calmness is a feature.

Klarweg is built on a small set of non-negotiable principles. They shape every screen.

Respect the adult learner

No streaks, no mascots, no "You've got this!" confetti. Specific, earned feedback and real metadata — the tone of a serious tool, not a game.

No urgency, no anxiety

No countdowns, no manufactured pressure. The only timer in Klarweg is the one in the real Goethe exam. Learning a language is a calm, long road.

Colour means grammar — only

Colour in Klarweg always carries meaning. It never decorates. If something is coloured, it's telling you something about the language.

Premium, light, quiet

A warm off-white canvas, careful type, and motion that never shouts. The register of Linear and Apple Education — not a generic course platform.
