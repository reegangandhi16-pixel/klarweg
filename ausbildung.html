Ausbildung · vocational training B1 → B2 path

German for your Ausbildung.

An Ausbildung is a paid, structured apprenticeship in Germany — the route thousands of Indian nurses, care workers and skilled trainees take into the German workforce. The one thing every programme requires is German. Klarweg gets you to the level placement demands.

Why it matters

The visa follows the language.

Most German employers and the visa process expect at least a B1 before you arrive, and many healthcare programmes ask for B2. The language certificate isn't a formality — it's the gate.

Klarweg is built for exactly this learner: the capable adult professional who needs real, Goethe-aligned German on a clear timeline — not a gamified app that wastes their evenings.
