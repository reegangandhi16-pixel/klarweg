B2 German for Nurses: Handover Practice

Quick answer

This page gives you a B2-level German handover drill for reporting confirmed details, flagging missing information and assigning a follow-up. Check the relevant authority for recognition, language evidence and any profession-specific exam expectations.

On this page

  • Practise what the next shift needs to know
  • Rehearse a handover with one missing detail
  • Listen for what is confirmed and what is estimated

Practise what the next shift needs to know

Make three columns before speaking: confirmed information, an open item and who owns the next step. Practise in a fictional scenario and avoid identifying any real patient. For actual recognition and language requirements, check the responsible authority for the role. The federal information portal for nursing professionals is a starting point.

Rehearse a handover with one missing detail

A fictional person is waiting for transport. Reception has called the transport team, but there is no confirmed pickup time. Tell the next shift what is complete and ask them to verify the time.

Compare one B2 handover

Die Person wartet noch auf den Transport. Der Empfang hat den Fahrdienst bereits informiert. Eine Abholzeit wurde bisher nicht bestätigt. Könntest du beim Fahrdienst nachfragen und die Uhrzeit dokumentieren?

Use bereits for the completed call and bisher nicht bestätigt for the open item. Ask your partner to repeat who should act. Then practise with an estimated time that you must clearly label as provisional.

For programme and career context, see Klarweg’s German for nursing in Germany guide.

Listen for what is confirmed and what is estimated

A partner says Die Abholung ist voraussichtlich um vierzehn Uhr. Report it as Eine Abholung gegen vierzehn Uhr ist vorgesehen, aber noch nicht bestätigt. Then assign a next step: Bitte frag beim Fahrdienst nach und gib der nächsten Schicht Bescheid. Have your partner repeat both the uncertainty and who will follow up.
