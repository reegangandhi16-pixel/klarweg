### Cleanups required before any of these is written

**⚠ Parenthetical parser notes.** Many glosses carry an occurrence annotation —
"reviewers **(Satzende)**", "submitted **(Satzende, gewöhnliches Passiv)**". These
are per-occurrence parser metadata, not dictionary glosses, and must be stripped.

**⚠ Romanised Hindi.** Four glosses are Hinglish rather than Devanagari:
`Reform` → *sudhar*, `wirksam` → *prabhaavshaali*, `erfordern` →
*zaroorat hai*, `Ministerium` → *mantraalay* (one chapter; another gives
मंत्रालय). Devanagari should be preferred where a second chapter supplies it;
otherwise this is a Hindi-sourcing decision, not something I should transliterate.

**⚠ Inflected glosses used as lemma glosses.** `Gutachter` is glossed as the
plural "reviewers / समीक्षकों", `Kritiker` and `Wissenschaftler` are typed
"Noun · plural", `förmlicher` is the comparative, `erhobenen` is a Partizip II,
and `erfordert`/`leitet` are 3rd-person forms. The lemma entry needs the
citation form, so these glosses need adjusting — a curriculum call, not a
mechanical one.

**⚠ Adverb-vs-adjective, again.** `sorgfältig`, `präzise` and `voraus` are
typed Adverb (`voraus` as "Separable prefix"), yet the corpus also uses them
attributively. This is the same situation as `klar`/`echt`, and the same
`<base> adjektiv` key pattern applies — but each needs its sentence checked
before deciding whether one entry or two is correct.

**⚠ `Vorjahr`'s gloss looks wrong**: "previous year / साल के". The Hindi is a
fragment ("of the year"). Needs review.

## Homograph and collision risks

- `Software`, `System`, `Drill`, `Protokoll` are **English-identical German
  nouns**. My English-leak filters must never later reclassify them out; worth a
  permanent allowlist.
- `Leitung` has two senses (management / cable, pipe). The corpus context is
  a1-45 *telefonieren*, which suggests the phone-line sense — **do not force one
  reading**; needs the sentence checked.
- `Gutachten` (n, expert report) vs `Gutachter` (m, reviewer) vs `Gutachterin`
  (f, already authored in the 129). Three distinct lemmas, similar spelling.
- `sorgfältig`, `präzise`, `voraus`: adverb entries exist in class terms but not
  as headwords; adding adjectives needs the `<base> adjektiv` pattern.
- `treten` — I have **not** verified that `trat` (a2-28) is the same sense as
  the b2-20 "enter" gloss. Two plausible readings; left unforced.
- `erheben` is derived from the participle `erhobenen` only. The infinitive is
  not attested in the corpus as a surface, so the lemma itself is an inference —
  flagged as medium confidence, not high.
