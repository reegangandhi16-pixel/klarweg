Live-site defect · Word Popup

Words the resolver could not reach

Two related defects, one mechanism. Learners could tap a coloured German word and get a popup containing nothing but the word and a dash — or find a word not clickable at all. In both cases the dictionary data existed and the resolver could not reach it, because the build-time lookup table was generated from a narrower set of forms than the curriculum actually uses.

The pipeline, as it exists

1 · How a word becomes clickable
Two independent routes. Authored tokens — story, reading, listening and exercise tokens[] arrays in each chapter-*-data.js — are rendered as span.rw by their own renderer and wired straight to openWordPop(). Everything else is swept by linkifyDe() → wrapWordsIn(), which wraps a word as span.de-link only if resolveClickableWord() says it has real data.
2 · What metadata is attached
An authored token carries occurrence facts only: w, role, sometimes case, type, why, lexicalUnit. Lexical facts (meaning, Hindi, IPA, example, paradigms) live in the dictionary, never on the token.
3 · How the click resolves
resolveFullEntry() → lookupWord(): exact surface → resolveLemma() (article/pronoun tables, then the build-time KW_INFLECTIONS table, then compound split) → chapter WORDIDX → KW_GLOBAL_LEXICON (7,802 entries) → story lexicon. All hits merge, richest field wins.
4 · How the popup is populated
renderWordPop() renders whatever the merged entry holds, section by section: headword + audio, pronunciation/IPA, meaning, Hindi, role and case badges, level, “Why this form?”, then paradigm grids (Comparison, Conjugation, Number, Declension) through the single shared renderParadigmGrid().
5 · What happened when metadata was missing
The sweep route was safe — a word with no entry never became clickable. The authored-token route has no such gate, so a thin token reached the renderer and printed the literal placeholder t.en || '—': a popup with a dash and a generic “Word” badge, and nothing else.

Root cause

B — incorrect lookup / key mismatch, with a small tail of E (words that genuinely have no entry). It surfaced in two separate key families, both traceable to the same build step.

Family 1 · Adjective declension — empty popups

German attributive adjectives inflect: neu becomes neue, neuen, neuem, neuer, neues. KW_INFLECTIONS, the build-time table the resolver consumes, was generated from authored plurals, comparison forms and conjugations — the adjective declension paradigm was never a candidate field. So neue reached resolveLemma(), missed every step, returned unresolved, and lookupWord() returned null — while neu → { en:"new", hi:"नया", type:"Adjective", compare:{…} } sat in the lexicon the whole time.

These forms are clickable because a chapter author wrote them into a story or reading token layer. Nothing was wrong with the content. The popup simply could not find the entry the curriculum already had.

Family 2 · Separable and reflexive verb person forms — not clickable at all

Reported from B1 Chapter 17, Speaking: mitmachst, zögert, Beruhige were plain text while their neighbours were clickable. The container was correctly swept (data-linked="1", six child spans) — the words were withheld because they resolved to nothing, which is the sweep gate working as designed on a resolver that was failing.

The build derives the present-tense person paradigm from each verb's authored conj.praesens: du = the 3rd-person form with trailing t → st, ich = infinitive minus n. Those rules assume a single bare verb form. But 433 separable verbs author the split form (mitmachen · "macht mit") and 62 reflexive verbs carry sich in the headword and the form (sich beruhigen · "beruhigt sich"). Fed the raw two-word string, the du derivation produced nothing — and for reflexives the ich derivation produced a two-word key that was discarded.

The signature is exact and was visible in the shipped table: mitmacht → mitmachen and beruhigt → sich beruhigen both existed, while mitmachst, beruhige and beruhigst did not. The forms derivable from the infinitive alone landed; the ones derived from the authored finite string did not. Every separable and reflexive verb in the curriculum lost its du form — the form a learner meets constantly in tasks and dialogue.

What it was not

  • A. Missing source metadata — no. The entries exist; 5 of the 9 empty popups in the C1 chapter resolved to fully authored entries with meaning, Hindi, comparison grid and examples.
  • C. Renderer not displaying data — no. Given an entry, the renderer shows every field it has.
  • D. Duplicate / ambiguous keys — no, and the fix adds none: 0 collisions among the 4,647 adjective candidates, 26 dropped among the 2,638 verb candidates.
  • Markup, capitalisation, punctuation, normalization — all ruled out for the three reported words. The speaking prompt is .speak-prompt.de and was swept normally; normWord() lowercases and strips punctuation before every lookup, so Beruhige and mitmachst. both reach the table in canonical form. The failure was purely that the table had no such key.
  • E. Intentionally data-free words — partly. Proper names (Timo, Bruns, Ida) and a handful of surfaces with no dictionary entry are legitimately data-free. They were the reason the popup needed a graceful empty state, not a resolver change.

Fields available vs. fields displayed

No gap. Every dictionary field the schema defines has a renderer: w, pron, ipa, en, hi, type (role badge), case, level, why, ex/exEn/exHi, examples[], compare, conj, plural, declension, lexicalUnit, audio. The defect was upstream of the renderer, in resolution.

The fix

Three changes, all system-level. No curriculum content, vocabulary, dialogue or explanation was touched.

1 · Adjective declension enters the inflection index shipped

chapter/BUILD_INFLECTION_INDEX.md freezes an invariant: the runtime never guesses morphology, the build step owns it, and the table stays collision-free. The fix respects that — it adds a candidate family (step 2b) rather than a runtime heuristic. For every entry classed plainly Adjective (or Adjective / adverb), the positive base is indexed under base + -e/-en/-em/-er/-es, pointing at that authored headword.

The three contraction families are excluded outright, because they are not derivable by suffixation and must never be guessed: bases in -el (dunkel → dunkle), bases in -er (teuer → teure), and hoch → hohe. Compound, participle, nominalised and chain adjective classes are excluded too.

2 · Person-form derivation reads the normalized verb form shipped

Same build layer, same principle: normalize the authored string, then apply the morphology that was already sanctioned. A standalone sich is stripped from the headword and the finite form; a separable verb's split form is re-joined into the surface a Nebensatz shows, and only when the authored infinitive literally starts with that particle — a data test, not a hand-kept particle list. Multi-word infinitives (spazieren gehen) are excluded: they are not prefix verbs.

Verbs with no authored conj.praesens are still skipped. Deriving du from an infinitive alone would have to guess stem changes (fahren → fährst), and this build never guesses morphology.

3 · The popup degrades gracefully shipped

A clickable word with genuinely no entry — a proper name, a one-off surface — no longer renders a dash. It states the situation plainly and keeps the audio button live, so the word is still useful to hear:

No-data state

This form is not in the dictionary yet. You can still hear it.

यह रूप शब्दकोश में अभी दर्ज नहीं है। उच्चारण सुन सकते हैं।

A second variant covers the case where forms or examples exist but no translation does, pointing the learner at what is there. Nothing is invented, no word is removed, and a word with only a meaning still shows that meaning cleanly.

zögern is not a dictionary headword anywhere in the curriculum. It exists in exactly one place: an authored story token in B2·19, { w:'zögern', en:'hesitating', hi:'देरी किए', pron:'TSÖ-gern' }. The Global Lexicon is compiled from vocab[] headwords only, so meaning authored on a token is visible in its own chapter and nowhere else — zögern is clickable in B2·19 and dead in B1·17.

That is a third, separate gap, and it needs a decision rather than a patch: harvesting token-level glosses into the dictionary would import occurrence-bound translations as headword meanings (“hesitating”, not “to hesitate”) across the whole corpus. And even with a zögern entry, resolving zögert needs a conj.praesens the token does not carry. Both routes are authoring decisions, so nothing was changed. The word stays correctly non-clickable rather than opening an empty popup.

Verification

  • All six levels re-tested — 4,045 word clicks, 0 empty popups. Re-run after both lexicon regenerations, so the figure is post-fix throughout. A seventh spot-check on A1·3 Nominativ (223 clicks) after the cache-token bump was also clean.
  • Rich metadata — präsentiert: “to present · प्रस्तुत करना”, Verb badge, Conjugation grid, Example.
  • Newly resolved — umfangreiche: “extensive · व्यापक”, Adjective + C1 badges, Comparison grid, Examples.
  • Minimal metadata — begrenzten: “limited · सीमित”, Adjective + B1 badges, Example, no comparison data and no filler in its place.
  • Missing metadata — Timo, ambitioniert: no-data state, audio live.
  • Audio — the header button renders and is enabled in every case tested, including the no-data state.
  • Existing behaviour preserved — inline role colours unchanged on the words checked (entscheidende and begrenzten keep their authored colour while gaining a populated popup); breadcrumb navigation, recursive in-popup links and paradigm grids untouched.
  • B1·17 re-audited — 1,063 word clicks, 0 empty popups; mitmachst and Beruhige clickable with their authored entries.
  • No English words wrongly linked — checked on B1·11 Relativsätze and A1·13 Adjektive for the coincidence set (relative, reflexive, feminine, negative, comparative, dative…): zero hits.
  • Occurrence QA sweep (chapter/qa-runner.html, 3,587 occurrences over the six chapters) — 24 findings, of which 22 are pre-existing and unrelated (CASE_COLLISION_CAPITALISED on Essen/Kosten; role/badge contradictions on richtig, und, natürliche, weltberühmt). The 2 new ones are both ganze hitting the runner's existing dual-class quirk: an Adjective / adverb type is bucketed as adverb while the span is coloured adjective — the same finding richtig already produced four times in the same chapter before this change. No new finding category.

Content guarantee

No vocabulary list, dictionary entry, chapter explanation, dialogue line, reading passage, exercise or audio ID was added, removed or reworded. KW_GLOBAL_LEXICON and every chapter-*-data.js file are byte-identical. The fix only teaches the resolver to reach data that was already authored, and gives the popup an honest empty state.
