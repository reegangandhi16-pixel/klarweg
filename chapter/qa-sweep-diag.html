Diagnostic Sweep — P15 infrastructure

Same measurement path as qa-sweep14.html (render-stability gate, injection liveness check, bounded retry, errors never counted as zero). The only difference is the capture layer: findings are stored through qa-finding-serializer.js, which carries every field the engine emitted instead of a hand-picked subset. Nothing runs on load — KWDIAG.selfTest([slugs]) validates the serializer against real findings; KWDIAG.run() would sweep the corpus into a separate storage key and is intentionally NOT started here.
