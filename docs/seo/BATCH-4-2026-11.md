## 3. Sources (checked 5 Nov 2026)
- Goethe-Institut A2 *Durchführungsbestimmungen* (1 Sept 2025): 90-min written exam, 15-min paired speaking with
  no preparation, 25 points per part, pass = 60 overall with ≥45 written and ≥15 oral.
- Goethe-Institut A2 and B1 *Übungssatz Erwachsene*: part-by-part task types, word counts; B1 writing and
  speaking assessment criteria (Erfüllung E → 0 for the task; all five slides for the top band).
- HRK/KMK *RO-DT* (2020 version) §1, §2, §3, §4, §8: accepted university language proofs and exemptions.
  Goethe C1 is not listed; DSH-1/TDN 3 and DSH-3 as differentiated entry levels.
- A2 fee (₹10,600) as in batch 3 (owner to confirm on goethe.de/ins/in).
