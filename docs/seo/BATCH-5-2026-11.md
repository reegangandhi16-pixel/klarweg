## 2. Sources (checked 5 Nov 2026)
- Nursing Professions Act (PflBG) §2 no. 4 (German needed for the licence), §6 (3 years full-time), §11 (entry),
  §40 (recognition); Residence Act §2, §16a (B1 for Ausbildung, exceptions), §16d (recognition measures: as a
  rule at least A2).
- anerkennung-in-deutschland.de (official recognition portal, via search extracts): B2 for recognition as a
  nurse; some states require nursing-specific B2 or a Fachsprachenprüfung.
- Federal Employment Agency, hospital recruitment programme page (fetched): full-time course in the home
  country ~7–8 months to B1, ~10–12 months to B2.
- Goethe-Institut C1 *Durchführungsbestimmungen* (1 Sept 2025) and the current *C1 Modellsatz*
  (`C1_modular`): 4/4/2/2 parts, item counts, suggested times, 230/120 words, 20-min speaking preparation.
  The older C1 model test (offered until 31.12.2023) was deliberately not used.
