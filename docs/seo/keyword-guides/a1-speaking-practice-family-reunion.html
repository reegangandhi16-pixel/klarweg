Practise four everyday German speaking tasks

Use fictional details on a public call. Say your name and city, spell a surname, confirm an appointment date and ask the other person to repeat a detail. This is language practice. Use the official instructions for your application to verify which proof, if any, applies to you.

  1. Introduce a fictional person named Mira Patel, who lives in Pune.
  2. Spell the surname slowly and ask whether the listener has it correctly.
  3. Confirm Tuesday at 10:00, then ask where to go.
  4. Ask the other speaker to repeat the address more slowly.
Compare four short A1 replies

Ich heiße Mira Patel. Ich wohne in Pune.

P wie Paula, A, T, E, L. Haben Sie das richtig verstanden?

Der Termin ist am Dienstag um zehn Uhr, richtig? Wohin soll ich kommen?

Können Sie die Adresse bitte langsamer wiederholen?

Run the practice twice

Round one: use the models after each turn. Round two: change the street, day and time and ask a partner to respond without showing you the script. Your check is whether you can repeat the agreed date and place correctly. For application details, see the Klarweg family-reunion language guide and the official sources linked there.
