A1 to B2: the official estimate

The Goethe-Institut estimates that reaching B2 from zero takes 600–800 lessons of 45 minutes, which is 450–600 hours, depending on your prior knowledge and how you learn.

Lesson ranges: Goethe-Institut India exam pages for A1, A2, B1 and B2, checked 22 November 2026 (“depending on your previous knowledge and learning requirements”). Hours and weeks are Klarweg’s arithmetic from those ranges; they do not include exam booking or waiting for results.

At 15 hours a week, an intensive pace, B2 takes about 30–40 weeks (7–9 months). At 10 hours, about 45–60 weeks (10–14 months). At 6 hours, about 75–100 weeks (17–23 months).

Why the ranges are wide

  • Prior knowledge: learners who already know another European language, or some German from school, often start nearer the low end.
  • Practice between lessons: class hours alone rarely reach the low end; review and speaking practice count.
  • Feedback on writing and speaking: B1 and B2 exams grade free production. Without correction, learners often plateau.
  • Breaks: a month off usually costs more than a month, because you must revise.

Work backwards from a deadline

Example: you need a B2 certificate in 12 months and you are starting at A1.

  1. Confirm which certificate the authority or employer accepts, and by when it must be submitted.
  2. Find an exam date at least a month before that, and leave time for results.
  3. Divide the remaining weeks by the hours you need: 450–600 hours in about 10 months means 11–14 hours a week.
  4. If that is not realistic, change the plan now: more hours, an intensive phase, or a later deadline.
Check your own plan

Weeks available × hours per week should reach at least the low end of the range for your target level. If it does not, the plan depends on luck. Add a level check after each stage and replan if a mock test shows a gap.
