Prepare three truthful answers for your trade

Read the actual apprenticeship listing and pick two tasks that interest you. Build a short answer using a real example from school, a course, volunteering or work. The required language level and certificate depend on the role and organisation; verify the listing before booking an exam.

  1. Motivation: Warum möchten Sie diese Ausbildung machen?
  2. Experience: Wann haben Sie schon im Team gearbeitet?
  3. Clarification: Was machen Sie, wenn Sie eine Aufgabe nicht verstehen?
