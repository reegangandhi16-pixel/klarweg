Build a free first week of German

You can try Klarweg’s first chapter at every level for free. If you’re new, start with A1 to hear the sounds and learn a few phrases. Then use free grammar and conversation tasks to practise recalling them. The chapters after the free preview are part of Klarweg’s paid self-paced courses.

  1. First session: Open the A1 chapter and repeat five sounds aloud.
  2. Next session: Learn how to give your name and where you live. Try the self-introduction practice without reading the model first.
  3. Later this week: Try one free practice set and repeat any answers you missed the next day.
  4. Weekend: Add one listening activity from the Goethe-Institut free practice collection.

Check what you can do on day seven

Record yourself giving your name, city and one language you speak. Listen back and repeat the line that was hardest. If you want more structure, compare the full A1 roadmap. If your main challenge is reacting to a person in real time, a short teacher demo can show you what guided practice feels like.

Example first-week recording

Ich heiße Nisha. Ich wohne in Jaipur. Ich spreche Hindi.

Change the three details to your own. You can use familiar words before you try a longer sentence.
