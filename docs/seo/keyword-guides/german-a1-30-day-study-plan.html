What 30 days can and cannot do

The Goethe-Institut estimates 80–200 lessons of 45 minutes to reach A1, about 60–150 hours. One hour a day for 30 days is 30 hours: enough for a solid first part of A1, not the whole level. This plan covers the first 17 Klarweg A1 chapters of 47, with speaking and listening every day.

Chapter 1 is free: start A1 Chapter 1. Chapters 2–47 are in the A1 course (₹1,999, two years).

Days 1–7: sounds and first conversations

  1. Chapter 1: listen to the alphabet and repeat it.
  2. Spell your name and city aloud.
  3. Chapter 2: the vowels and umlauts.
  4. Chapter 3: count aloud to 20, then to 100 in tens.
  5. Chapter 4: greet someone formally and informally.
  6. Say your phone number in pairs of digits.
  7. Record a 30-second introduction from memory.

Checkpoint: can someone understand your name, city and number from the recording?

Days 8–14: introductions and questions

  1. Chapter 5: Ich heiße …, ich komme aus …, ich wohne in …
  2. Ask a partner the same three questions.
  3. Chapter 6: W-questions.
  4. Write five questions you could ask a new colleague.
  5. Chapter 7: yes/no questions with the verb first.
  6. Chapter 8: first exam rehearsal.
  7. Redo the part of the rehearsal you found hardest.

Checkpoint: ask and answer five questions without reading.

Days 15–21: the grammar that carries A1

  1. Chapter 9: everyday verbs in the present tense.
  2. Chapter 10: der, die, das. Learn every noun with its article.
  3. Practise ten nouns with articles aloud.
  4. Chapter 11: kein and nicht.
  5. Chapter 12: Akkusativ (einen, den).
  6. Chapter 13: adjectives with sein.
  7. Write six sentences about your home using the last five chapters.

Checkpoint: describe your room in five sentences with correct articles.

Days 22–30: everyday situations

  1. Chapter 14: food and drink.
  2. Chapter 15: order in a café and ask for the bill.
  3. Role-play the café with a partner.
  4. Chapter 16: hobbies and free time.
  5. Write a 30-word message inviting a friend.
  6. Chapter 17: second exam rehearsal.
  7. Redo the weakest part.
  8. Do the day-30 check below.
  9. Plan the next month: chapters 18–34 at the same pace.

Day-30 check

1. How do you say 17 in German?

siebzehn: the n of sieben drops. siebzig is 70.

2. Which question asks where someone comes from?

Woher = from where. Wo asks where someone is or lives.

3. Complete: Ich habe ___ Auto.

Use kein to negate a noun with ein/no article; das Auto is neuter, so kein.

4. Complete: Ich kaufe ___ Tisch.

der Tisch is masculine. As the object (Akkusativ) ein becomes einen.

5. Which yes/no question is correct?

In a yes/no question the verb comes first.

6. In a café, what is the polite way to order?

Ich hätte gern … or Ich möchte … is the polite way to order.

Five or six right: carry on with chapters 18–34. Fewer: repeat the chapter behind each missed question before moving on.

If you miss a day

Do not double the next day. Do the one task that the following days depend on, such as numbers before times, and drop the review task instead. Keep the day-30 check on day 30.
