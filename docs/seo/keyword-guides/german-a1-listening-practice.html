Six A1 listening exercises with instant answers

Press Play, listen for the one detail in the question, then choose your answer. Each clip is short and spoken at a steady beginner pace. Play it a second time before checking, as you would in most parts of the A1 exam. All clips are original Klarweg practice. They are spoken by a German voice on your device.

If your device has no German voice, ask a partner to read the transcripts aloud.

1. Appointment: When does the German course start?

Viertel vor neun is a quarter to nine: 08:45. Dienstag is Tuesday; Thursday is Donnerstag.

2. Station announcement: Which platform does the train to Bonn leave from today?

You hear two platforms. statt means “instead of”, so platform 4 is the one being replaced.

3. At a market stall: What is not available today?

nicht mehr means “no longer”: there is no bread left. The soup costs €5.

4. Phone message: Which number should you call back?

German says the unit before the ten: dreiundvierzig is “three and forty”, 43. Write the second digit first as you listen, then add the first.

5. Invitation: When is the party?

am dritten Mai is 3 May. 30 would be dreißigsten. March is März, which sounds different from Mai.

6. Weather report: What will the weather be on Sunday?

25 degrees is Saturday. For Sunday you hear am Sonntag regnet es: it rains. Listen for the day before the detail.

After checking, change one detail, such as the time, platform, number or day. Ask a partner to read the new version and repeat back only the changed detail.

Build a 10-minute daily listening routine

  1. Gist first (2 min): play a clip once and say in English or Hindi what it is about: an appointment, a station or a shop.
  2. Details (4 min): play again and write only numbers, days and places. At A1, most exam questions test exactly these.
  3. Check (2 min): open the transcript and underline the word that gave the answer.
  4. Shadow (2 min): read the transcript aloud with the audio to fix the rhythm in your memory.
