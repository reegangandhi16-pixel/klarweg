Listen for one detail at a time

Press Play to hear each short clip at a steady beginner pace. Write your answer before opening the transcript. Play it again to check what you heard. These are original clips spoken by an on-device German voice, where available.

  1. Appointment: What day and time does the course start?
  2. Station: Which platform does the train leave from today?
  3. Shopping: What does the soup cost and what is unavailable?

If your device has no German voice, ask a partner to read the transcripts aloud.

Transcripts and explained answers

Tuesday at 08:45. Viertel vor neun is a quarter to nine.

Check the station announcement

Der Zug nach Bonn fährt heute von Gleis sieben statt von Gleis vier.

Check the shopping message

Die Suppe kostet fünf Euro. Brot haben wir heute leider nicht mehr.

The soup costs €5, and there is no bread left today. Nicht mehr tells you it is no longer available.
