How Goethe A2 listening works

About 30 minutes, 4 parts, 20 tasks, 25 of the 100 points. Two parts are heard only once, so you cannot wait for the second play.

PartWhat you hearPlayed
1Five short texts, such as radio messages or announcementsTwice
2One conversation; match five things to picturesOnce
3Five short everyday conversationsOnce
4An interview; decide Ja or Nein for five statementsTwice

Source: Goethe-Institut A2 exam information, rules dated 1 September 2025. Full guide: Goethe A2 exam.

Three clips: catch the change

A2 announcements often correct earlier information. Play each clip once, write the time, the entrance and the item, then answer the questions below before opening a transcript.

If playback is unavailable, ask a partner to read the scripts aloud.

Transcript: pickup time

Die Abholung ist nicht um drei, sondern erst um fünf.

Transcript: entrance

Der Haupteingang ist geschlossen. Benutzen Sie bitte den Eingang im Hof.

Transcript: required item

Bitte bringen Sie statt der Eintrittskarte einen Ausweis mit.

1. Clip 1: when is the pickup?

nicht um drei, sondern erst um fünf. The first time is the one that no longer applies; sondern brings the correction.

2. Clip 2: which entrance do you use?

Der Haupteingang ist geschlossen: the main entrance is closed. im Hof means in the courtyard.

3. Clip 3: what must you bring?

statt der Eintrittskarte einen Ausweis: an ID instead of the ticket. Both items are mentioned, which is exactly the trap.

4. Which word warns you that a correction is coming?

nicht … sondern replaces one thing with another. Also listen for statt, doch and leider.

5. In parts 2 and 3 you hear each recording once. What should you do before it starts?

You get reading time before each part. Use it to know what you are listening for, because there is no second play.

6. In part 4 you decide whether statements are right. Which answer format do you use?

Part 4 is an interview with five Ja/Nein statements, played twice. Use the second play to confirm the ones you were unsure about.

A ten-minute routine for one-play parts

  1. Read the questions for 30 seconds and say aloud what you expect: a time, a place or a price.
  2. Play the clip once only. Write the detail in numbers, not words.
  3. Check with the transcript and underline the word that gave the answer away.
  4. Ask a partner to change one detail and read it again. Write only the new detail.

When you get five of six above, try the official A2 practice set with its audio under time, then the A2 mini test.
