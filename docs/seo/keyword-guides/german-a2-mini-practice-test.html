Try writing and speaking

Write to a friend to move your Sunday picnic because of rain. Suggest an indoor place and a new time. Then say your proposal aloud and ask whether it works.

Check one possible answer

Hallo Lea, am Sonntag soll es regnen. Treffen wir uns lieber um zwei im Café am Bahnhof? Passt dir das?

Check that you gave a reason, a new place and a time. Repeat aloud with a different time. Score this by details communicated, then use official model materials for exam-specific rehearsal.
