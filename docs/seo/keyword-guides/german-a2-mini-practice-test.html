The Goethe A2 exam in one table

The Goethe-Zertifikat A2 is not modular: you sit the whole written exam (90 minutes, no breaks) and a 15-minute speaking test in pairs, and the result counts as one exam. Format as published by the Goethe-Institut, exam rules dated 1 September 2025.

Lesen (reading)30 min4 parts, 20 tasks25

To pass: 60 of 100 points, including at least 45 of the 75 written points and at least 15 of the 25 speaking points. Fee in India: ₹12,000 for exam dates from January 2027 at Goethe-Institut India centres, including GST (fees and booking). Full walk-through of every part: Goethe A2 exam guide.

Mini test: eight questions, checked on the page

This is original Klarweg practice in the style of the exam, not an official test. Read the two texts. For question 5, ask someone to read the voicemail aloud once while you look away, as Hören parts 2 and 3 are played only once.

Text A, an email

Hallo Priya, vielen Dank für deine Einladung! Am Samstag kann ich leider nicht kommen, weil meine Schwester Geburtstag hat. Hast du am Sonntagnachmittag Zeit? Dann bringe ich einen Kuchen mit. Liebe Grüße, Jonas

Text B, a notice

Freibad am Stadtpark: Wegen Reparaturen vom 3. bis 14. Juli geschlossen. Das Hallenbad in der Bergstraße ist täglich von 7 bis 21 Uhr geöffnet.

Voicemail script for a partner (question 5)

Guten Tag, Herr Singh, hier ist das Autohaus Krämer. Ihr Auto ist fertig. Sie können es heute bis 18 Uhr abholen. Morgen haben wir geschlossen.

1. Text A: why can Jonas not come on Saturday?

weil meine Schwester Geburtstag hat: because his sister has her birthday. The reason follows weil, with the verb at the end.

2. Text A: what does Jonas offer to do on Sunday?

Dann bringe ich einen Kuchen mit: then I will bring a cake. mitbringen is separable, so mit goes to the end.

3. Text B: you want to swim on 10 July. Where can you go?

The Freibad is closed from 3 to 14 July, so on 10 July only the Hallenbad is open. In the exam, look for the date range before the place name.

4. Text B: can you swim at the indoor pool at 9:30 p.m.?

von 7 bis 21 Uhr: from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. German notices use the 24-hour clock, so 21:30 is too late.

5. Voicemail: when can Mr Singh collect his car?

heute bis 18 Uhr abholen, and Morgen haben wir geschlossen: tomorrow they are closed. A distractor often names the day that does not work.

6. Which sentence is correct?

Verbs of movement from A to B form the Perfekt with sein: ich bin … gefahren. A2 writing and speaking expect the Perfekt.

7. Complete: Ich komme später, ___ der Bus Verspätung hat.

The verb hat is at the end, so you need weil. With denn or aber the verb stays in second place: denn der Bus hat Verspätung.

8. Speaking, part 3: your partner suggests Friday, but you cannot. What is the best reply?

In part 3 you are graded on reacting and suggesting. Say why it does not work and offer an alternative: Wie wäre es mit …?

Writing: an SMS with three points

Schreiben part 1 gives three points to cover in 20–30 words. Missing one costs marks, however good your German is.

You are on the train and will be late meeting your friend Sara. Write an SMS: say that you will be late, say why, and suggest a new time.

Check one possible answer (26 words)

Hallo Sara, ich komme leider eine halbe Stunde später. Mein Zug hat Verspätung. Können wir uns um halb sieben vor dem Kino treffen? Bis später, Anil

Tick the three points: late, why, new time. Then write the formal version of the same message to a teacher, with Sie and a formal greeting. More tasks: A2 writing practice.

Speaking: four question cards

In speaking part 1 you turn a keyword into a question for your partner, then answer theirs. Make a question from each keyword and answer it in a full sentence with one extra detail: Wohnort? · Beruf? · Wochenende? · Lieblingsessen?

Check model questions and answers

Wo wohnst du? – Ich wohne in Nagpur, in der Nähe vom Bahnhof.

Was bist du von Beruf? – Ich bin Krankenpfleger und arbeite im Moment nachts.

Was machst du am Wochenende? – Am Samstag treffe ich meistens Freunde.

Was ist dein Lieblingsessen? – Ich esse sehr gern Dosa, am liebsten mit Kokos-Chutney.

Part 2 (talk about your life) and part 3 (plan something together) are in the A2 speaking guide.

What your score means

  • 7–8 correct: try a full official practice set under time, then book the exam.
  • 5–6 correct: look at which part you missed. Reading and listening: A2 listening practice. Grammar: Perfekt and weil-clauses.
  • 4 or fewer: finish the A2 course content first. A2 usually needs 200–350 lessons of 45 minutes in total from zero, according to the Goethe-Institut.
