Build questions from a partner’s answer

Start with Was hast du am Wochenende gemacht? Your partner says Ich war auf dem Flohmarkt. Ask when they went, what they bought or who went with them. Ask only one question at a time and wait for the answer.

Try a second topic without a script

Your partner says they started a German course. Ask on which days it meets and what they like about it. Give your own answer when your partner asks you back.

Check one possible answer

An welchen Tagen hast du Unterricht? Was gefällt dir an dem Kurs? Ich mag die Gespräche, weil ich dabei viel sprechen kann.

Use a follow-up that reuses something from the answer. If you did not understand the course days, ask for those again before switching topics.

Listen and ask a useful next question

Try each item before opening its answer. Use the feedback to correct one thing, then try again.

  1. Your partner says, “Der Film beginnt um acht.” Ask where you should meet.

    Check your answer

    Wo treffen wir uns?

    You already know the time. A location question helps finish the plan.

  2. Your partner says, “Ich habe das Fahrrad gebraucht gekauft.” Ask what it cost and listen to the reply.

    Check your answer

    Wie viel hat es gekostet?

    Es refers to das Fahrrad. If you miss the number, ask for the price again.

  3. Your partner says, “Ich fahre morgen nach Bonn.” Ask why, then say which word in their answer you can ask about next.

    Check your answer

    Warum fährst du nach Bonn? — Ich besuche meine Schwester. — Wohnt sie schon lange dort?

    Schwester gives you a person to ask about. Dort refers to Bonn, so you can keep the conversation on the same topic.
