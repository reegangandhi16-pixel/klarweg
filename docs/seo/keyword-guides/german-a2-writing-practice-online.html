Write three details before worrying about grammar

Your classmate Ben expects to meet you Tuesday at 17:00 at the library. You have to work. Write a short message that names the original plan, explains why you cannot come and proposes Wednesday at 18:00 at the same place.

Compare your message with the model

Read your draft as Ben. Check that you can identify which plan changed, what time you should come instead and whether the location changed.

Check one possible answer

Hallo Ben, am Dienstag um 17 Uhr kann ich leider nicht in die Bibliothek kommen, weil ich arbeiten muss. Können wir uns stattdessen am Mittwoch um 18 Uhr dort treffen? Viele Grüße, Mira
