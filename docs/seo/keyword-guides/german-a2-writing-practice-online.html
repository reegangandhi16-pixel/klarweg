What Goethe A2 writing asks for

30 minutes, two short texts, 25 of the 100 points. Each task lists three points you must cover. Missing one costs marks, however good the grammar.

Task 1: move a meeting (informal)

Your classmate Ben expects you on Tuesday at 17:00 at the library. You have to work. Write 20–30 words: say which meeting you cannot make, say why, and suggest Wednesday at 18:00 at the same place.

Check one possible answer (28 words)

Hallo Ben, am Dienstag um 17 Uhr kann ich leider nicht in die Bibliothek kommen, weil ich arbeiten muss. Können wir uns am Mittwoch um 18 Uhr treffen? Liebe Grüße, Mira

Three points: which meeting, why, new time. weil sends the verb to the end: weil ich arbeiten muss.

Task 2: write to the language school (formal)

You cannot attend your German class on Monday. Write 30–40 words to your teacher, Frau Kaiser: apologise and give the reason, ask what the class will cover, and ask whether you can join another group that week.

Check one possible answer (39 words)

Sehr geehrte Frau Kaiser, leider kann ich am Montag nicht zum Kurs kommen, weil ich einen Arzttermin habe. Was machen wir in dieser Stunde? Könnte ich in dieser Woche vielleicht in einer anderen Gruppe mitmachen? Mit freundlichen Grüßen, Rahul Mehta

Check the register: formal greeting, Sie-forms (Könnte ich …), formal closing, your full name.

Check the details examiners notice

1. You write to a teacher you have never met. Which greeting fits?

A formal email starts Sehr geehrte Frau … or Sehr geehrter Herr …. Hallo is for friends; Liebe needs a first name.

2. Which closing belongs to the formal email?

Mit freundlichen Grüßen closes a formal email. Use Liebe Grüße or Viele Grüße for a friend.

3. Which sentence has the right word order?

In a weil-clause the conjugated verb goes to the end: weil ich arbeiten muss.

4. You write to Ben, a friend. Which question fits?

With a friend use du and the matching verb form kannst. Mixing du and Sie in one message loses marks.

5. The task has three points and you covered two very well. What happens?

Task fulfilment is graded. Before you write, number the three points in the margin and tick each one in your draft.

6. Your formal email has 52 words. What should you do?

Part 2 asks for about 30–40 words. Extra sentences add mistakes without adding points.

Next steps

Rewrite task 1 for a different reason and time each day this week. Then take the A2 mini test and compare your writing with the official A2 practice set.
