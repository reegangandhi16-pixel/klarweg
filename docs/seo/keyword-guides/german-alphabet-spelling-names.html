Give one letter at a time

J is called Jot and Y is Ypsilon. The names of V and W differ too: Vau and We. If someone misses a letter, repeat only that letter and give an example word. Spelling a word letter by letter differs from pronouncing the word itself.

Correct a spelling on the phone

A receptionist wrote Ware instead of Vare. Correct only the first letter and then spell the fictional surname from the start.

Check one possible answer

Mein Nachname ist Vare. Am Anfang steht ein V, kein W. V wie Vogel, A, R, E.

Ask your partner to repeat the spelling. Then create another name with J or Y and practise the same correction.

Do a read-back drill

Try each item before opening its answer. Use the feedback to correct one thing, then try again.

  1. Your partner writes Yana as Jana. Correct the first letter and spell the name.

    Check your answer

    Am Anfang steht ein Y, kein J. Y, A, N, A.

    Name the letter you want and the one you are correcting. Ask your partner to read the full name back.

  2. You heard W but you are unsure. Ask whether the first letter is V or W.

    Check your answer

    Steht am Anfang ein V oder ein W?

    This asks about the letter itself, which can differ from the sound of the first letter in a word.

  3. Your partner has the first letter right but missed the last one. Ask them to repeat the whole surname after your correction.

    Check your answer

    Der letzte Buchstabe ist ein E. Können Sie den ganzen Nachnamen bitte wiederholen?

    Your partner’s read-back is the check. Switch names and see if both of you write the same result.
