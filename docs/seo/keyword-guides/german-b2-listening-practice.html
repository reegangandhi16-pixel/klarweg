How Goethe B2 listening works

About 40 minutes, 4 parts, 30 items. It is a separate module scored out of 100; you pass with 60. You hear interviews, talks, conversations and statements from everyday life and the radio, and must catch key messages and important details. Source: Goethe-Zertifikat B2 rules (1 September 2025) and the Goethe-Institut India B2 page. Fee and module rules: B2 exam preparation.

At B2 the difficulty is less the words than the structure: who proposes what, who objects, and what was actually decided.

One clip: proposal, objection, decision

Play the clip once. Note the idea, the objection and the decision, then answer the questions before you open the transcript.

If playback is unavailable, ask a partner to read the script aloud.

Transcript

Vorschlag: Wir könnten die Bibliothek abends länger öffnen. Einwand: Dafür brauchen wir mehr Personal. Beschluss: Wir testen die längeren Öffnungszeiten vier Wochen lang und prüfen anschließend die Nachfrage und den Personalaufwand.

1. What is proposed?

die Bibliothek abends länger öffnen: open the library longer in the evening. könnten marks it as a suggestion, not a fact.

2. What is the objection?

Dafür brauchen wir mehr Personal. Distractors at B2 often sound plausible but are never said.

3. What did the group decide?

Wir testen … vier Wochen lang: a trial, not a permanent change. Separate the decision from the original proposal.

4. What will be checked after the trial?

prüfen anschließend die Nachfrage und den Personalaufwand: afterwards they check demand and staffing effort.

5. Which signal word often introduces a restriction in a B2 recording?

allerdings (however) restricts what came before. außerdem adds, zuerst orders. Also listen for jedoch and dennoch.

Summarise in two German sentences

Tell another learner what the group will do next and why it has not decided on a permanent policy yet.

Check one possible answer

Die Gruppe testet die längeren Öffnungszeiten vier Wochen lang. Über eine dauerhafte Änderung entscheidet sie erst, wenn Nachfrage und Personalaufwand ausgewertet sind.

Then ask a partner to replace the staffing concern with a cost concern and update your summary.

A weekly routine for longer recordings

  1. Two radio or podcast segments of 3–5 minutes in German each week. Before playing, read the title and write three questions you expect it to answer.
  2. Listen once and note only positions: who is for, who is against, what is decided.
  3. Listen again with a transcript, if there is one, and mark signal words such as allerdings, deshalb, im Gegenteil.
  4. Once a week, do one part of the official B2 practice set under time.
