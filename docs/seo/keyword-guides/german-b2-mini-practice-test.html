The Goethe B2 exam: four separate modules

You can take the B2 modules together or one at a time, and retake a module as often as the centre’s dates allow. Each module is scored out of 100; you pass it with 60.

ModuleTimePartsWhat you do
Lesen65 min5 parts, 30 itemsForum posts, articles, commentaries and regulations: main points, details, opinions and rules
Hörenabout 40 min4 parts, 30 itemsInterviews, talks, conversations and radio: key messages and details
Schreiben75 min2 partsA forum post giving and justifying your opinion; a formal message in a work context
Sprechenabout 15 min + 15 min preparation2 partsA short presentation with questions; a discussion with your partner

Mini test: six questions

Original Klarweg practice in the style of B2 reading and writing, not an official test.

Seit die Stadtbibliothek montags bis freitags bis 22 Uhr geöffnet ist, kommen abends deutlich mehr Studierende. Der Personalrat weist allerdings darauf hin, dass dafür zusätzliche Schichten nötig sind, die bisher nur befristet finanziert werden. Die Stadt will im Frühjahr entscheiden, ob die längeren Öffnungszeiten bleiben.

1. What has changed since the longer opening hours?

kommen abends deutlich mehr Studierende: clearly more students come in the evening. Weekends are not mentioned (Monday to Friday).

2. What is the staff council’s concern?

zusätzliche Schichten … die bisher nur befristet finanziert werden: extra shifts that so far are funded only temporarily. befristet = limited in time.

3. Has the city decided to keep the longer hours?

will im Frühjahr entscheiden: wants to decide in spring. B2 reading often tests whether something is decided, planned or only proposed.

4. Which word fits? ___ die Nachfrage gestiegen ist, fehlt das Geld für das Personal.

Obwohl starts a subordinate clause with the verb at the end (gestiegen ist). Trotzdem and deshalb are adverbs and take verb-second word order.

5. You write a formal message to the library management. Which sentence fits the register?

Schreiben part 2 is a formal message in a work or official context. Ich möchte Sie bitten, … zu … is formal and precise.

6. In a forum post you must give your opinion. Which sentence also gives a reason?

Schreiben part 1 asks you to express and justify an opinion. Each opinion needs a because, an example or a consequence.

Write a forum post and defend it aloud

Should the library keep its longer hours? Write about 150 words: state your position, use the observation from the text, address the staffing concern without inventing figures, and end with a proposal.

Check the opening of one possible answer

Ich würde die längeren Öffnungszeiten zunächst auf zwei Abende begrenzen. Zwar nutzen deutlich mehr Studierende die Bibliothek am Abend, doch die zusätzlichen Schichten sind nur befristet finanziert. Nach einigen Monaten sollte die Stadt die Nachfrage und den Personalaufwand vergleichen und dann entscheiden.

Then present your position aloud in two minutes and answer a partner who asks why your proposal is better than returning to the old hours.

Plan which module to take first

  • Strong reader, weaker speaker: take Lesen and Hören first, then book Sprechen once you can hold four minutes on a topic. Practice: B2 speaking practice.
  • Listening is the gap: start with B2 listening practice, then the official practice set with audio.
  • Need all four for a job or recognition: check whether the authority accepts modules taken on different dates before booking separately.

Full guide with a study plan: Goethe B2 exam preparation. The Goethe-Institut estimates 600–800 lessons of 45 minutes in total from zero to reach B2.
