How Goethe B2 speaking works

StageTimeWhat happens
Preparation15 minYou get the task sheet. Your notes in key words may be used in the exam.
Introductionabout 1 minA short get-to-know-you exchange. It is not graded.
Part 1: presentationabout 4 min per personYou choose one of two topics and present it. Your partner and an examiner then ask questions, which you answer.
Part 2: discussionabout 5 min togetherYou and your partner discuss a controversial topic.

The exam is normally taken in pairs (about 15 minutes); in exceptional cases alone (about 10 minutes, with an examiner as the partner). Speaking is its own module: you need 60 of 100 points to pass it. Source: Goethe-Zertifikat B2 rules, dated 1 September 2025.

Part 1: a presentation in five moves

A clear order makes four minutes feel short. Practise this structure with the topic „Homeoffice für alle?“:

  1. Introduce the topic and say how you will proceed: Ich spreche heute über … Zuerst …, danach …
  2. Describe the situation in your country, with one example.
  3. Weigh advantages and disadvantages: Ein Vorteil ist, dass … Auf der anderen Seite …
  4. Give your opinion with a reason: Meiner Meinung nach …, weil …
  5. Close and invite questions: Damit komme ich zum Schluss. Haben Sie Fragen?
Check a model opening (about 40 seconds)

Ich spreche heute über die Frage, ob alle Beschäftigten im Homeoffice arbeiten sollten. Zuerst beschreibe ich die Situation in Indien, danach nenne ich Vor- und Nachteile, und am Ende sage ich meine Meinung. In Städten wie Bengaluru arbeiten viele IT-Fachleute zwei oder drei Tage pro Woche von zu Hause, weil sie so lange Fahrzeiten sparen.

Record your own version, then listen for the three signposts: zuerst, danach, am Ende.

Part 2: defend a decision without repeating yourself

Your team can hold a two-hour training online or at the office. Online reaches more people; in person allows easier informal conversation. Choose one format. Then ask a partner to challenge you: Was machen wir, wenn einige Teilnehmende zu Hause keinen ruhigen Arbeitsplatz haben? Answer by changing one practical detail.

Check one possible answer

Ich würde das Training online anbieten, weil mehr Kolleginnen und Kollegen teilnehmen können. Allerdings braucht man einen ruhigen Platz. Wir könnten deshalb für diejenigen, die zu Hause keinen geeigneten Arbeitsplatz haben, einen Raum im Büro reservieren.

Your reply should accept the problem, then solve it. Next, argue the opposite position with the same criterion: can people concentrate and contribute?

Choose the phrase that fits

1. Your partner makes a point you partly agree with. What do you say?

Partial agreement keeps the discussion going and shows range: Da haben Sie teilweise recht, aber … or Das stimmt zwar, aber ….

2. After your partner’s presentation you must ask a question. Which one works?

A precise follow-up question shows you listened. Warum? alone is too thin for B2.

3. Which sentence correctly links a concession and a consequence?

zwar …, aber … is a B2 pattern for weighing a point. With obwohl the verb goes to the end: obwohl Homeoffice bequem ist.

4. You want to suggest a compromise. Which is best?

Wie wäre es, wenn … and Wir könnten … make polite proposals and invite a reaction.

5. You have ten seconds left in your presentation. How do you close?

A clear closing sentence signals structure, which is part of the grading for a presentation.

6. Can you use your preparation notes in the exam?

The rules allow the brief notes you made during the 15-minute preparation. Write key words, not full sentences you would read out.

Practise with feedback

Record part 1 twice a week and time it: under three minutes usually means you skipped a step. For a partner and corrections, see the Goethe B2 Sprechen guide or a teacher demo.
