A 90-minute week around your job

Monday, 15 minutes to listen and repeat. Wednesday, 15 minutes to recall the phrases from memory. Friday, 30 minutes for a lesson. Weekend, 30 minutes to rehearse one work situation aloud. If you miss Friday, move the lesson to the weekend and do the speaking task the next day.

Ninety minutes a week keeps German alive, but it is slow: at that pace A1 alone takes most of a year. Raise it to 5 hours when a deadline is fixed.

Practise German you will use at work

Start with one task: confirming a deadline. Say Kannst du die Liste bis Donnerstag prüfen? Then add who sends the updated version. Next week, do the same with a new deadline and formal Sie.

Check a model exchange

Ich schicke Ihnen die Liste bis Donnerstag. Können Sie sie bis Freitag prüfen? – Ja, das geht. Ich melde mich am Freitagvormittag.

Record a 30-second update with a deadline and a next step. A week later, record it again with a changed deadline and compare: is the owner, time and task clear?
