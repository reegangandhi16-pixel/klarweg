Try a 90-minute week around your job

Monday, 15 minutes to listen and repeat. Wednesday, 15 minutes to retrieve the phrases from memory. Friday, 30 minutes to work through a lesson. Weekend, 30 minutes to rehearse one workplace situation. If you miss Friday, use the weekend block for the core lesson and do the speaking task the following day.

Choose work German you will actually use

Start with one task this week, such as confirming a deadline. Say Kannst du die Liste bis Donnerstag prüfen? Then write a second line that says who will send the updated version. Next week, revise the same task with a different deadline and a formal Sie relationship.
