Three speaking rounds for an online call

Set a six-minute timer. Read a role, speak without the model, and let your partner respond. Reverse roles before moving on. If you are alone, record your side, pause and respond as the other person.

  1. A1, café: You want tea and need the total. Your partner says the price.
  2. A2, appointment: Today no longer works. Offer one new time, then let your partner reject it and suggest another.
  3. B1, choosing a plan: Your partner prefers the faster route. Propose the cheaper route and explain when it would still get you there on time.
Compare model lines

A1: Einen Tee, bitte. Was kostet das? — Das macht drei Euro.

A2: Heute schaffe ich es leider nicht. Geht Freitag um vier? — Um vier arbeite ich noch. Geht es um sechs?

B1: Ich würde den Bus nehmen, weil er günstiger ist. Wenn wir um acht losfahren, sind wir trotzdem pünktlich da.

Give feedback that improves the next round

Ask your partner to repeat the price, appointment or departure time you agreed on. If their answer differs from yours, check which detail was unclear. Then correct one phrase and try the round again with a new number. For a teacher session, send your level and one recording or sentence you struggled with.
