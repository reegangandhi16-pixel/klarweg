Practise asking, confirming and handing over information

Train the language in short rounds. Ask someone to explain a timeline, repeat it back in everyday words, then summarise what was said for a colleague. Use fictional examples during practice. If you are planning to work as a physician in Germany, verify the language and recognition procedures with the relevant authority. See the federal guide for international physicians.

Try a timeline clarification

A fictional patient says their discomfort started over the weekend and changed yesterday. Ask when it began and how it changed. Summarise only what the person confirms. Practise the exchange first with the patient, then with a colleague.

Compare plain-language and handover wording

Wann genau hat es angefangen? Was hat sich seit gestern verändert? Habe ich richtig verstanden, dass es am Samstag angefangen hat?

Die Person berichtet, dass die Beschwerden am Samstag begonnen hätten. Wie sie sich verändert haben, ist noch nicht geklärt.

Explain what you heard in the first turn. In the handover, berichtet attributes the report to the speaker. State explicitly when a detail is still open. This is a language drill.

Keep one fact separate from one unanswered question

If a fictional patient says only Seit dem Wochenende, ask Meinen Sie Samstag oder Sonntag? If they are unsure, summarise Die Beschwerden bestehen nach Angaben der Person seit dem Wochenende. Der genaue Beginn ist unklar. In the next round give an exact day, then check that your summary changes rather than assuming the date.
