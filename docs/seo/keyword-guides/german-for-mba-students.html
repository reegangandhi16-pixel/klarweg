Build a vocabulary list from decisions, not departments

Choose one seminar case and write the phrases you need to summarise a decision, challenge an assumption and agree on a next step. If your programme is taught in English, use German practice for the internships, group projects or conversations you actually expect. Verify entry-language requirements with the programme itself.

Present a recommendation with an assumption

A fictional service company can open in two cities. City A costs less, and City B has more existing customers. Recommend one location, identify a key assumption and propose how to test it.

Compare one short case response

Ich würde zunächst Stadt A wählen, weil die laufenden Kosten dort niedriger sind. Die Empfehlung setzt voraus, dass wir genügend neue Kundschaft gewinnen. Das könnten wir mit einem zeitlich begrenzten Pilotprojekt prüfen.

Separate recommendation, assumption and test. Now argue for City B and ask your partner how much additional demand would justify the additional cost.

Run a three-person case discussion

The first person presents the recommendation. The second asks Welche Annahme ist für diese Empfehlung entscheidend? The third summarises the next action: Wir prüfen die Nachfrage vier Wochen lang und vergleichen danach die Kosten. Swap roles and change the assumption from demand to operating cost.
