Try the same test in any beginner app

Open one lesson on the device you normally use. Choose a new German word, listen to it, say it, then recall it from the meaning with the lesson closed. You want an app that makes all four steps easy.

Do a five-minute Klarweg test on your phone

Open the free A1 chapter in your browser and try one German sound. Listen, repeat it twice, and come back after a short break to see what you remember. If you use an app alongside a class, bring one phrase from each lesson to the next live session so you can use it with a person.

What to check before you pay for an app

Look for access duration, what you can try before buying, feedback on speaking or writing, and whether you can use it on the devices you already own. If offline use matters, test it before subscribing.

Try one mini lesson before choosing the app

Say Ich lerne Deutsch aloud. Hide the German and recall it from “I’m learning German.” Next, say “I live in Pune” as Ich wohne in Pune. Compare whether the app lets you see the model only after your attempt and whether it explains what to correct. If pronunciation matters to you, try the same word again on your phone with headphones.
