Start with two sound groups

Say ich, Licht and nicht with the tongue raised near the hard palate. Say ach, Bach and Buch with the sound made farther back. Keep air moving in both. These are common patterns; borrowed words and regional pronunciation can behave differently.

If playback is unavailable, ask a partner to read the scripts aloud.

Check forward ch examples

Ich. Licht. Nicht. Das Licht ist an.

Listen to the ch after i.

Check back ch examples

Ach. Bach. Buch. Ich lese das Buch.

Compare the ch in ach and Buch with the sound in ich.

Practise two phrases, then switch

Say Ich lese das Buch. Mark ich as the forward sound and Buch as the back sound. Compare Licht and Loch. Ask a partner or use the available German voice to hear the examples before recording yourself.

Check one possible answer

Ich lese das Buch. Das Licht ist an. Das Loch ist klein.

Produce the contrast without forcing the sound. If the two sounds merge, return to ich and ach at a slow pace before retrying the full sentence.
