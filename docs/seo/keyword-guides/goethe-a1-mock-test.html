Try four mini tasks before a full A1 mock

This is original Klarweg practice, with one short task for each skill. It does not produce an official Goethe score. For full-length official model material, see the Goethe-Institut A1 practice page.

  1. Listening: Have a partner open the read-aloud script below. Listen for the day, arrival time and room without reading it yourself.
  2. Reading: Read Am Freitag schließt die Bibliothek schon um 15 Uhr. Am Samstag ist sie von 9 bis 12 Uhr geöffnet. Can you visit Friday at 16:00? What are Saturday’s hours?
  3. Writing: You cannot come to a friend’s dinner on Monday. Write a short message that apologises, explains you are working and offers Wednesday.
  4. Speaking: Say your name and city, ask what time your partner can meet and ask them to speak more slowly.
Read-aloud script for your practice partner

Guten Tag. Ihr Deutschkurs beginnt am Dienstag. Bitte kommen Sie um Viertel vor neun in Raum zwölf.
