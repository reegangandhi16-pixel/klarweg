Choose one of three request cards

At a café, ask for a glass of water. In class, ask someone to open the window. At reception, ask the person to repeat an address. Say all three before checking the model. These are original drills rather than official exam items.

Practise taking both sides

Ask for water and reply that sparkling water is available. Then repeat with the classroom and reception cards. If you cannot help, state that politely and offer an alternative if there is one.

Check one possible answer

Könnte ich bitte ein Glas Wasser bekommen? Ja, gern. Möchten Sie Wasser mit oder ohne Kohlensäure?

The listener should know exactly what you want. For the other cards, try Können Sie bitte das Fenster öffnen? and Können Sie die Adresse bitte wiederholen?

Make a request and react

Try each item before opening its answer. Use the feedback to correct one thing, then try again.

  1. Ask for a pen. Your partner has no pen but offers a pencil. Accept the pencil.

    Check your answer

    Haben Sie bitte einen Stift für mich? — Ich habe nur einen Bleistift. — Ja, danke. Ein Bleistift ist auch gut.

    Acknowledge the item offered, since it is different from the one you asked for.

  2. You can’t hear. Ask the other person to speak more slowly. When the person repeats the sentence clearly, thank them.

    Check your answer

    Können Sie bitte langsamer sprechen? — Ja, natürlich. — Jetzt verstehe ich es. Vielen Dank.

    Use langsamer when speed is the problem. Use wiederholen when you need to hear the same words again.

  3. Your partner asks you to open the window. It is already open. Reply politely.

    Check your answer

    Das Fenster ist schon offen. Soll ich die Tür auch öffnen?

    Answer the request before making another suggestion.
