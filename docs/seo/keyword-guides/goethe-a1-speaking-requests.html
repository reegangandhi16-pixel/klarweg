Choose one of three request cards

At a café, ask for a glass of water. In class, ask someone to open the window. At reception, ask the person to repeat an address. Say all three before checking the model. These are original drills rather than official exam items.

Practise taking both sides

Ask for water and reply that sparkling water is available. Then repeat with the classroom and reception cards. If you cannot help, state that politely and offer an alternative if there is one.

Check one possible answer

Könnte ich bitte ein Glas Wasser bekommen? Ja, gern. Möchten Sie Wasser mit oder ohne Kohlensäure?

The listener should know exactly what you want. For the other cards, try Können Sie bitte das Fenster öffnen? and Können Sie die Adresse bitte wiederholen?
