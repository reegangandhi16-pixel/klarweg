Choose a partner card

Study session: you can only meet after six. Your partner needs a quiet place. Neighbourhood event: you can bring food, but your partner cannot carry tables. Trip: you need a low-cost ticket, while your partner must arrive before noon. Pick one topic and keep your own constraint private until your partner makes a proposal.

Close the discussion with a clear agreement

Once you agree, say the time, place and who will do what. If there is no agreement yet, ask a specific follow-up instead.

Check one possible answer

Dann treffen wir uns am Donnerstag um halb sieben in der Bibliothek. Ich bringe die Aufgaben mit, und du reservierst einen Raum. Habe ich das richtig zusammengefasst?

Ask your partner to retell the agreement without notes. If their summary differs, clarify the one detail they missed.
