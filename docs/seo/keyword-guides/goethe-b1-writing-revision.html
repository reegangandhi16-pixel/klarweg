Find what the first draft leaves out

Prompt: you borrowed a book from Lea and cannot return it on Monday. Explain why, propose Tuesday or Wednesday and ask which day suits her. Draft: Hallo Lea, ich kann am Montag nicht kommen. Bis bald. Identify the missing reason, alternative and question.

Write a clearer second draft

Keep it brief, but include all requested points. Then change the message for someone you address formally and keep the time and action clear.

Check one possible answer

Hallo Lea, ich kann dir das Buch am Montag leider nicht zurückbringen, weil ich länger arbeiten muss. Ich könnte es dir am Dienstag oder Mittwoch vorbeibringen. Welcher Tag passt dir besser? Liebe Grüße, Neha

Mark each requested point in your draft. Only then check spelling and verb order. The verb muss sits at the end of the weil clause.
