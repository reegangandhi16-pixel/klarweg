Compare weekly work before comparing finish dates

Write down live teaching hours, expected homework and time for speaking feedback. Ask what “intensive” means for that specific course. Check course dates, time zone, makeup policy, and whether the listing covers the whole level or just a part. The Goethe-Institut India online course listings illustrate why formats and schedules need to be compared individually.

These are sample schedules. Verify actual teacher and batch availability before committing.

Trial the workload for seven days

Block your proposed times in IST. Do a short lesson on the first day, explain one phrase from memory after 24 hours, and give a short spoken answer at the end of the week. If the review sessions are the first things you skip, reduce contact hours or protect a different block before choosing a course.

One question to send to a provider

“How many live hours and independent study hours are expected each week? Could you share the complete dates, level coverage, and what happens if I miss a class?”

Estimate what you can actually finish

Suppose you have six free hours each week. A programme requiring eight hours of classes and three of homework exceeds that budget by five hours. Either protect five additional hours, choose a lighter schedule, or use self-paced study and book targeted feedback. Trial the calendar for a week before paying.
