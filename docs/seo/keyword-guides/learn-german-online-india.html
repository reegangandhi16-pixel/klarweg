Pick your route by goal

Most learners in India learn German for one of four goals. The goal decides the level you need, whether you need an exam, and how much speaking feedback to pay for. Klarweg prices were checked on 22 November 2026; Goethe exam fees are for exam dates from January 2027.

Self-paced, live or both?

  • Irregular hours: self-paced lessons in short sessions. Klarweg courses explain grammar with Hindi support and include pronunciation audio.
  • A fixed weekly slot and you want a teacher: live classes. Klarweg lists group classes at ₹16,000 per level (₹13,000 with code GROUP3000), but no batches have dates yet. A 20-minute ₹100 demo can be booked when slots are open.
  • Exam within three months: self-paced study for grammar and vocabulary, plus timed practice of all four exam parts. Try the A1 mini mock to see where you stand.

Run a first-week starting check

Say your name, city and reason for learning. Then ask one simple question. If that is already easy, explain what you did last weekend instead and note where you get stuck.

Check one possible answer

Ich heiße Ravi. Ich wohne in Jaipur. Ich lerne Deutsch für meine Arbeit. Warum lernst du Deutsch?

If you could say this without help, start A1 at Chapter 1 and move quickly; if not, plan the full A1 path. If last weekend was easy to describe in German, try the free A2 Chapter 1 instead.
