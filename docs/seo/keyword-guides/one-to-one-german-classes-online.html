When one-to-one German makes sense

Choose an individual class if you have a specific speaking or writing problem you want the teacher to hear and correct. If you mainly need a predictable timetable and peer conversation, compare a small group as well. Ask whether individual sessions follow a full course syllabus or focus on selected goals.

  • For a beginner, bring a 30-second introduction.
  • For an exam task, bring one attempt and ask for feedback on task coverage.
  • For work, bring a fictional version of a conversation you actually need.

Use the demo to test one correction

Start with Ich wohne in Delhi und ich arbeite in einem Hotel. Change the city and job to your own. Ask the teacher to point out one thing to improve, then say the revised sentence without reading it. A useful demo gives you a correction you can use in your next attempt.

Compare two teachers after each trial

Write down what you were asked to do, the correction you understood, whether you could repeat the phrase independently, and the times actually available. Check total fee, session length, cancellation terms and practice between sessions.

What to send in the first message

“I’m learning German at A2 and want help speaking at work. I’m available Tuesday or Thursday after 7 p.m. IST. Can we use part of the trial session for a short workplace role-play?”
