Try a first-class introduction

Say your name and city out loud. Then ask a classmate where they live. Use Hindi as a cue if you forget the next line.

See a model with Hindi cues

Ich heiße Asha. Ich wohne in Delhi. Wo wohnst du?

मेरा नाम आशा है। मैं दिल्ली में रहती हूँ। तुम कहाँ रहते हो?

Change both personal details, then say the three lines without reading. If you are speaking to a stranger formally, ask Wo wohnen Sie?

Take one question to your demo

“I’m starting from zero and can study on Tuesday and Saturday in IST. Could we practise a short introduction, then discuss the next available beginner schedule?” Decide after the demo whether you can reproduce one correction independently.
