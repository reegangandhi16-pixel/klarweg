Decide whether you are ready for A2

Try telling someone where you live, when you work and what you did yesterday. If you can manage the first two but need help with the third, A2 practice can build on your existing skills. If you still need a script for the first two, review the A1 foundations first.

Try an A2 appointment role-play

You booked a haircut for Tuesday at three. Say you cannot come, give one reason and ask for Thursday after four. Have a partner offer Thursday at five and repeat the details back.

Check one possible answer

Am Dienstag um drei kann ich leider nicht, weil ich arbeiten muss. Wäre Donnerstag nach vier Uhr möglich? Donnerstag um fünf passt mir gut.

Check for the original time, a reason and a confirmed new time. Bring your first attempt to a demo if you want feedback on word order and speaking.
