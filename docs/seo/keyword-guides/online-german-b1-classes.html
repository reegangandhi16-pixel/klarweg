Check your B1 starting point

Before choosing a class, try to explain one recent event, give an opinion with a reason and ask a follow-up question. If you can get the main message across but need help with longer turns or linking ideas, bring that exact gap to a teacher. If basic present and past forms still stop you frequently, review Klarweg A2 first.

  • Self-paced: Use the B1 roadmap if you want regular lessons and can schedule your own practice.
  • Live group: Ask how often you will speak and how the teacher corrects you.
  • One-to-one: Bring a specific goal, such as explaining a process at work or improving a B1 speaking response. Verify available times and fees before enrolling.

Try a B1 speaking task before booking

Your team wants to meet every Friday. Explain why Thursday would be easier for you, propose a time and ask whether it works for the others.

Review a model and try a follow-up

Donnerstag wäre für mich besser, weil ich freitags länger arbeite. Könnten wir uns donnerstags um vier treffen? Passt das für euch?

Check for a reason, an alternative and an actual question. If the teacher says Thursday at four is too early, suggest a new time without repeating your original answer.
