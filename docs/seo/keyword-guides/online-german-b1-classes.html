Are you ready for B1? Six quick questions

B1 builds on A2 grammar. If you miss more than two of these, finish A2 first. Nothing is saved.

1. Which sentence is correct?

weil sends the conjugated verb to the end of the clause: … weil ich in Deutschland arbeiten will.

2. “Last weekend I went to Pune.” Which is correct?

Verbs of movement form the Perfekt with sein, and the verb stays in second place after a time phrase.

3. Which sentence politely asks for help?

Könnten Sie …? (Konjunktiv II) is the polite request form you need throughout B1.

4. Choose the right word: “Der Kurs, ___ ich besuche, ist online.”

In the relative clause, Kurs is the object of besuche, so it takes the accusative: den.

5. Choose the right word: “Ich interessiere mich ___ Politik.”

sich interessieren für is a fixed verb–preposition pair. B1 has many of these.

6. “If I had time, I would come.” Which is correct?

An unreal condition uses Konjunktiv II: hätte … würde … kommen. Option a is a real condition (“if I have time, I come”).

Try a B1 speaking task before you book

Your team wants to meet every Friday. Explain why Thursday would be easier for you, propose a time and ask whether it works for the others.

Review a model and try a follow-up

Donnerstag wäre für mich besser, weil ich freitags länger arbeite. Könnten wir uns donnerstags um vier treffen? Passt das für euch?

Check for a reason, an alternative and a real question. If someone says Thursday at four is too early, suggest a new time without repeating your first answer. Bring this task to a demo if you want a teacher to correct it.
