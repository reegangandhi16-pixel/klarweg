Course fee and certificate fee are two separate payments

An online German course teaches you the language. A certificate that a visa office, employer or university accepts usually comes from a separate language exam, such as the Goethe-Zertifikat, telc or ÖSD. You pay the exam centre directly. Before you buy any course “with certificate”, ask the provider what kind of document they mean.

DocumentWho issues itTypically accepted for visas, jobs or Ausbildung?
Course completion or attendance certificateThe course providerUsually not as proof of a language level. It shows that you took a course.
Language exam certificate (Goethe, telc, ÖSD and others)The exam provider, after you pass an examThis is what visa offices, employers and universities normally ask for. Always confirm which exams and level they accept.

Klarweg does not issue certificates. Klarweg courses prepare you for the level. You book and pay for the exam separately with an exam centre.

Compare two course offers correctly

A course that says “with certificate” can look more expensive or cheaper than it really is. Compare total cost to the certificate you actually need.

Example: Offer A charges ₹4,000 for tuition only. Offer B charges ₹7,000 for tuition and its own completion certificate. If your visa needs the Goethe A1 exam, both still need the ₹10,000 exam fee: ₹14,000 against ₹17,000. Offer B’s certificate does not replace the exam.

Copy these questions for any course provider
  1. What is the full fee, and which level and access period does it include?
  2. What is the exact name of the certificate you issue, and is it an exam result or a course completion document?
  3. Is registration for an external exam included? If so, which provider, level and date?
