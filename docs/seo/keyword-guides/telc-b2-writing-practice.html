Read the facts and write your own complaint

You registered for a weekend workshop advertised as including two feedback sessions. After attending, you received only one. Write to the organiser. Mention the promised sessions, describe what actually happened, and request either the missing session or a concrete alternative.

Compare your complaint with a model

Review your draft for a subject, facts, a request and a formal ending. Check whether a reader could act on what you wrote.
