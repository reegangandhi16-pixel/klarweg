Compare courses against your actual TestDaF goal

Before paying, record which exam and results your university accepts and when it needs the proof. Then use the official digital TestDaF sample tasks to see which academic activities you need to practise. Ask a provider to show you how their course uses those task types and where you get individual feedback.

  • Format: Will you practise in the same medium as your chosen exam?
  • Feedback: Will somebody mark a full writing response and review a recorded speaking response?
  • Retesting: Can you revisit the same skill with a fresh task after feedback?

Try a source-comparison warm-up

In a fictional student survey, 60 of 100 respondents prefer recordings, while 45 say live sessions help them ask questions. Write two sentences that report both figures without assuming respondents could choose only one option.

Compare a model response

Sechzig der hundert Befragten bevorzugen Aufzeichnungen. Zugleich gaben fünfundvierzig Personen an, dass ihnen Live-Sitzungen das Nachfragen erleichtern.

The figures may overlap. Keep the two findings separate and avoid claiming a causal effect. This warm-up is original and shorter than a full exam task.
