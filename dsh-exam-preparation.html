DSH Exam Preparation: Plan and Writing Practice

Quick answer

Prepare for the DSH with your target institution’s published rules and samples. Practise turning a source into a short summary and separating its findings from your interpretation. The exercise on this page gives you a starting point.

Use the rules of your target institution

Find the language requirement for your specific programme. Then open the DSH instructions and sample material published by the institution where you plan to sit the exam. Write down the required result, booking deadline, task types and permitted materials. The DAAD overview of German language requirements is a useful starting point for understanding language evidence.

If two programmes use different wording, keep two checklists and ask admissions about anything unclear before booking.

Try one short source-based response

A fictional university reports that longer library opening hours increased evening visits. Its student council says commuters still leave early because of transport. Summarise the finding and the limitation, then propose one question for a follow-up survey.

Compare a possible academic response

Längere Öffnungszeiten gingen mit mehr Besuchen am Abend einher. Für Pendelnde kann der öffentliche Nahverkehr allerdings weiterhin eine Hürde darstellen. Eine Befragung könnte klären, welche Verbindungen nach neunzehn Uhr fehlen.

Separate observation, possible limitation and next question. Use your institution’s posted model for actual timing and scoring. This task practises academic language.

Rehearse source-based writing in four steps

Underline the claim about evening visits. Mark the student council’s transport concern as a separate view. Write one sentence reporting the finding, a second explaining the limitation, and a final question that could test it. For a retry, replace the transport concern with work schedules and revise only the last two sentences.
