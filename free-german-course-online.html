A free first week for complete beginners

  1. Day 1: open A1 Chapter 1 and repeat the alphabet sounds aloud with the audio.
  2. Day 2: learn to give your name and where you live with the self-introduction practice. Try it before reading the model.
  3. Day 3: do the sein exercises and say each answer aloud.
  4. Day 4: do the der, die, das exercises.
  5. Day 5: try the first three exercises in A1 listening practice.
  6. Day 6: print the A1 worksheet and do exercises 1–10.
  7. Day 7: record the check below and redo any exercise you got wrong this week.

Check what you can do on day seven

Record yourself giving your name, city and one language you speak. Listen back and repeat the line that was hardest.

Example first-week recording

Ich heiße Nisha. Ich wohne in Jaipur. Ich spreche Hindi.

Change the three details to your own. If you can say them without reading, continue with the 30-day A1 study plan. If reacting to a person in real time is the hard part, a 20-minute ₹100 teacher demo shows you what guided speaking practice feels like.
