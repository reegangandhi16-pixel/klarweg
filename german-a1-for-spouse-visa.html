A1 German for a spouse visa from India

A1 German is relevant to many spouse-reunification applications from India, but language proof is not required in every case. Check your official route first, then use this guide to plan language study and practise basic communication.

Last checked 9 November 2026 · Original learning material

Quick answer

  • Check your spouse’s nationality and exact German residence status before booking a language examination.
  • If language proof applies, check the mission’s current certificate requirements and your personalised document list.
  • Use A1 study to build communication skills; a language course does not decide visa eligibility.

Do Indian spouses always need A1 German?

No. The German Missions in India spouse guidance treats language proof as conditional on the application route. It distinguishes cases by the reference person’s status. The government’s skilled-worker family guidance says German-language proof is not required for family members joining a skilled worker with a valid residence title. Confirm the route that applies to your circumstances in the official portal.

Before paying for a test, write down your partner’s nationality and the exact residence-title information. Use that information when answering the official route questionnaire. A friend’s experience may involve a different residence category, even if both couples are Indian. Do not infer your requirement from a social-media checklist or a course advertisement.

Keep two decisions separate in your planning notebook: what evidence the application needs, and what German you want to learn for everyday life. Someone who does not need language proof may still want beginner lessons. Someone who does need proof must check the accepted examination rather than assuming that any course completion document will serve the same purpose.

Check the certificate requirement before booking

Where proof applies, the India mission page specifies a recognised certificate at A1 or higher, with the exam taken no more than one year earlier. It names Goethe/Max Mueller Bhawan, ÖSD and telc examples. This is the mission’s evidence rule for this route, not a general claim that every German certificate expires after a year. Read the current language-certificate section.

Check the exact examination name with the provider and compare it with the mission guidance. Save the current instructions and record when you checked them. If your certificate or circumstances do not fit the stated instructions, ask the responsible mission how to proceed before paying for another examination.

Prepare communication skills alongside the proof

Begin with the language you need to give basic personal information, ask simple questions and understand short everyday messages. Make your own information accurate: name, address, phone number and routine. Keep sensitive personal data out of public practice groups. Fictional details are enough for exercises shared with other learners.

Practise reading a short message and identifying its purpose before translating every word. Who is writing, what action is requested and which date or place matters? Then practise listening for the same types of details. A written word that you recognise immediately may still be difficult to identify in a spoken sentence.

For writing, work on a brief relevant message with a greeting, the requested information and a suitable ending. For speaking, practise giving a direct answer and asking for repetition when needed. The Goethe A1 overview links preparation to the examination. Use the provider’s actual practice material for the exam format you book, rather than treating the exercises here as an official simulation.

Original A1 practice with Hindi meaning checks

The following fictional examples are for language learning. They are not visa-interview scripts, official exam items or model statements about your personal circumstances. Change details only when you understand the words and grammar you are using.

Original Klarweg practice

Ich heiße Mira. Ich komme aus Indien. Ich wohne in Pune und lerne Deutsch.

My name is Mira. I come from India. I live in Pune and am learning German. Practise answering a single question about one detail, rather than always reciting all four sentences.

Ich lerne Deutsch. means मैं जर्मन सीख रही हूँ / सीख रहा हूँ। (main German seekh rahi hoon / seekh raha hoon). The German sentence stays the same for these two Hindi speaker-gender forms. Use the Hindi to check meaning, and the German spelling and lesson audio to practise sound.

Original Klarweg practice

A: Wann haben Sie Zeit?
B: Am Freitag habe ich Zeit.
A: Um wie viel Uhr?
B: Um drei Uhr.

A asks when B is available, then asks for a time. B supplies the requested information. Change the day and time, and ask your partner to write down what they heard.

Original Klarweg practice

Guten Tag, ich kann am Montag nicht kommen. Haben Sie am Dienstag einen Termin? Vielen Dank.

Hello, I cannot come on Monday. Do you have an appointment on Tuesday? Thank you. Identify the two jobs in this short message: explain unavailability and ask about an alternative.

After each example, close the text and reconstruct its meaning in English or Hindi. Then produce a new German version using a different day, city or time. If a changed word makes the sentence uncertain, ask a teacher to check that change before memorising the result. Use the A1 Sprechen examples and A1 Schreiben examples for longer task-focused practice.

Build a preparation plan from your starting point

Start with a small baseline check. Can you understand a simple question about yourself without seeing the text? Can you read a short appointment message and identify the time? Can you write a few connected details without copying a model? Keep the result descriptive. It tells you what to practise next; it does not provide an official level or a promised exam outcome.

Divide study between learning new language and using earlier material. For example, one session can introduce useful words and question patterns, while another uses them in a short exchange. Include reading and listening from the beginning. Waiting until the last week to hear spoken German can leave a large gap between your notebook knowledge and practical understanding.

Before choosing an examination date, try current official practice for your selected provider and arrange feedback on speaking and writing. Allow time to act on that feedback. Course duration, your available hours, test availability and official application arrangements are different planning inputs; a fixed “finish A1 in a few days” slogan cannot settle them.

Keep a study log with the activity, what you could do independently and the next correction. Replace “studied German” with something specific, such as “understood an appointment time after one replay.” Specific records make your next session easier to plan and show whether repeated practice is addressing the actual difficulty.

Avoid common planning mistakes

Do not confuse attendance with demonstrated language ability. Ask what a course teaches, what feedback it includes and which external exam, if any, you are preparing for. Klarweg provides learning material; it does not issue a Goethe certificate or decide a visa application.

Do not learn answers that describe somebody else’s life. Use truthful personal information when it is appropriate to the real situation, and fictional information for shared learning exercises. The aim is to understand and respond, not to repeat a paragraph whose meaning you cannot explain.

Do not assume an exemption from a single label such as “working in Germany.” Check the exact official route and seek clarification on unresolved points. Keep current official guidance as your reference when planning proof; use the A1 vocabulary guide to support the separate work of building everyday language.

Questions

Is A1 compulsory for every Indian spouse?

No. The requirement depends on the route and applicable circumstances. Start with the India mission’s current guidance and the personalised official document list.

Can I use a course attendance certificate?

Do not assume it replaces recognised language-exam evidence. Compare the exact document with the mission’s language-proof instructions and clarify uncertainty before booking.

Does my certificate expire after one year?

The India spouse-route page currently sets a maximum age for the exam used as evidence. That route-specific submission rule should not be confused with a universal certificate-expiry statement.

Should I study if language proof does not apply?

You can still choose to build everyday German for communication. Keep that learning decision separate from the evidence required by your application route.

Can Klarweg guarantee a visa or an A1 pass?

No. Learning activities and feedback support preparation. An examination result and a visa decision are made by their respective responsible bodies.

Official sources and scope

German Missions in India: joining a spouse or civil partner — route distinctions and current language-proof instructions. Make it in Germany: spouses joining non-EU citizens — skilled-worker family guidance. Both opened 9 November 2026. Recheck the official instructions when planning an application; individual questions belong with the responsible mission.
