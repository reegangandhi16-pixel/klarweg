German A1 Syllabus: Topics, Grammar and Exam Parts

Quick answer

German A1 covers sounds and numbers, introductions and questions, present-tense verbs and articles, Akkusativ and Dativ, the Perfekt and everyday topics like food, time and family. The Goethe A1 exam tests all four skills; 60 of 100 points pass.

What A1 means

At A1 you can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very simple sentences: introduce yourself, ask and answer simple personal questions, and get by in basic situations if the other person speaks slowly. The Goethe-Institut estimates 80–200 lessons of 45 minutes to reach it.

The A1 syllabus, topic by topic

This is the order of Klarweg’s 47 A1 chapters. Other courses group topics differently, but an A1 course that skips one of these rows will leave a gap in the exam.

ChaptersTopics
1–3Alphabet and spelling, vowels and umlauts, numbers 0–100
4–7Greetings, introducing yourself, W-questions, yes/no questions
9–13Present-tense verbs, der/die/das, kein and nicht, Akkusativ, adjectives with sein
14–16, 21–24Food, café, hobbies, the clock, jobs, family and possessives, dates and ordinal numbers
18–20, 25Irregular verbs, word order, modal verbs, separable verbs

36–42Dativ, prepositions with Dativ, two-way prepositions, places, Dativ verbs and pronouns, welcher/dieser

What the A1 exam checks

PartTimeWhat you doTry it now
Hörenabout 20 min3 parts: everyday conversations, announcements, phone messagesHave a partner say Der Kurs beginnt um halb zehn. Write the time.
Lesen25 min3 parts: notes and emails, small ads, signs and noticesHeute geöffnet von zehn bis zwölf. Can you arrive at 13:00?
Schreiben20 minFill a form; write a message of about 30 wordsInvite a friend to meet on Saturday at 10 at the station.
Sprechen15 min, in a groupIntroduce yourself, ask and answer questions, make and answer requestsAsk where the station is, then ask the person to repeat.

You need 60 of 100 points; below 35 of the 75 written points you are not admitted to speaking. Full guide: Goethe A1 exam.

Check the four “try it now” answers

Listening: 9:30. Reading: no, it closes at 12:00.

Writing: Hallo Sam, hast du am Samstag Zeit? Treffen wir uns um zehn am Bahnhof? Viele Grüße, Ana

Speaking: Entschuldigung, wo ist der Bahnhof? Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?

Tick off what you can already do

  • Spell my name and say my phone number.
  • Introduce myself in five sentences without notes.
  • Ask the time, the price and the way.
  • Use der/die/das and kein correctly in simple sentences.
  • Say what I did yesterday with the Perfekt.
  • Write a 30-word message that covers three points.

All six: try the A1 mini mock. Fewer: the chapter table above shows where to go back.
