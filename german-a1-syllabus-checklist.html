German A1 Syllabus and Skills Checklist

Quick answer

German A1 covers basic everyday communication: hear names and times, read short notices, write simple messages and take part in brief exchanges. Try one task for each skill below and check the sample answers.

Four things to be able to do at A1

SkillWhat to practiseTry it now
ListeningListen for names, numbers, times and places.Have a partner say Der Kurs beginnt um halb zehn. Write the time without reading it.
ReadingFind useful information in notices, messages and forms.Heute geöffnet von zehn bis zwölf. Can you arrive at 13:00?
WritingFill basic details and send a short message.Invite a friend to meet Saturday at 10 by the station.
SpeakingIntroduce yourself, ask a simple question and make a request.Ask where the station is and ask the person to repeat their reply.
Check all four answers

Listening: 09:30. Reading: No, it closes at 12:00.

Writing: Hallo Sam, hast du am Samstag Zeit? Treffen wir uns um zehn am Bahnhof? Viele Grüße, Ana

Speaking: Wo ist der Bahnhof? Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?

Use grammar to make each task easier

Practise present-tense verbs for introducing yourself, W-questions for gathering information, basic articles and cases for requests, and time expressions for schedules. Choose one unfinished skill from the table and pick the grammar point that helps with it. Compare the full topic sequence in the Klarweg A1 grammar syllabus.
