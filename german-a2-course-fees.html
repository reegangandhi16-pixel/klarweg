German A2 Course Fees: Compare Klarweg Plans

Quick answer

Klarweg lists self-paced A2 at ₹2,999 for two years and Lifetime A1–C2 at ₹4,999. The first A2 chapter is free. Live teaching and external exam fees are separate.

Compare what you will actually use

Choose A2-only if you want to focus on the next level and two years of access fits your plan. If you also expect to study A1 or B1 and beyond, compare the single Lifetime payment. The listed difference is ₹2,000. Check current checkout terms before paying.

Calculate a full A2 budget

Write down your tuition, any teacher fee, the exam centre’s quote and travel. Using hypothetical exam and travel costs of ₹6,000 and ₹800, what total would you budget with A2-only?

Check one possible answer

₹2,999 + ₹6,000 + ₹800 = ₹9,799.

The exam and travel values are examples. Replace them with your actual quotes. If you only want to learn A2 and do not need a certificate, remove the exam line from the comparison.
