German A2 Course Fees: Self-Paced, Live and Exam Costs

Quick answer

Klarweg’s self-paced A2 course costs ₹2,999 one-time for two years, and Lifetime A1–C2 costs ₹4,999. Live A2 classes are listed at ₹16,000 (group) or ₹22,000 (one-to-one) before coupons, but no batches are open yet. The Goethe A2 exam is separate: ₹12,000 for dates from January 2027.

Is A2-only or Lifetime better value?

Choose A2 only if you have finished A1 elsewhere and only need A2 within the next two years. Choose Lifetime if you also need A1 revision or plan to continue to B1. B1 alone costs ₹3,999, so A2 plus B1 bought separately is ₹6,998, more than Lifetime at ₹4,999.

Check your understanding: what would A2 and B1 bought separately, plus the Goethe B1 exam, cost?

₹2,999 + ₹3,999 + ₹20,000 = ₹26,998. With Lifetime instead of two single levels, the same plan costs ₹24,999.

Try A2 before paying

Open the free A2 Chapter 1. If the explanations are easy to follow, you are ready for A2. If you get stuck on basic word order or verb endings, review A1 first. Then try the A2 mini practice test to find your weakest skill.
