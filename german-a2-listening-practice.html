German A2 Listening Practice: Catch the Change

Quick answer

At A2, listen for a correction after words such as doch, statt and ab. Try the three short messages on this page and identify what actually applies. Playback uses a German voice on your device when available.

On this page

  • Listen for the new detail
  • Explain what changed

Listen for the new detail

Write the pickup time, the entrance to use and the item to bring. In each clip, distinguish the original information from the instruction that still applies.

If playback is unavailable, ask a partner to read the scripts aloud.

Check pickup time

Die Abholung ist nicht um drei, sondern erst um fünf.

Pick up at five. Nicht … sondern replaces the earlier time.

Check entrance

Der Haupteingang ist geschlossen. Benutzen Sie bitte den Eingang im Hof.

Use the courtyard entrance. The main entrance is closed.

Check required item

Bitte bringen Sie statt der Eintrittskarte einen Ausweis mit.

Bring an ID instead of the ticket. Statt signals what is replaced.

Explain what changed

Use the transcript to underline the changed detail. Then have a partner replace one detail and repeat it back.

Check one possible answer

Die Abholung ist erst um fünf, der Eingang ist im Hof, und ich brauche einen Ausweis.

If you remembered the first time or entrance instead, listen for the contrast word immediately before the correction.
