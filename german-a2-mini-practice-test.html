Try reading and listening

Read Der Kochkurs beginnt am Freitag erst um 18 Uhr. Bitte bringen Sie eine Schürze mit. When does the course start, and what should you bring?

For listening, ask a partner to open the script and read it aloud while you look away. Where should you wait for the bus?

Read-aloud script for a partner

Der Bus hält heute wegen Bauarbeiten am Rathaus statt an der Schule.

Check the reading and listening answers

Friday at 18:00, with an apron. Wait at the town hall. Statt introduces the stop the bus is replacing.

Try writing and speaking

Write to a friend to move your Sunday picnic because of rain. Suggest an indoor place and a new time. Then say your proposal aloud and ask whether it works.

Check one possible answer

Hallo Lea, am Sonntag soll es regnen. Treffen wir uns lieber um zwei im Café am Bahnhof? Passt dir das?

Check that you gave a reason, a new place and a time. Repeat aloud with a different time. Score this by details communicated, then use official model materials for exam-specific rehearsal.
