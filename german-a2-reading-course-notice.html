Use this guide to find a changed room, preparation task and alternative contact action. Work through original German examples, Hindi meaning checks and exercises with explanations.

Understand this topic

A course message can change one practical detail while leaving others unchanged. Notice what remains the same before assuming that a room change also cancels the meeting. This original passage includes an action before the session and a response for learners who cannot open a file. All arrangements are fictional and do not describe a real Klarweg course, classroom or support policy.

Read the original passage

Original fictional text · not an official paper

Information zum nächsten Lesetreffen
Liebe Teilnehmerinnen und Teilnehmer,
unser Lesetreffen am Mittwoch beginnt wie geplant um 18 Uhr. Wir treffen uns diesmal jedoch nicht in Raum 12, sondern in Raum 8 im Erdgeschoss. Der andere Raum wird am Abend für eine Veranstaltung gebraucht.
Bitte lesen Sie vor dem Treffen die kurze Geschichte aus der letzten Nachricht. Notieren Sie zwei Stellen, die Sie interessant oder unklar finden. Sie müssen nicht jedes unbekannte Wort im Wörterbuch nachschlagen. Wir möchten zuerst über den Inhalt und die Figuren sprechen.
Wenn Sie die Datei nicht öffnen können, antworten Sie bitte auf diese Nachricht. Wir schicken Ihnen den Text dann in einer anderen Form. Eine gedruckte Ausgabe brauchen Sie nicht mitzubringen.
Am Ende des Treffens wählen wir gemeinsam den Text für die nächste Woche.
Viele Grüße
Mara Sommer

The fictional reading meeting remains on Wednesday at 18:00 but moves from room 12 to room 8. Participants should read the earlier story and note two interesting or unclear passages. They should reply if the file will not open. A printed copy is not required, and the next text will be chosen together.

Try the questions before reading the English overview again. The overview is a meaning check, not the source of extra facts to add to the passage. When a detail is absent, distinguish “not stated” from “false”: those are different reading decisions. Do not infer a real timetable, service policy or general research finding from this fictional text.

Try it yourself

Write a short answer to each question and identify the sentence or phrase that supports it. Do not use a likely real-world answer if the passage says something different. The key explains why the answer follows from this particular text.

  1. Does the start time change?
  2. Which room should participants use?
  3. What should they note before the meeting?
  4. What should someone do if the file will not open?
  5. Must a printed copy be brought?

Answers and explanations

Show answers after your attempt
  1. Does the start time change?

    No: it remains 18 Uhr. Wie geplant signals that the time stays as planned.

  2. Which room should participants use?

    Room 8 on the ground floor. Room 12 is the old room, explicitly rejected.

  3. What should they note before the meeting?

    Two interesting or unclear passages. The task is more specific than translating every word.

  4. What should someone do if the file will not open?

    Reply to the message. The organiser offers another form after that reply.

  5. Must a printed copy be brought?

    No. The message explicitly says it is not needed.

Use it in a new situation

Underline the unchanged information once and changed information twice. Then write a short reminder containing only day, time, new room and preparation task. Compare it against the original. If the reminder adds a cancellation or a new time, remove the unsupported detail rather than changing the source to match your memory.

Finish by writing a short summary without adding facts from outside the passage. Reopen the source and remove any detail you cannot support. Then choose one unfamiliar expression and study its role in the sentence. This combines meaning, evidence and vocabulary work instead of turning reading into the translation of isolated words.

Questions

Should I translate the entire passage first?

Try identifying its purpose and requested details first. Use the glossary for a focused meaning check, then return to the relevant sentence.

Can I answer from general knowledge?

Base comprehension answers on this fictional passage. A plausible real-world fact does not supply evidence for something the text does not say.

Does a correct answer establish my CEFR level?

No. One short reading activity cannot certify a level or predict a full examination result. The level in the title describes the intended practice context.

What should I do after checking the key?

Locate the supporting phrase for each answer, explain any error and summarise the text in your own words. Revisit a specific unclear expression rather than rereading without a purpose.
