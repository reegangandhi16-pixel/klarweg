Asking for Clarification in German with Hindi

Use this guide to identify what you did not understand and request useful clarification. Work through original German examples, Hindi meaning checks and exercises with explanations.

Original Klarweg learning material · 9 November 2026

Understand this topic

A clarification request can target sound, speed, meaning or confirmation. These are different problems. Repeating an unknown word more loudly may not explain its meaning, and translating the word may not help if you misheard a time. Choose the question that matches the difficulty. The model deliberately moves from an unclear instruction to a confirmed action, rather than simply saying yes to end the conversation.

A conversation to understand and adapt

Original fictional dialogue

A: Bitte schicken Sie die Datei bis morgen.
B: Entschuldigung, welche Datei meinen Sie?
A: Die Datei mit Ihrer kurzen Vorstellung.
B: Soll ich sie per E-Mail schicken?
A: Ja, bitte.
B: Habe ich Sie richtig verstanden: bis morgen per E-Mail?
A: Genau. Vielen Dank.
B: Gut, dann mache ich das so.

Read the meaning notes below before rehearsing with a partner.

Follow what each turn does
TurnMeaning
A: Bitte schicken Sie die Datei bis morgen.Please send the file by tomorrow.
B: Entschuldigung, welche Datei meinen Sie?Excuse me, which file do you mean?
A: Die Datei mit Ihrer kurzen Vorstellung.The file with your short introduction.
B: Soll ich sie per E-Mail schicken?Should I send it by email?
A: Ja, bitte.Yes, please.
B: Habe ich Sie richtig verstanden: bis morgen per E-Mail?Have I understood you correctly: by tomorrow, by email?
A: Genau. Vielen Dank.Exactly. Thank you.
B: Gut, dann mache ich das so.Good, then I will do it that way.

Read one turn, hide its response and decide what information the speaker needs next. Then uncover the answer and compare its function with your own. A different answer can be appropriate if it is clear, relevant and grammatical. Do not treat this single model as the only permitted wording for the situation.

Try it yourself

Respond to the prompts without reading the dialogue. For an open response, aim to perform the requested action with information you understand. The key gives one possible wording, not an official scoring rule. Compare the response’s purpose as well as its grammar.

  1. What detail is unclear first?
  2. Ask for an example.
  3. How should the file be sent in the model?

Answers and explanations

Show answers after your attempt
  1. What detail is unclear first?

    Which file is meant. The listener understood that a file was requested but not its identity.

  2. Ask for an example.

    Können Sie ein Beispiel geben? This targets meaning or application, not volume.

  3. How should the file be sent in the model?

    By email. The listener explicitly checks the channel.

Use it in a new situation

Give your partner a short instruction with one intentionally missing detail. The listener must ask a focused question before acting. Repeat with an unclear word, time and place. Compare which clarification phrase helps each time, and avoid treating a repeated sentence as an explanation when its meaning remains unknown.

For a second round, exchange roles and remove the script. Give your partner one new detail to react to. Afterward, each person summarises the agreed information or outcome. Compare those summaries: a grammatically accurate turn is not enough if the speakers leave with different understandings of the practical arrangement.

Questions

Do I have to memorise the whole dialogue?

Learn what each turn does, then change a practical detail. Being able to respond to a new question is more useful than reciting an unchanged script.

Can I use a different response?

Yes, when it performs the required action clearly and correctly. The answer key gives sample wording and does not replace feedback on every possible response.

Should I use du or Sie?

Follow the relationship in the model and in your actual situation. Keep the chosen form consistent; a new relationship may require changes across the sentence.

Are these official examination questions?

No. The dialogue and tasks are original fictional practice for identify what you did not understand and request useful clarification. They do not predict an examination card.
