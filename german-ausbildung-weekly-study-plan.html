German for Ausbildung: A Weekly Study Plan

Quick answer

Build your weekly German practice around an apprenticeship listing you genuinely want to apply to. Identify its main tasks and stated language requirement, then practise one of those tasks in German. Requirements vary by listing.

  • Practise clarifying an instruction

Turn one listing into three study blocks

Day 1: copy three verbs from the listing and write a sentence with each. Day 3: explain one task as though to a new colleague. Day 5: ask a partner to interrupt you with a question, then practise clarifying the instruction. Keep a written note on which part caused trouble.

Practise clarifying an instruction

Imagine a colleague says a delivery must be checked before it is placed in storage. Ask whether you should compare the quantity with the delivery note and tell the colleague what you will do afterward.

Check one possible answer

Soll ich zuerst die Menge mit dem Lieferschein vergleichen? Danach bringe ich die Lieferung ins Lager.

Change the example to another trade by swapping the task and its two steps. Keep your schedule if it works, but change next week’s practice after reviewing your weakest point.
