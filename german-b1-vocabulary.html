These groups support explanations, decisions and everyday problem solving. Some entries may already be familiar from earlier study; level labels do not place every common word in a sealed box. Notice that abstract nouns such as responsibility and support are often used in the singular in these meanings. A possible plural in a dictionary does not mean every sentence needs one.

Use B1 vocabulary to explain a point

A useful next step is to connect a claim with a reason, an example or a consequence. Do not insert an unfamiliar abstract noun only to make a sentence sound advanced. Choose a word that expresses the point accurately and combine it with language you can control. All passages below are original fictional practice, not official assessment samples.

Original Klarweg practice

Ich habe Erfahrung im Verkauf. Bei meiner letzten Arbeit habe ich oft mit Kunden gesprochen.

I have experience in sales. In my previous job I often spoke with customers. The second sentence supports the first with a concrete example. Change the field only if you can also give a relevant supporting detail.

Original Klarweg practice

Der Vorschlag hat einen Vorteil: Wir sparen Zeit. Ein Nachteil ist, dass wir früher aufstehen müssen.

The suggestion has an advantage: we save time. A disadvantage is that we have to get up earlier. The two nouns help compare a proposal; they do not by themselves explain which option you prefer.

Original Klarweg practice

Die Veranstaltung fällt aus, weil der Raum nicht frei ist. Wir suchen nach einer anderen Lösung.

The event is cancelled because the room is not available. We are looking for another solution. Separate what happened, why it happened and what you will do next before joining those ideas in your own writing.

Learn useful partners such as eine Entscheidung treffen (make a decision), Verantwortung übernehmen (take responsibility) and einen Vorschlag machen (make a suggestion). These combinations reduce the need to translate an English or Hindi verb word for word. Check clause order with the grammar syllabus when adding reasons and subordinate clauses.

Check context before choosing the Hindi meaning

die Folge means परिणाम (parinaam) in the problem-solving group. The same noun can mean an episode in another context. A one-word translation cannot settle every possible use. Record the meaning from the actual text you are reading and keep a separate example when you meet a different meaning.

die Ausbildung here refers to vocational training. It should not automatically replace every English use of education. Similarly, Bewerbung describes an application, while Vorstellungsgespräch names the interview. Grouping these words in a process helps you retain their differences instead of memorising an isolated list of English equivalents.

Hindi and Roman glosses support comprehension, but your German sentence still needs its own grammar. Do not decide German noun gender from the Hindi translation. To refresh earlier daily-life nouns, use the A1 starter vocabulary and the A2 vocabulary practice selections, then return to the more connected tasks here.

Practise retrieval, reasons and reformulation

Begin with a choice you actually understand, such as taking a morning or evening course. Write one advantage and one disadvantage for each. Explain which matters more to you and give a practical reason. A small, specific comparison is more useful than an elaborate opinion whose vocabulary you cannot explain afterwards.

Next, turn a complaint into a short problem-solving message. Describe the problem, give one relevant detail and ask for an achievable next step. Do not add invented threats, legal claims or promises to make the text sound formal. The purpose of this exercise is clear communication and an appropriate request.

Finally, close the table and explain one idea without using its listed noun. For “decision,” describe choosing between two options. Then reopen the table and rebuild the explanation using the noun and a natural verb partner. This checks whether you understand the concept rather than recognising a memorised translation. For exam-oriented writing, continue with the B1 Schreiben guide.

A manageable vocabulary review routine

Choose six entries for a study session. Read the forms and meanings, then close the page and retrieve them from a context cue. Write a sentence for two entries and read it aloud. Record the specific error: article, plural, meaning, spelling or sentence construction. Different errors need different follow-up work; rewriting the same list may not address the actual difficulty.

At the next session, test the earlier set before adding a new group. Put uncertain entries back into short situations, while allowing secure ones to appear in fresh reading or conversation. The number six is a manageable activity size, not a scientific requirement or a promise about how quickly you will reach a level.

Keep a small record with three columns: I can recognise it, I can retrieve it, and I can use it in a sentence. A word can be in the first column for a while before it belongs in the third. Ask a teacher or proficient reviewer to check new sentences, especially when the article or verb combination feels uncertain.

Questions

Is this the complete B1 word list?

No. This is a focused 24-noun practice selection. Open the linked official PDF for the provider’s broader reference, and use a structured course to develop the language around it.

Are these words exclusive to B1?

No. Common vocabulary is reused across learning stages. The label describes the intended practice context, not an exclusive classification for each word.

Should I learn the article and plural together?

Yes. Record the noun with its article and full plural, then practise the form needed in a real sentence. A singular dictionary entry alone does not show every grammatical change.

Does Roman text show German pronunciation?

No. It transliterates the Hindi meaning for readers who need that aid. Use the German spelling and reliable German audio for pronunciation.

Will memorising this table be enough to pass?

No. It is a small learning activity and cannot establish exam readiness. Use official practice and feedback across the language skills; no pass result is promised.
