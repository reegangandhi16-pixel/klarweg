German B2 Listening Practice: Compare Two Views

Quick answer

For B2 listening, track who says what and whether they are proposing, objecting or deciding. This original exercise separates a suggested policy, a concern and the final decision.

On this page

  • Listen for three roles in a discussion
  • Summarise in two German sentences

Listen for three roles in a discussion

Record the idea, the objection and the decision. Ask yourself which statement has already been agreed. Try to answer before reading the script.

If playback is unavailable, ask a partner to read the scripts aloud.

Check proposal and decision

Vorschlag: Wir könnten die Bibliothek abends länger öffnen. Einwand: Dafür brauchen wir mehr Personal. Beschluss: Wir testen die längeren Öffnungszeiten vier Wochen lang und prüfen anschließend die Nachfrage und den Personalaufwand.

The proposal is longer opening, the concern is staffing, and the decision is a four-week trial.

Summarise in two German sentences

Tell another learner what the group will do next and why it has not decided on a permanent policy yet.

Check one possible answer

Die Gruppe testet die neuen Öffnungszeiten vier Wochen lang. Über eine dauerhafte Änderung entscheidet sie erst, wenn die Nachfrage und der Personalaufwand ausgewertet sind.

Keep the four-week test separate from a permanent rollout. Then ask a partner to replace the staffing concern with a cost concern and update your summary.
