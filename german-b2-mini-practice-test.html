German B2 Reading and Writing Mini Test

Quick answer

This original mini test practises careful B2 reading and building an argument from evidence. Use it to check how well you can separate a finding, an objection and a recommendation.

Explore Klarweg B2

On this page

  • Read two views before taking a position
  • Write a recommendation and defend it aloud

Read two views before taking a position

Seit die Bibliothek länger öffnet, kommen abends mehr Studierende. Der Personalrat weist darauf hin, dass zusätzliche Schichten nötig sind.

What change was observed? What concern does the staff council raise?

Check the source details

Evening student visits increased. The staff council says additional shifts are needed. The text gives no cost or attendance figures, so keep those out of your conclusion.

Write a recommendation and defend it aloud

Recommend keeping the longer hours, limiting them to some days or returning to the old hours. Use the observation and address the staffing concern without adding invented figures.

Check one possible answer

Ich würde die längeren Öffnungszeiten zunächst auf zwei Abende begrenzen. Zwar nutzen mehr Studierende die Bibliothek am Abend, doch zusätzliche Schichten müssen eingeplant werden. Nach einem Monat sollten wir die Nachfrage und den Personalaufwand vergleichen.

Check that you used one source fact and addressed one constraint. Then answer a partner who asks why your proposal is better than returning to the old timetable.
