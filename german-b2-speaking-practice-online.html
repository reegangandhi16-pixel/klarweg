German B2 Speaking Practice: Explain a Trade-Off

Quick answer

Practise B2 speaking by explaining a choice, acknowledging a trade-off and answering a follow-up. Use this drill with a partner or record a response and answer your own follow-up question.

On this page

  • Plan a 90-second decision
  • Defend your choice without repeating your opening

Plan a 90-second decision

Your team can hold an event online or at the office. Online is accessible to more people. In-person allows easier informal conversation. Choose a format for a two-hour training session and explain what you would do about its main disadvantage.

Defend your choice without repeating your opening

Ask your partner to challenge the plan with this question: Was machen wir, wenn einige Teilnehmende zu Hause keinen ruhigen Arbeitsplatz haben? Answer by changing one practical detail.

Check one possible answer

Ich würde das Training online anbieten, weil mehr Kolleginnen und Kollegen teilnehmen können. Allerdings braucht man einen ruhigen Platz. Wir könnten deshalb für diejenigen, die zu Hause keinen geeigneten Arbeitsplatz haben, einen Raum im Büro reservieren.

Check that your final reply addresses the objection. Try the opposite position and keep the same criterion: can participants concentrate and contribute?
