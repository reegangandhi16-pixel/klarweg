German Certificate for Studying in Germany: What to Check

German-language admission requirements vary by university, degree programme, course language and applicant pathway. A certificate accepted by one institution or programme may not satisfy another.

Quick orientation: This page is a practical Klarweg guide. For current exam rules, admission conditions or official assessment criteria, use the linked primary source.

Understand the task

Before booking an exam, check the programme’s official admission page for: required CEFR level; accepted certificates; minimum score or module requirements; certificate age rules; and whether conditional admission or a preparatory course is possible.

How to prepare

Goethe-Zertifikat C1 and C2 are recognised by many institutions, but recognition is not universal across every programme. Use the university’s own list as the final authority and save a copy of the requirement page.

Common questions

Is this information an official guarantee?
No. It is guidance to help you prepare or research. The exam provider, employer, school or university publishes the binding requirements for your specific situation.

Where should I verify details?
Start with the official source below and check the current version before making a booking or application.
