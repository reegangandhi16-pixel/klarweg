German Classes for Working Professionals

Quick answer

If your work hours are predictable, reserve a fixed study block and compare available live options. If your roster changes, start with self-paced German and add a teacher session when you can commit to a time. Klarweg has self-paced A1–C2 and teacher-led options at A1–B2.

Try a 90-minute week around your job

Monday, 15 minutes to listen and repeat. Wednesday, 15 minutes to retrieve the phrases from memory. Friday, 30 minutes to work through a lesson. Weekend, 30 minutes to rehearse one workplace situation. If you miss Friday, use the weekend block for the core lesson and do the speaking task the following day.

Choose work German you will actually use

Start with one task this week, such as confirming a deadline. Say Kannst du die Liste bis Donnerstag prüfen? Then write a second line that says who will send the updated version. Next week, revise the same task with a different deadline and a formal Sie relationship.

Check teacher availability for speaking feedback.

Give your work practice a measurable outcome

In week one, record a 30-second update with the deadline and next action. In week two, repeat it with a changed deadline and answer one follow-up. Compare the two recordings for a clear owner, time and task. For meetings, try Ich schicke Ihnen die Liste bis Donnerstag. Können Sie sie bis Freitag prüfen?
