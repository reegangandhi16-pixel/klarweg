Comparing Options in German with Hindi

Use this guide to compare two choices using a criterion and a reason. Work through original German examples, Hindi meaning checks and exercises with explanations.

Understand this topic

A comparison needs an explicit criterion. A course can be cheaper but farther away; that does not make it better in every respect. Say which difference matters for your goal and why. The fictional exchange compares study arrangements without making a product recommendation. Learn the relationship between the options, rather than adding a list of comparative adjectives without a decision.

A conversation to understand and adapt

Original fictional dialogue

A: Welcher Kurs passt besser zu unserem Zeitplan?
B: Der Abendkurs beginnt später.
A: Dafür dauert die Fahrt länger.
B: Der Onlinekurs ist flexibler.
A: Aber zu Hause werde ich leicht abgelenkt.
B: Dann ist ein fester Lernort vielleicht wichtiger für dich.
A: Ja, ich würde deshalb den Abendkurs wählen.
B: Gut, dann prüfen wir die genauen Zeiten.

Read the meaning notes below before rehearsing with a partner.

Follow what each turn does
TurnMeaning
A: Welcher Kurs passt besser zu unserem Zeitplan?Which course fits our schedule better?
B: Der Abendkurs beginnt später.The evening course starts later.
A: Dafür dauert die Fahrt länger.On the other hand, the journey takes longer.
B: Der Onlinekurs ist flexibler.The online course is more flexible.
A: Aber zu Hause werde ich leicht abgelenkt.But I am easily distracted at home.
B: Dann ist ein fester Lernort vielleicht wichtiger für dich.Then perhaps a fixed study place matters more to you.
A: Ja, ich würde deshalb den Abendkurs wählen.Yes, I would therefore choose the evening course.
B: Gut, dann prüfen wir die genauen Zeiten.Good, then let us check the exact times.

Read one turn, hide its response and decide what information the speaker needs next. Then uncover the answer and compare its function with your own. A different answer can be appropriate if it is clear, relevant and grammatical. Do not treat this single model as the only permitted wording for the situation.

Try it yourself

Respond to the prompts without reading the dialogue. For an open response, aim to perform the requested action with information you understand. The key gives one possible wording, not an official scoring rule. Compare the response’s purpose as well as its grammar.

  1. Which option is described as more flexible?
  2. What matters to the final choice?
  3. Say “The journey takes longer.”

Answers and explanations

Show answers after your attempt
  1. Which option is described as more flexible?

    The online course. Flexibility is one criterion, not the final decision by itself.

  2. What matters to the final choice?

    A fixed study place. The speaker values this despite a longer journey.

  3. Say “The journey takes longer.”

    Die Fahrt dauert länger. Länger compares duration here.

Use it in a new situation

Compare two fictional study options using time, location and ability to concentrate. Give each option one advantage and one disadvantage. Then explain which criterion you prioritise. Change the priority in a second round and see whether your conclusion changes; a useful comparison makes the reason for the choice visible.

For a second round, exchange roles and remove the script. Give your partner one new detail to react to. Afterward, each person summarises the agreed information or outcome. Compare those summaries: a grammatically accurate turn is not enough if the speakers leave with different understandings of the practical arrangement.

Questions

Do I have to memorise the whole dialogue?

Learn what each turn does, then change a practical detail. Being able to respond to a new question is more useful than reciting an unchanged script.

Can I use a different response?

Yes, when it performs the required action clearly and correctly. The answer key gives sample wording and does not replace feedback on every possible response.

Should I use du or Sie?

Follow the relationship in the model and in your actual situation. Keep the chosen form consistent; a new relationship may require changes across the sentence.

Are these official examination questions?

No. The dialogue and tasks are original fictional practice for compare two choices using a criterion and a reason. They do not predict an examination card.
