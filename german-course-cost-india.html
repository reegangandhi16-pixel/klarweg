How much does it cost to learn German in India?

The honest answer has three parts: what you pay to learn, what you pay for the exam, and the hours you put in. This guide shows each part with real numbers where they exist, and explains which format is worth paying for at which stage.

Klarweg prices as on klarweg.in · Exam fees as listed by Goethe-Institut / Max Mueller Bhavan India · Last checked 5 November 2026

In 30 seconds

  • Learning: costs vary widely by format: self-paced online, live online classes, classroom institutes or a private tutor.
  • Exam: Goethe exam fees in India were listed at ₹9,400 for A1 and ₹18,800 for B1. For many learners the exams cost more than the course.
  • Time: the Goethe-Institut's rough guideline is 80–200 teaching units of 45 minutes for A1, and 350–650 for B1, counted from the beginning.
  • The cheapest path is the one you finish without repeating a level or an exam.

Learning formats compared

Prices for courses differ by city, provider and schedule, so instead of quoting other providers' fees, here is what each format gives you and where it falls short.

FormatStrengthsLimits
Self-paced online courseLowest cost per level; study at any hour; repeat explanations as often as you needNo live speaking partner; needs self-discipline
Live online group classFixed schedule and a teacher; speaking practice with classmatesPace set by the group; fixed times can clash with work
Classroom instituteStructured, social, often linked to an exam centreTravel time; only in larger cities; usually the highest cost per level
Private tutorFull attention, flexible focus, ideal for speaking and exam rehearsalCost per hour adds up quickly over a whole level

Many learners combine them: a structured self-paced course for grammar, vocabulary and listening, plus a few sessions of live speaking practice before the exam. That gives you the volume of practice cheaply and the human feedback where it matters most.

The time cost

Time is the cost people underestimate. The Goethe-Institut gives a rough guideline for the total guided instruction needed to complete each level, counted in 45-minute teaching units from the beginning:

LevelTeaching units (45 min)Roughly, in hours
A180–20060–150
A2200–350150–260
B1350–650260–490
B2600–800450–600
C1800–1,000600–750
C21,000+750+

At about ten hours a week, reaching B1 from zero takes roughly six months to a year. Studying a little every day beats long weekend sessions, because vocabulary needs repetition to stick.

What you can do for free

  • Klarweg's free grammar guides and full syllabus, and Chapter 1 of every level.
  • The Goethe-Institut's free official practice sets, linked in our A1 and B1 exam guides.
  • Free courses and audio from public broadcasters such as Deutsche Welle.

Free material can take you a long way. What usually costs learners more is not having a clear order to follow, and stopping halfway.

Questions

Is there a monthly fee for Klarweg?

No. Each level is a one-time payment for two years of access; Lifetime is one payment for every level, with no expiry.

Do I need to buy all levels at once?

No. You can start with one level and upgrade later. If you already know your goal is B1 or higher, Lifetime is usually the lower total.

Can I learn German for free?

You can get far with free resources. Most learners who need a certificate by a deadline benefit from a structured course, because it removes the guesswork about what to learn next.
