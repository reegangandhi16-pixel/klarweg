Official German exam practice papers

Official source directory · Last checked 8 November 2026

Start with your booked exam, not only the level

German exam practice papers help you understand a provider’s task format. First check the full name of the exam, level, adult or youth version, and any special purpose such as work or university admission. Goethe, telc and other qualifications are not interchangeable simply because a level label matches. Confirm acceptance with the organisation requesting your certificate.

This is a directory of official preparation resources, not a collection of leaked papers or predictions of future questions. It does not operate an exam portal, generate scores or reproduce copyrighted test questions. Use the provider’s current source page to find the appropriate paper, audio and instructions.

Goethe exam PDFs and practice material: A1–C2

The Goethe-Institut official exam-training hub organises practice by exam. Select your level there, then check whether you need adult or youth material. Keep each paper with its matching recording and answer key. Online accessible training may be useful if you prefer working on a screen.

telc: match the exact qualification

Start from telc’s official German examinations directory. Select the exact qualification and follow its preparation and practice links. Check the title on the paper against the exam you plan to take. Do not substitute a similarly named general, vocational or university-oriented qualification without checking the distinction.

Resource availability and purchase conditions vary. Look for materials explicitly offered free by the provider; do not assume that a product listed in a shop is a free download. Keep the version information visible in your study folder so a tutor can confirm you are practising the right format.

Use a sample paper in two different ways

Learning attempt: work through a section with pauses, consult the instructions and inspect the key afterwards. Identify why each wrong option was tempting. If language is the obstacle, review it with the grammar syllabus or your coursebook before attempting another paper.

Timed attempt: use a fresh paper and follow its own limits, audio pauses and permitted repetitions. Record your answers before checking the key. Reusing a familiar paper can improve your understanding, but its score may reflect remembered answers rather than readiness for a new test.

After either attempt, separate missing vocabulary, misunderstood instructions, lost concentration and answer-transfer errors. Those problems call for different practice. For writing and speaking, seek feedback from a qualified teacher; an answer key alone cannot give individual feedback on your performance.

Before treating a download as current

Check the provider’s source page, the exact exam title and any version or publication information. Older material may still support language practice while no longer representing the current task format. Avoid treating unofficial “previous-year paper” labels as evidence of authenticity.

For learners in India, booking arrangements and accepted certificates depend on the centre and receiving organisation. Read the Goethe India registration guide for orientation and confirm current instructions directly with your centre. No resource on this page guarantees a result or replaces those instructions.

Turn a resource into a study session

Choose one specific outcome before opening a file: recognise a sentence pattern, retrieve a set of words or complete one task without help. Read the instructions, attempt the work, then check the key. Record the cause of each error, not only the correct answer. On the next day, try the difficult point again before looking at your notes.

Keep one main learning sequence and use other materials to solve a named problem. If you change books whenever an exercise becomes difficult, you lose the chance to revisit and consolidate. Conversely, if most instructions and examples remain inaccessible, step back to an easier level rather than collecting more advanced files.
