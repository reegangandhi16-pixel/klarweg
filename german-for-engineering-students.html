German for Engineering Students: Lab Practice

Start with the German for reading measurements, naming deviations and assigning the next lab task. This page gives a measurement scenario, a model response and a second reading to try.

Review Klarweg’s B1 learning path

Start with the German you need in the lab

Make a glossary from a safe sample lab sheet. Give each entry an article, a verb and a sentence you can say, such as der Messwert – ablesen – Ich lese den Messwert ab. Start with units, changes, instrument labels and who will document the result. Keep course-specific safety instructions separate and use the lab’s actual instructions.

Report a deviation and agree on a handoff

Your fictional target is 20.0 mm and the reading is 20.4 mm. Tell your partner the target, measured value and difference. Ask them to repeat the measurement and write it in the log.

Check the numbers and wording

Der Sollwert liegt bei 20,0 Millimetern, der Messwert bei 20,4. Die Abweichung beträgt 0,4 Millimeter. Kannst du die Messung wiederholen und den Wert im Protokoll festhalten?

Say Sollwert and Messwert in separate clauses. Read back the unit and decimal. Replace the measurement with 19.8 mm and explain that it is 0.2 mm below the target.

Write a two-line lab update

Use Sollwert: 20,0 mm. Messwert: 19,8 mm. Then speak the result as Der Messwert liegt 0,2 Millimeter unter dem Sollwert. Ask your partner to read the value and unit back. End with one owner and action, such as Lea trägt den Wert ins Protokoll ein.
