German for Healthcare Reception: Three Role-Plays

Quick answer

At reception, practise checking a name, confirming the appointment time and giving one clear instruction. Use fictional information for these language drills.

Build a short check-in exchange

Your visitor is called Samira Khan and has an appointment at 14:30 with Dr Weber. Ask for the surname and check the time. Then explain that the waiting room is on the right.

Confirm what the visitor heard

Try the exchange once with the model and again with a new name and time. Ask the visitor to repeat where to go.

Check one possible answer

Wie lautet Ihr Nachname? Haben Sie um halb drei einen Termin bei Frau Dr. Weber? Das Wartezimmer ist gleich rechts.

Use Ihr with a formal address. Ask a partner to spell the fictional surname if you do not hear it clearly. This exercise covers reception language.
