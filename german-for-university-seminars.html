German for University: Seminar Discussion Practice

Quick answer

For German-language seminars, practise summarising a source, qualifying a claim and asking a clear follow-up. Use the exercise below to rehearse those moves. Confirm any language proof directly with your programme.

Separate an observation from its interpretation

A fictional study finds that students who attended an optional tutorial asked more questions in class. The study did not randomly assign students. Summarise what the study found and say what it cannot establish.

Challenge a claim respectfully

A classmate says the tutorial made students ask more questions. Respond by acknowledging the result and offering an alternative explanation.

Check one possible answer

Die Teilnehmenden stellten häufiger Fragen. Daraus folgt aber nicht unbedingt, dass das Tutorium die Ursache war. Möglicherweise haben sich besonders aktive Studierende eher dafür angemeldet.

Keep the observation and possible explanation in separate sentences. Ask what additional data would help distinguish the explanations.
