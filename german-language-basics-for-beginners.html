German Language Basics for Beginners

Quick answer

Start with three building blocks: a simple statement, a noun with its article and one polite question. Say each example out loud, then change one detail without looking at the answer.

Build a statement and a question

Start with Ich lerne Deutsch. Change the time in Heute lerne ich Deutsch. Notice that lerne is still the second element. Learn nouns as pairs, such as der Bahnhof and die Bibliothek, rather than learning only the noun.

Ask how to find a place

Say you live in Pune, ask where the library is and ask someone to repeat their answer slowly. Replace the library with the station and change its article.

Check one possible answer

Ich wohne in Pune. Wo ist die Bibliothek? Können Sie das bitte langsam wiederholen? Wo ist der Bahnhof?

Use Wo ist at the start of the location question. The article changes with the noun. Try once without looking, then listen to a related example in the free A1 chapter.
