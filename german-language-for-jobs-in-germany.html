German Language for Jobs in Germany

Quick answer

The German you need for a job depends on the role and employer. Read the advert for the exact wording, then ask which tasks depend on German and what proof is expected. This page gives sample phrases and an interview drill.

Translate the job advert into questions you can ask

Copy the employer’s exact language wording into one column and what it asks you to do into another. A preference for good German does not tell you which certificate an employer accepts. Ask about the actual language tasks on the job and whether formal proof is expected. For general context, see the federal guide to when German is needed.

Example advert wordingWhat to verify
Deutschkenntnisse erwünschtWhich tasks use German and how often?
Sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse erforderlichWhat does the employer regard as evidence?
Sprachnachweis erforderlichWhich credential and level, and by what date?

Try a job-specific interview response

The role includes customer contact. Give one example of handling a customer question, and ask whether calls or written support are more common.

Compare a model you can adapt

In meiner bisherigen Stelle habe ich Kundenanfragen per E-Mail beantwortet. In welcher Form findet der Kundenkontakt bei Ihnen hauptsächlich statt?

Use a truthful example from your experience and name the channel. Practise one spoken clarification and one written reply for the job you are targeting.

Write an employer question you can send

Try Welche Aufgaben erfordern regelmäßig Deutsch? Benötigen Sie einen bestimmten Sprachnachweis? If the answer mentions phone calls, practise taking a caller’s name, reason and callback time. If the answer mentions email, practise a written acknowledgement with one clear follow-up date.
