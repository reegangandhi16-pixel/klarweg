Request Information in German: Email Example

Use this guide to ask focused questions that a recipient can answer separately. Work through original German examples, Hindi meaning checks and exercises with explanations.

Original Klarweg learning material · 9 November 2026

Understand this topic

An information request is easier to answer when the topic appears early and questions are grouped logically. Ask for the detail you actually need instead of saying “send everything.” The fictional writer asks about a study group, its schedule and participation arrangements. This does not advertise a real group or provide course fees, availability or booking conditions.

An original worked writing example

Original fictional text · not an official paper

Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,
ich interessiere mich für Ihre Lerngruppe für Deutschlernende und hätte dazu einige Fragen. Ich möchte regelmäßig das Sprechen üben und suche ein Angebot am Abend.
An welchen Wochentagen trifft sich die Gruppe? Wie lange dauert ein Treffen? Außerdem würde ich gern wissen, ob neue Teilnehmer zuerst an einem einzelnen Treffen teilnehmen können.
Ich lerne zurzeit selbstständig und kann einfache Gespräche über meinen Alltag führen. Falls Sie weitere Informationen brauchen, schreiben Sie mir bitte, welche Angaben hilfreich sind.
Vielen Dank im Voraus für Ihre Antwort.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Mira Mehta

Mira states her interest and evening preference, then asks about meeting days, duration and whether a newcomer can attend one meeting first. She gives a modest description of her current ability and offers to provide relevant additional information.

Subject: Fragen zu Ihrer Lerngruppe

Why the message is organised this way

  • The purpose and timing preference help the recipient understand the enquiry.
  • Questions concern separate practical details and can be answered one by one.
  • The ability statement describes an activity rather than claiming an official level or certificate.
  • The final sentence asks what further information would help instead of sending unnecessary personal data.

The English note explains the whole message rather than giving a word-for-word substitute for every German expression. Before copying a phrase, identify the job it does: greeting, context, reason, question or closing. Remove details that do not belong to your own task. A fluent-looking sentence that gives the wrong information still leaves the reader’s question unanswered.

Try it yourself

Answer the content questions first, then create the requested language. Use the model only to check a point after your own attempt. For a new message, the names, relationships and practical details must fit the new situation; accuracy matters more than copying the model’s length.

  1. Which time of day does Mira prefer?
  2. Ask on which weekdays the group meets.
  3. Does Mira claim an official certified level?

Answers and explanations

Show answers after your attempt
  1. Which time of day does Mira prefer?

    Evening. Her preference appears in the opening paragraph.

  2. Ask on which weekdays the group meets.

    An welchen Wochentagen trifft sich die Gruppe? This asks for a schedule rather than duration.

  3. Does Mira claim an official certified level?

    No. She describes simple conversation ability instead.

Use it in a new situation

Write a new enquiry about a reading club or workshop. Choose three necessary questions and supply one relevant fact about your interest. Read the draft from the recipient’s perspective: can each question be answered directly, and have you avoided asking the same thing twice in different words?

For revision, check the purpose before checking small spelling details. Can the reader identify what happened and what response you want? Then check forms of address, verb position, article changes and the spelling of names or dates. Ask a proficient reviewer about uncertain wording; an answer key for the content questions is not a complete assessment of a fresh text.

Questions

Can I copy this email for every writing task?

Use its structure only when the purpose and relationship fit. Replace the information carefully and remove sentences that do not serve your actual message.

Is there a required word count here?

This page teaches a focused writing activity. It does not set an exam word limit; follow the official instructions of any examination you actually take.

Does the model guarantee a pass?

No. It is original learning material. A fresh text needs feedback on task completion, clarity and language; no examination result is promised.

How should I check my own draft?

First check whether it helps the recipient respond. Then review address forms, sentence order and spelling. Use the worked example to understand choices rather than copy unrelated details.
