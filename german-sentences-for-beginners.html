12 German Sentences for Beginners

Start with four lines you can change today: Ich heiße Asha. Ich wohne in Pune. Ich spreche Hindi. Ich lerne Deutsch. The table below gives 12 phrases with English meanings and Hindi cues.

12 sentences you can say today

Read one group aloud, cover the German and recall it from the meaning. Replace the city, item or time with something from your day.

Test yourself without looking

Ask for a station, ask a stranger to repeat their answer, then thank them. Say three lines without looking at the table.

Compare one possible answer

Wo ist der Bahnhof? Können Sie das wiederholen? Vielen Dank für Ihre Hilfe.

Sie suits an exchange with a stranger. Replace Bahnhof with Bibliothek and change der to die.

Use the same sentence pattern with your own details

Keep Ich wohne in and change only the city. Then turn it into a question for a classmate: Wo wohnst du? For a stranger, use Wo wohnen Sie? Finally, introduce yourself and ask one question without reading the table. The goal is to use the pattern, not recite the given name.
