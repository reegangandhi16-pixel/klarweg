C1 · Study and campus

State a limitation in a research abstract in German

Your fictional study interviewed volunteers at one campus. Write a sentence that limits what can be concluded.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Da ausschließlich Freiwillige an einem Standort befragt wurden, lassen sich die Ergebnisse nur eingeschränkt auf andere Hochschulen übertragen.

Since only volunteers at one location were surveyed, the findings can only be transferred to other universities to a limited extent.

A limitation specifies the sample and the affected claim. Nur eingeschränkt avoids overgeneralisation.

Try it with a change

Add a second limitation about self-reported behaviour without repeating the first limitation in different words.
