A2 · Appointments and services

Confirm when altered trousers will be ready in German

A tailor has shortened your trousers. Ask whether you can collect them Friday after 4 p.m.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Kann ich die Hose am Freitag nach sechzehn Uhr abholen?

Can I pick up the trousers on Friday after 4 p.m.?

German uses singular die Hose here. Nach sechzehn Uhr gives the earliest pickup time.

Try it with a change

You cannot collect the trousers yourself. Ask whether another person can collect them with your receipt.
