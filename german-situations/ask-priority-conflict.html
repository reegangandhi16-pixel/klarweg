Ask a manager which task takes priority in German

You have two tasks due today and time to finish only one. Share the impact and ask for a priority decision.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Wenn ich heute die Kundenanfrage bearbeite, kann ich die Auswertung erst morgen abschließen. Welche Aufgabe hat Vorrang?

If I handle the customer query today, I won’t finish the analysis until tomorrow. Which task takes priority?

Erst morgen communicates the schedule impact. Vorrang asks for an explicit decision.

Try it with a change

Your manager prioritises the request. Confirm what you will postpone and when you will send a progress update.
