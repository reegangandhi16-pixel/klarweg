A1 · Daily life

Ask for the last bread roll in German

The tray has one roll left. Ask whether you can have it and whether you can pay by card.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Kann ich das letzte Brötchen haben? Kann ich mit Karte bezahlen?

Can I have the last roll? Can I pay by card?

Das letzte Brötchen refers to the specific roll you can see. Use mit Karte for the payment method.

Try it with a change

Ask for the last pretzel instead. Notice how das letzte Brötchen changes to die letzte Brezel.
