B1 · Public transport

Find the bicycle carriage in German

You have a bicycle. Ask which part of the train has spaces and where to wait on the platform.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

In welchem Wagen gibt es Fahrradplätze? Wo soll ich auf dem Bahnsteig warten?

Which carriage has bicycle spaces? Where should I wait on the platform?

Fahrradplätze answers what you need. Auf dem Bahnsteig answers where to wait, so it takes the dative here.

Try it with a change

The bicycle spaces are full. Ask where bikes are allowed on the next train instead.
