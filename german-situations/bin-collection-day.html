A1 · Daily life

Ask when a bin is collected in German

Your new neighbour mentions bin collection. Ask which day the paper bin is emptied.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Wann wird die Papiertonne geleert? Am Dienstag oder am Mittwoch?

When is the paper bin emptied? On Tuesday or Wednesday?

Papiertonne means the bin for paper. The second question makes the choice easy to answer.

Try it with a change

You need the glass bin instead. Ask whether you can take glass there on a Sunday, then listen for the local rule.
