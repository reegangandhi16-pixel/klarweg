Ask about a reference-only book in German

A book label says Präsenzbestand. Ask whether you can take it home or only use it in the library.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Darf ich dieses Buch ausleihen, oder kann ich es nur hier lesen?

May I borrow this book, or can I only read it here?

Nur hier contrasts local use with borrowing. Read the label again before assuming it can leave the building.

Try it with a change

Ask whether you can photocopy a short section if the book cannot leave the reading room.
