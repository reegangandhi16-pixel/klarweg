A2 · Public transport

Find a replacement stop after a detour in German

Your bus no longer stops at the library because of roadworks. Ask where to board instead.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Wo kann ich stattdessen einsteigen? Hält der Bus am Rathaus?

Where can I board instead? Does the bus stop at the town hall?

Stattdessen refers to the alternative. Hält asks whether the bus stops at a named place.

Try it with a change

The town-hall stop is closed too. Ask if the replacement stop is within walking distance of the library.
