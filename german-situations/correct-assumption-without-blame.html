B2 · Precisely polite German

Correct an assumption about a handoff in German

A colleague says the file was never sent. You sent it Monday and can resend it. Correct the record calmly.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich hatte die Datei am Montag geschickt. Möglicherweise ist die Nachricht nicht angekommen. Ich leite sie Ihnen gern noch einmal weiter.

I sent the file on Monday. The message may not have arrived. I’m happy to forward it to you again.

Distinguish what you know from a possible explanation. Offer a remedy before debating blame.

Try it with a change

You discover that the link expired. Acknowledge that fact and send a fresh link instead of reopening the question of who was at fault.
