Correct a detail in meeting minutes in German

The minutes show Wednesday, but you agreed on Thursday. Ask for the correction and quote the correct deadline.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Im Protokoll steht Mittwoch. Wir hatten Donnerstag, den zwölften, vereinbart. Könnten Sie den Termin bitte korrigieren?

The minutes say Wednesday. We agreed on Thursday the twelfth. Could you correct the date, please?

Quote both old and new values. The article den introduces the date in this formulation.

Try it with a change

The date is correct but the owner is wrong. Write the corrected line and specify who should make the update.
