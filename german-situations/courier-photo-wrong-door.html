B2 · Customer service

Explain why a delivery photo does not match in German

A courier marked your parcel delivered, but the photo shows a red door and yours is blue. Provide the evidence and request an investigation.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Laut Sendungsverfolgung wurde das Paket zugestellt. Auf dem Foto ist jedoch eine rote Tür zu sehen, während meine Tür blau ist. Bitte prüfen Sie die Zustellung.

According to tracking, the parcel was delivered. The photo shows a red door, however, while my door is blue. Please investigate the delivery.

Separate the courier’s claim from the evidence. Während connects the two contrasting observations.

Try it with a change

The photo shows the correct door but a different floor number. Describe the discrepancy without claiming to know where the parcel is.
