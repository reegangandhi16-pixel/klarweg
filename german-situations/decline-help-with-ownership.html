Decline help you cannot commit to in German

A colleague asks you to take over a task. Explain that you cannot do it today and say when you could help.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Heute kann ich die Aufgabe leider nicht übernehmen. Morgen Vormittag könnte ich mir aber einen Teil davon ansehen.

I unfortunately cannot take over the task today. But tomorrow morning I could look at part of it.

Offer only what you can do. Einen Teil davon defines the boundary of your help.

Try it with a change

You cannot help tomorrow either. Refer the colleague to a specific resource and avoid implying someone else has agreed to take over.
