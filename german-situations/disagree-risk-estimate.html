C1 · Workplace

Challenge a risk estimate constructively in German

A team estimates low risk using last year’s data, but the current process changed. Acknowledge the evidence and propose a check.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Die Vorjahresdaten sind ein sinnvoller Ausgangspunkt. Da sich der Ablauf geändert hat, würde ich die Einschätzung anhand aktueller Stichproben überprüfen.

Last year’s data is a sensible starting point. Since the process has changed, I would verify the estimate using current samples.

Acknowledge what the evidence supports and state why it may not carry over. Offer a concrete verification step.

Try it with a change

The team can only review ten records. Explain what a small sample might catch and what uncertainty will remain.
