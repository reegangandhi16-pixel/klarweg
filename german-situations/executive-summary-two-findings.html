Summarise two findings for a busy reader in German

A fictional trial shortened response times but raised error rates. Write a summary that captures the trade-off and a recommendation.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Der Versuch verkürzte zwar die Bearbeitungszeit, erhöhte jedoch zugleich die Fehlerquote. Vor einer Einführung empfiehlt sich daher eine zweite Testphase mit zusätzlicher Qualitätskontrolle.

The trial shortened processing time, but also increased the error rate. A second testing phase with added quality control is therefore advisable before rollout.

Connect the recommendation to both findings. Avoid presenting faster processing as an unqualified success.

Try it with a change

The team has one week for a second trial. Propose one measure of speed and one measure of quality and connect them to the decision.
