A1 · Appointments and services

Explain how much hair to cut in German

You want two centimetres taken off, only at the ends. Say both details before the haircut starts.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Bitte nur die Spitzen schneiden, ungefähr zwei Zentimeter.

Please trim only the ends, about two centimetres.

Nur restricts the request to the ends. Ungefähr signals that two centimetres is approximate.

Try it with a change

Ask that the hair at the front stays long while the ends at the back are trimmed.
