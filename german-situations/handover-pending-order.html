B1 · Workplace

Hand over a pending customer order in German

A customer has paid, but the item will arrive tomorrow. Tell the next colleague what is done and what still needs action.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Die Kundin hat schon bezahlt. Die Ware kommt voraussichtlich morgen. Bitte ruf sie an, sobald die Lieferung eintrifft.

The customer has already paid. The goods are expected tomorrow. Please call her as soon as the delivery arrives.

Schon marks completed work, and sobald identifies when the colleague should act.

Try it with a change

The item arrives damaged. Hand over what changed and ask the colleague to contact the customer before pickup.
