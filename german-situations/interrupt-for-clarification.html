B1 · Precisely polite German

Interrupt a discussion to clarify a number in German

A colleague mentions either fifteen or fifty. Stop briefly to confirm the number, then let them continue.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Darf ich kurz nachfragen? Meinen Sie fünfzehn oder fünfzig? Danke, bitte sprechen Sie weiter.

May I quickly clarify? Do you mean fifteen or fifty? Thanks, please continue.

State both possibilities to make the question easy to answer. Kurz signals that the interruption will be brief.

Try it with a change

You heard the number correctly but not the unit. Confirm whether they meant euros or percent and repeat the full figure.
