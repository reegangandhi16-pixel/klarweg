B2 · Home and neighbours

Propose a fair laundry schedule in German

Two flats need the laundry room on Sunday. Acknowledge the competing schedules and suggest a recurring split.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich verstehe, dass Sie sonntags ebenfalls waschen müssen. Wie wäre es, wenn wir die Zeiten aufteilen und die Vormittage abwechselnd nutzen?

I understand that you also need to do laundry on Sundays. How about splitting the times and alternating the mornings?

Ebenfalls acknowledges the shared need. Abwechselnd defines how to rotate access.

Try it with a change

Your neighbour cannot alternate mornings. Offer alternating full weekends instead and explain what you would keep fixed.
