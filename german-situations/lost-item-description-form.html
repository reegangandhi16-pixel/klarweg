Describe a lost bag in a short form in German

A bag went missing on a bus. Write its colour, where you left it, and one identifying detail that would help staff.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Die Tasche ist dunkelgrün und hat einen gelben Anhänger. Ich habe sie vermutlich am Dienstag im Bus der Linie zwölf liegen lassen.

The bag is dark green and has a yellow tag. I probably left it on the route 12 bus on Tuesday.

Vermutlich marks information you are unsure of. Include a distinctive detail so staff can match the item.

Try it with a change

You are unsure whether you left the bag on the bus or at the stop. Update the form without presenting either place as certain.
