Write a short out-of-order sign in German

A meeting-room printer will not work. Tell colleagues to use the printer on the second floor.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Der Drucker ist außer Betrieb. Bitte benutzen Sie den Drucker im zweiten Stock.

The printer is out of order. Please use the printer on the second floor.

An effective sign names the broken item and the alternative. Im zweiten Stock gives the location.

Try it with a change

The alternative printer is on the same floor, behind reception. Rewrite the sign to use this location.
