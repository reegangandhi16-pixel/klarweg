Buy half a kilo of apples in German

You want half a kilo of apples and want to check the total before paying.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ein halbes Kilo Äpfel, bitte. Was macht das zusammen?

Half a kilo of apples, please. How much is that altogether?

Ein halbes Kilo gives the quantity. Zusammen asks for the total.

Try it with a change

You now want one kilo of pears and half a kilo of apples. Ask for both in one turn before requesting the total.
