B1 · Writing

Send a meeting recap with an action owner in German

One person will update a list on Monday and another checks it Tuesday. Write two lines specifying each responsibility.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Mina aktualisiert die Liste am Montag. Jonas prüft die Änderungen am Dienstag und meldet offene Fragen zurück.

Mina updates the list on Monday. Jonas checks the changes on Tuesday and reports any outstanding questions.

Each line names an owner, output and time. Avoid an ambiguous wir when responsibility matters.

Try it with a change

Jonas is away on Tuesday. Reassign the check and state whether the date changes.
