A2 · Writing

Text that you will be five minutes late in German

You are meeting a friend at the cinema. Say you will be five minutes late and ask them to wait at the entrance.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich komme etwa fünf Minuten später. Warte bitte am Eingang auf mich.

I’ll be about five minutes late. Please wait for me at the entrance.

Später is relative to the agreed time. Auf mich belongs with warten.

Try it with a change

Your friend is already at the entrance. Ask them to reserve your ticket and give them an updated arrival time.
