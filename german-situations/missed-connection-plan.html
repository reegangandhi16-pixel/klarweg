A2 · Public transport

Ask for the next connection in German

Your inbound train is late and you missed your connection to Kassel. Ask about the next available train.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich habe meinen Anschluss nach Kassel verpasst. Wann fährt der nächste Zug dorthin?

I missed my connection to Kassel. When does the next train there leave?

Dorthin refers back to Kassel. State your destination before asking for the next option.

Try it with a change

There is a bus as an alternative. Ask whether it goes directly to Kassel or whether you have to change again.
