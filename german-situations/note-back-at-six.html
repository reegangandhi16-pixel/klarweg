Leave a note saying when you will return in German

You stepped out to buy food and will be back at six. Leave a note for your flatmate.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich bin kurz einkaufen. Um sechs bin ich wieder da. Bis später!

I’ve popped out to do some shopping. I’ll be back at six. See you later!

The useful information is your return time. Um sechs comes first in the second sentence.

Try it with a change

Add that your keys are on the table. Keep the return time easy to spot when someone reads the note quickly.
