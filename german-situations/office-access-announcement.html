B1 · Reading and listening

Distinguish normal and temporary access in German

Read: Normalerweise benutzen Gäste den Haupteingang. Wegen Bauarbeiten gelangen sie bis Freitag über den Hof ins Gebäude. Where should guests go this week?

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Sie sollen bis Freitag über den Hof ins Gebäude gehen. Der Haupteingang ist der normale Weg für Gäste.

Until Friday they should enter through the courtyard. The main entrance is the usual route for guests.

Normalerweise introduces the default. Wegen Bauarbeiten explains the temporary change.

Try it with a change

Ask a partner to specify that deliveries still use the side door. Say which entrance applies to guests and which to deliveries.
