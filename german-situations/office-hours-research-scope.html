Narrow a research question in office hours in German

You have a broad question about transport. Propose narrowing it to weekday bus access in one district and ask if that scope is feasible.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich würde die Fragestellung auf die Busverbindungen an Werktagen in einem Stadtteil eingrenzen. Halten Sie diesen Umfang für realistisch?

I would narrow the research question to weekday bus connections in one district. Do you think that scope is realistic?

Eingrenzen names the change. Umfang refers to what you can realistically cover.

Try it with a change

The lecturer thinks your scope is still too wide. Specify a date range and explain what you would exclude.
