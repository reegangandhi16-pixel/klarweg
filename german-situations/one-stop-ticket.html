A1 · Public transport

Ask if a short-trip ticket covers one stop in German

You need to travel one stop and see a short-trip ticket. Ask whether it is valid for your journey.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich fahre nur eine Station. Gilt das Kurzstreckenticket für diese Fahrt?

I’m only going one stop. Is the short-trip ticket valid for this journey?

Gilt asks about the specific ticket’s validity. Rules vary by transport network, so listen for the local answer.

Try it with a change

Replace one stop with two and name your destination. Ask specifically whether changing trains is allowed with that ticket.
