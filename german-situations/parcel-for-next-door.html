A2 · Home and neighbours

Return a parcel left at your door in German

A parcel with your neighbour’s name was left at your door. Tell them where you found it and ask if it is theirs.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Dieses Paket lag vor meiner Tür. Es ist an Sie adressiert. Gehört es Ihnen?

This parcel was outside my door. It’s addressed to you. Is it yours?

Gehören takes the dative, so use Ihnen. Vor meiner Tür describes where it lay.

Try it with a change

Nobody answers next door. Write a one-sentence note saying you have the parcel and when they can collect it.
