Check where the queue begins in German

Two lines have formed. Ask where to wait for the checkout and whether this person is in that queue.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ist das die Schlange für die Kasse? Stehen Sie auch hier an?

Is this the line for the checkout? Are you in this line too?

Anstehen splits into stehen and an in the question. Kasse identifies the line you need.

Try it with a change

The person says they are waiting for returns, not the checkout. Ask which line is for paying.
