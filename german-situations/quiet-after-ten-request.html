A2 · Home and neighbours

Ask a neighbour to lower the volume in German

You can hear loud music late in the evening. Explain that you need to get up early and ask for quieter music.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich muss morgen früh aufstehen. Könnten Sie die Musik etwas leiser machen?

I have to get up early tomorrow. Could you turn the music down a little?

Give the reason briefly. Etwas leiser makes the requested change specific.

Try it with a change

You would rather agree on a quiet time for future evenings. Propose turning the music down after ten.
