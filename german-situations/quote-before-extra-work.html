B2 · Appointments and services

Ask for approval before extra repairs in German

You are leaving a bike for repair. Ask the shop to send a quote before carrying out any work beyond the agreed service.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Falls weitere Arbeiten nötig sind, schicken Sie mir bitte zuerst einen Kostenvoranschlag. Ich möchte die zusätzlichen Arbeiten vorab freigeben.

If more work is needed, please send me a quote first. I’d like to approve any additional work in advance.

Falls states the trigger. Vorab sets the time at which you want to approve it.

Try it with a change

The quote arrives with a range. Ask which repair is necessary now and which work you could postpone.
