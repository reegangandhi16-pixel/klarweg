B2 · Writing

Decline a professional invitation warmly in German

You cannot attend a panel because of another commitment. Thank the organiser and suggest a way to contribute in writing.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Vielen Dank für Ihre Einladung. Wegen eines anderen Termins kann ich leider nicht teilnehmen. Gern würde ich Ihnen vorab eine kurze schriftliche Einschätzung schicken.

Thank you for your invitation. I unfortunately cannot attend due to another commitment. I would be glad to send you a short written assessment beforehand.

A polite decline confirms the decision first. Vorab makes the timing of the alternative clear.

Try it with a change

You can attend the final half-hour by video. Ask whether it would still help to join for questions.
