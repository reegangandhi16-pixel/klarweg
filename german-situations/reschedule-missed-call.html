B1 · Appointments and services

Follow up after a missed appointment call in German

A service provider called while you were working. Explain why you could not answer and offer a callback window.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Ich konnte Ihren Anruf vorhin nicht annehmen. Sie erreichen mich heute zwischen fünfzehn und siebzehn Uhr.

I couldn’t take your call earlier. You can reach me today between 3 and 5 p.m.

Zwischen … und gives a clear window. Vorhin refers to an earlier time on the same day.

Try it with a change

The person reaches voicemail again. Leave your name, purpose and one more precise ten-minute window.
