C2 · Precisely polite German

Remove a charged implication from a report in German

Replace “Die Abteilung hat die Warnung ignoriert” when the only evidence is that there is no recorded response.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

In den vorliegenden Unterlagen ist keine Reaktion der Abteilung auf den Hinweis dokumentiert.

The available records do not document a response by the department to the notice.

Keine Reaktion dokumentiert describes the evidence. Ignoriert would imply intent beyond the evidence.

Try it with a change

Replace “Das Team weigerte sich zu antworten” when the records only show a reply arrived after the stated deadline.
