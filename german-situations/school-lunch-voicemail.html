A1 · Reading and listening

Pick out a lunch order from a voice message in German

Ask a partner to read this once, then answer without looking: Für Lea bitte Nudeln ohne Käse. Abholen um zwölf Uhr. Who is it for, what is excluded and when is pickup?

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Für Lea. Ohne Käse. Um zwölf Uhr.

For Lea. No cheese. At noon.

Listen for the short phrases after für, ohne and um. Repeat the three fields back before checking the transcript.

Try it with a change

Ask your partner to change the name, cut the pickup time by one hour and replace Käse with Soße. Repeat the three changed details.
