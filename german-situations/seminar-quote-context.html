Challenge a seminar interpretation politely in German

A classmate reads a quotation as a general claim. Point out that it describes one sample and ask if the conclusion should be narrower.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Die Aussage bezieht sich offenbar auf diese Stichprobe. Sollten wir die Schlussfolgerung deshalb vorsichtiger formulieren?

The statement apparently refers to this sample. Should we phrase the conclusion more cautiously for that reason?

Offenbar leaves room for correction. Stichprobe identifies the evidence you are discussing.

Try it with a change

The classmate finds a second study with similar results. Say what that supports and which claim still seems too broad.
