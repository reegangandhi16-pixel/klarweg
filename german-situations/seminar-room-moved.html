Find the replacement seminar room in German

Your room is locked and a notice says the seminar has moved. Ask which room it is in and whether the time changed.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

In welchem Raum findet das Seminar jetzt statt? Beginnt es weiterhin um zehn Uhr?

Which room is the seminar in now? Is it still starting at ten?

Stattfinden separates in the first question. Weiterhin checks whether the original time remains valid.
