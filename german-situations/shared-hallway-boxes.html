B1 · Home and neighbours

Negotiate space in a shared hallway in German

Boxes in a communal hallway make it hard to pass. Ask when they can be moved and suggest a temporary spot.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Die Kartons stehen so, dass man kaum vorbeikommt. Könnten wir sie bis morgen in den Abstellraum stellen?

The boxes are placed so that it is hard to get past. Could we put them in the storage room until tomorrow?

So, dass links the position to its consequence. Bis morgen sets a temporary limit.

Try it with a change

The storage room is locked. Ask the owner to move the largest box first so that people can pass.
