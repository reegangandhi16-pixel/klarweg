A2 · Customer service

Ask for another performance time in German

The evening show is sold out. Ask whether seats are available for the afternoon performance the next day.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Sind für die Vorstellung morgen Nachmittag noch Plätze frei?

Are there still seats for tomorrow afternoon’s performance?

Noch asks about remaining availability. Morgen Nachmittag identifies the alternative slot.

Try it with a change

There are two nonadjacent seats. Ask whether there are adjacent seats at a later performance.
