Agree in principle with one condition in German

You support a proposed earlier start, provided that childcare schedules are considered. State both parts clearly.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Grundsätzlich halte ich einen früheren Beginn für sinnvoll, sofern die Betreuungszeiten berücksichtigt werden.

In principle I think an earlier start makes sense, provided that childcare schedules are taken into account.

Grundsätzlich signals support. Sofern introduces a condition that must be met.

Try it with a change

Add a second condition about a three-month trial. Make clear what would be reviewed before the timetable becomes permanent.
