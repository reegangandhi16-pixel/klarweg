Thank a host for yesterday’s visit in German

Thank a family for inviting you and tell them which part of the visit you enjoyed.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Vielen Dank für die Einladung gestern. Besonders gut hat mir das gemeinsame Kochen gefallen.

Thank you for inviting me yesterday. I especially enjoyed cooking together.

Besonders gut tells the reader what stood out. The experience is the subject of hat … gefallen.

Try it with a change

Add one specific memory from the meal and say that you would like to invite the family next time.
