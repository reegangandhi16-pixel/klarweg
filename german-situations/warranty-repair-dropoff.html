B1 · Customer service

Explain an intermittent fault at drop-off in German

Your headphones only stop working after several minutes. Describe the pattern and say you brought the receipt.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Die Kopfhörer funktionieren zunächst, aber nach einigen Minuten fällt der Ton aus. Den Kaufbeleg habe ich dabei.

The headphones work at first, but after a few minutes the sound cuts out. I have the receipt with me.

Zunächst and nach einigen Minuten make the fault reproducible. Mention the receipt without claiming a specific warranty outcome.

Try it with a change

The fault now occurs only while walking. Name the changed condition and ask staff to write it on the intake form.
