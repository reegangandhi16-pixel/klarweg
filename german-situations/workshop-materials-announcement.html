A2 · Reading and listening

Listen for what to bring to a workshop in German

Have a partner read: Papier bekommen Sie vor Ort. Bringen Sie bitte eine Schere und Klebstoff mit. What do you bring, and what will be provided?

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Eine Schere und Klebstoff mitbringen. Papier gibt es vor Ort.

Bring scissors and glue. Paper is available on site.

Mitbringen introduces what learners bring. Vor Ort identifies what is supplied there.

Try it with a change

Change the announcement so that participants receive scissors instead and need to bring coloured paper. Listen for the change in what is provided.
