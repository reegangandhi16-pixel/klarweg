Goethe B1 Exam Preparation: Four-Week Plan

Quick answer

Use four weeks to diagnose your weakest B1 skills, practise targeted tasks and run a timed rehearsal. This plan includes an original speaking task. Official Goethe material is linked at the end for format and timing.

Start with a four-skill diagnostic

Read a short notice, listen to an announcement, write a reply and speak about the same topic. Record which task you could do without help and where you lost time. Goethe’s B1 exam assesses reading, listening, writing and speaking. Use the official model for actual timing and scoring.

Your four-week preparation plan

WeekWork to doCheck before moving on
1Try one task per skill. Keep a record of errors.Choose your two weakest task types.
2Practise how to read for evidence and listen for changed details.Explain which words supported each answer.
3Write a complete reply and record a speaking task with a partner.Check task coverage before grammar, then retry with new details.
4Run a timed official model and review the skills you struggled with.Schedule a specific retest instead of repeating only familiar tasks.

Try a short partner-speaking task

Plan a study meeting together. One person can only meet on Saturday and the other needs a quiet place. Agree on a time, place and what each person will bring.

Compare a model exchange

Wie wäre es mit Samstag um zehn in der Stadtbibliothek? Dort ist es ruhig. Ich bringe die Aufgaben mit. Kannst du deine Notizen mitbringen?

Ask your partner to accept or counter the suggestion. End by repeating the plan you both agreed on.
