The format

Four modules, every level.

From A1 to C2, every Goethe exam tests the same four skills. Klarweg builds all four into every single chapter — so you're preparing for the exam from day one, not cramming at the end.

Hören

Listening

Understand spoken German — announcements, conversations, interviews — at level-appropriate speed.

Lesen

Reading

Read and understand notices, emails, articles and texts, and find specific information.

Schreiben

Writing

Write messages, letters and structured texts appropriate to the level and situation.

Sprechen

Speaking

Introduce yourself, hold conversations, and present or argue a point with a partner or examiner.
