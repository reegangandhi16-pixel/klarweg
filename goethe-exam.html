Goethe-Zertifikat · A1 → C2 Outcomes, not promises

Built for the real exam.

The Goethe-Zertifikat is one of the most widely recognised proofs of German ability, used for visas, jobs, training and study in Germany. Klarweg's levels map directly to it, with the same four-module structure and honest, exam-board outcomes.

The format

Four skills at every level.

From A1 to C2, Goethe exams assess reading, listening, writing and speaking. At A1 and A2 these form written and oral parts; from B1 to C2 the four modules can be taken separately. Klarweg builds all four into every single chapter — so you're preparing for the exam from day one, not cramming at the end.

Hören

Listening

Understand spoken German — announcements, conversations, interviews — at level-appropriate speed.

Lesen

Reading

Read and understand notices, emails, articles and texts, and find specific information.

Schreiben

Writing

Write messages, letters and structured texts appropriate to the level and situation.

Sprechen

Speaking

Introduce yourself, hold conversations, and present or argue a point with a partner or examiner.

What each level certifies

Goethe's own descriptors.
