Goethe A1 Schreiben: form and email examples

Goethe A1 Schreiben has two tasks: complete missing information on a form and write a short message of about 30 words. This guide shows original worked examples and a way to check every requested point. The examples are practice material, not official answers or a promise of marks.

Checked against official adult practice material and exam regulations · Last checked 9 November 2026

Quick answer

  • Two tasks in 20 minutes: complete a form, then write a short message. Official duration →
  • The adult model set has five missing form details and a message of about 30 words covering three points. Official task instructions →
  • The worked examples below are original and fictional; the form is filled from its scenario, not from your own biography.

The two writing tasks

Goethe A1 adult writing format
PartTaskWhat to check
1Read the scenario and complete five missing form details.Use the supplied information and the correct field.
2Write a short message of about 30 words, covering three points.Content, suitable opening/closing and a clear message.

The official adult model set supplies the task pattern. A form and a message ask you to do different kinds of work: the first is precise information transfer, while the second requires you to express a short message for a reader. Do not spend the whole period perfecting a greeting while leaving the form unfinished.

The practice tasks here teach a checking method. They are not official papers, and the examples cannot establish what mark your own text would receive. Use the provider’s set for a complete rehearsal and a qualified reviewer for feedback on your German.

Teil 1: complete an original form

Read this fictional profile, then fill the five fields. The information belongs to the person in the scenario. It is not an invitation to enter your own name or contact details. Keep the original spellings and notice which question each field asks.

Original Klarweg form scenario · not an official exam item

Leena Kapoor wohnt in Bonn. Ihre Postleitzahl ist 53111. Sie möchte einen Kochkurs besuchen. Der Kurs beginnt am 14. Mai. Leena möchte am Abend lernen.

Leena Kapoor lives in Bonn. Her postcode is 53111. She wants to attend a cookery course beginning on 14 May and prefers studying in the evening.

Show the form answers and reasons
  • Familienname: Kapoor — surname, not the given name Leena.
  • Wohnort: Bonn — the city where she lives.
  • Postleitzahl: 53111 — copy the number accurately.
  • Kursbeginn: 14. Mai — the start date, not a date invented from your own calendar.
  • Tageszeit: am Abend — the preference stated in the scenario.

A practical method is to match field and evidence one at a time. Find the surname, complete that field, then move to the next label. After filling the form, reread the labels without looking at the profile. Does each answer have the right kind of information? A city in the postcode field is not repaired by neat handwriting.

In a second practice attempt, hide the answer key and change one fictional detail. Check whether you transfer the new information or repeat the old answer from memory. This tests the method rather than your ability to recognise a familiar page.

The form above is a static learning example. Nothing is submitted or saved. Use the answer document or interface specified in the actual examination; this page is not an electronic exam form.

Teil 2: cover every requested point

For the original task below, write to your friend Mira: invite her to cook with you on Saturday, give the meeting time and place, and ask her to bring fruit. Read all three requirements before drafting. A simple answer that supplies those details is a better rehearsal than a sophisticated paragraph that forgets the request.

Original Klarweg personal message · not an official exam item

Liebe Mira,
ich möchte am Samstag mit dir kochen. Hast du um fünf Uhr Zeit? Bitte bring etwas Obst mit. Wir treffen uns bei mir zu Hause.
Viele Grüße
Asha

Dear Mira, I would like to cook with you on Saturday. Are you free at five? Please bring some fruit. We will meet at my home. Best wishes, Asha.

The answer names Saturday, asks about five o’clock, gives the location and requests fruit. Those are content choices for this fictional task. Do not send exactly the same message when the prompt concerns a different activity, reader or time. Change the content first, then check the grammar of your new version.

The greeting and closing make the relationship clear. They do not replace the requested information. If the body merely says that you want to meet, the reader still does not know where, when or what to bring. Underline the words in your draft that answer each point.

A second original example: asking a course office

Write to a course office: say that you want an evening German course, ask when the next course starts and request the timetable. This changes the reader and the purpose, so the informal greeting from the first example is no longer the model to reuse.

Original Klarweg message to an office · not an official exam item

Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,
ich suche einen Deutschkurs am Abend. Wann beginnt der nächste Kurs? Bitte schicken Sie mir den Stundenplan.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Rohan Das

Dear Sir or Madam, I am looking for an evening German course. When does the next course begin? Please send me the timetable. Kind regards, Rohan Das.

The response remains short and direct. It states the purpose, asks a clear question and includes a request. It does not invent a reason for enrolling or add details that the office did not need. In your own attempt, use language you understand rather than a polished translation you cannot explain.

Use the 20 minutes as a complete task period

The official duration is 20 minutes; the division below is a Klarweg rehearsal suggestion, not a provider rule. Begin with a quick reading of both tasks so you know what remains. Complete the form carefully, draft the message and keep a short final check for unanswered fields or missing points.

One suggested home-practice allocation
StageSuggested minutesPurpose
Read and complete the form5Match each field with its source information.
Read message points and draft11Cover the three requirements in a coherent short text.
Check both tasks4Confirm fields, content and basic language.

Adjust the split after practice if it does not fit your needs. If you repeatedly lose time, note whether the difficulty is reading labels, selecting information, forming the request or trying to write far more than the task needs. The solution depends on the cause, not only on working faster.

Meaning checks for form fields and messages

Familienname means उपनाम (upnaam) in this form. Bitte schicken Sie mir den Stundenplan. means कृपया मुझे समय-सारणी भेजिए। (kripaya mujhe samay-saarani bhejiye). The Hindi gloss helps you check the meaning; its Roman text is not German pronunciation.

Use Hindi or English for brief planning notes if that helps, then form the German message with structures you know. Translating a long Hindi paragraph word by word often produces more material than this short task requires. Keep the intended reader and requested points visible while drafting.

A short revision checklist

  • Every form answer comes from the fictional source, not your own life.
  • The message covers all requested points and makes sense as a whole.
  • The greeting and closing fit the reader.
  • Names, dates and time details are consistent.
  • You can explain the meaning of every sentence you wrote.

For a specific construction, return to the word-order guide, negation guide or prepositions guide. Do not try to force all the grammar you have studied into one short email. The A1 overview places writing in the complete exam.

Questions

How long is A1 Schreiben?

The standard writing period is 20 minutes. Follow the current instructions for your booked format; the suggested home-practice split here is not an official rule.

How many words should the message contain?

The adult model set asks for about 30 words. Prioritise covering its points clearly rather than adding a much longer unrelated story.

Do I put my own information on the form?

Use the information requested by the actual task. In the scenario-based practice above, the fields belong to Leena, not to you.

Can I use one email for every question?

Reuse a structure only after checking the reader and required points. The content must change with the task.

Are these official model answers?
