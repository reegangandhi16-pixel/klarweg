Goethe A2 Schreiben: SMS and email examples

Goethe A2 Schreiben asks you to write two texts: a short message of 20–30 words and an email of 30–40 words, each addressing three points. This guide uses original models to explain how reader, purpose and content fit together. It is practice material, not an official answer bank.

Checked against official adult practice material and exam regulations · Last checked 9 November 2026

Quick answer

  • The adult model set asks for a 20–30-word short message and a 30–40-word email, each covering three points. Official instructions →

Tasks, lengths and the intended reader

Goethe A2 adult writing format
PartTextWord target in the model set
1A personal short message or SMS.20–30 words; cover three points.
2An email addressed to the specified reader.30–40 words; cover three points.

The official adult model set gives the task instructions. Short texts still need a purpose: the recipient should know why you wrote, what information you are supplying and what response you want. A memorised paragraph about yourself will not do that automatically.

The examples here use fictional situations and names. They are not taken from the official set. Their point is to show how a short message handles its particular requirements. Do not treat them as fixed answers to use regardless of the prompt.

Teil 1: a short personal message

Original task: you have found your friend Nisha’s key. Tell her that you found it, say where it is and explain when she can collect it. Ask whether the arrangement suits her. The extra question is useful here because the message proposes a collection time; it is not a fourth compulsory point in every official task.

Original Klarweg short message · not an official exam item

Hallo Nisha,
ich habe deinen Schlüssel gefunden. Er liegt bei mir zu Hause. Du kannst ihn heute nach sechs Uhr abholen. Passt dir das?
Liebe Grüße
Ravi

Hi Nisha, I found your key. It is at my home. You can collect it today after six. Does that suit you? Best wishes, Ravi.

The message moves from the reason for writing to the location and then the collection arrangement. A familiar object makes the pronoun reference easy to follow. In your own attempt, check that a pronoun still refers to the right person or thing after you change the scenario.

The text gives an actionable time. Saying only that Nisha can collect the key “later” might be too vague for this particular practice task. Read your own answer as the recipient: what would you still need to ask before acting on it?

A second original message: returning a book

Write to Sami: say that you finished his book, give a brief opinion and ask whether you should bring it to tomorrow’s course. Keep the reply short enough that the practical question remains clear.

Original Klarweg second short message · not an official exam item

Hallo Sami,
ich habe dein Buch fertig gelesen. Es hat mir gut gefallen. Soll ich es morgen zum Kurs mitbringen? Schreib mir bitte kurz.
Viele Grüße
Asha

Hi Sami, I finished reading your book and liked it. Should I bring it to the course tomorrow? Please send me a short reply. Best wishes, Asha.

Compare the two tasks. Both use an informal message to a friend, but the information is different. The key task needs a location and collection time; the book task needs completion, opinion and a return proposal. A greeting and a closing do not make two message bodies interchangeable.

For rehearsal, close the model and use only the three requirements. Then change one detail, such as the return day, and attempt it again. This makes the revision about expressing a new meaning rather than reproducing words in a familiar order.

Teil 2: an email for a specified reader

Original task: Frau Lenz has sent information about a computer course. Thank her, explain that you cannot attend on Monday and ask whether there is a Tuesday course. The fictional relationship is more formal than the message to a friend, so the greeting and forms of address change.

Original Klarweg email to a course organiser · not an official exam item

Sehr geehrte Frau Lenz,
vielen Dank für die Informationen zum Computerkurs. Ich möchte gern teilnehmen. Leider kann ich am Montag nicht kommen. Gibt es auch einen Kurs am Dienstag? Abends habe ich immer Zeit.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Anita Rao

Dear Ms Lenz, thank you for the computer-course information. I would like to attend but cannot come on Monday. Is there also a Tuesday course? I am always free in the evenings. Kind regards, Anita Rao.

The opening thanks the recipient, the next information explains the difficulty and the final question asks for an alternative. Notice that the writer does not pretend to have booked a place already. Keep the situation consistent with the prompt instead of adding an unsupported story.

A second original email: a room enquiry

Write to Herr Roth: say that you want a room for two nights in July, state that you will arrive on Friday and ask whether breakfast is included. This is a fictional language exercise, not a real booking request or advice about a hotel’s terms.

Original Klarweg room enquiry · not an official exam item

Sehr geehrter Herr Roth,
ich möchte im Juli ein Zimmer reservieren. Ich bleibe zwei Nächte und reise am Freitag an. Ist das Frühstück dabei? Bitte schicken Sie mir weitere Informationen. Ich reise mit meiner Schwester.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Nina Shah

Dear Mr Roth, I would like to reserve a room in July. I will stay two nights and arrive on Friday. Is breakfast included? Please send further information. I am travelling with my sister. Kind regards, Nina Shah.

The content points stay visible: month and length of stay, arrival day and the breakfast question. The request for more information supports the purpose without turning the text into a long list of unrelated questions. For a different prompt, change the actual information rather than keeping the model’s dates or details.

Do not measure quality only by length. A text in the target range can still leave out a point, and a longer text can create contradictions. After drafting, check that each requirement has an identifiable answer and that the whole message still fits its reader.

Choose the relationship before choosing the greeting

The informal examples address a friend; the email examples address a named organiser or contact. Keep the forms consistent within your text. A formal opening followed by an accidental switch to an informal personal form can confuse the relationship.

Useful original phrase fragments to study in context
GermanPurpose
vielen Dank für die InformationenThank the recipient for information.
Leider kann ich am Montag nicht kommen.Explain an attendance difficulty.
Gibt es auch einen Kurs am Dienstag?Ask about an alternative.
Schreib mir bitte kurz.Request a short reply from a friend.

These are learning fragments, not a requirement to include all four. Choose what the actual task needs. If a fragment does not fit, write something simpler that you understand instead of inserting it merely because it sounds formal.

Hindi meaning checks for the model phrases

Leider kann ich am Montag nicht kommen. means दुर्भाग्य से मैं सोमवार को नहीं आ सकता/सकती। (durbhagya se main somvaar ko nahin aa sakta/sakti). Schreib mir bitte kurz. means कृपया मुझे छोटा-सा जवाब लिखो। (kripaya mujhe chhota-sa javaab likho). The slash in the Hindi gloss shows gender alternatives; it is not part of the German sentence.

Use the Hindi meaning as a check on the message you intended. It does not mean you should draft a long Hindi text and translate every word. The short task is easier to control when you plan the reader, the required information and the question you want answered.

Plan, write and review both texts

The official writing period is 30 minutes. A practical home rehearsal can reserve time for reading both prompts and checking both answers; any split you choose is your study plan, not an official per-task countdown. Do not let an easy first message consume all the time needed for the email.

  1. Identify the reader and the three requested points before drafting.
  2. Use a greeting and closing suited to that reader.
  3. Underline where each content point appears in your answer.
  4. Check that names, days, objects and requests remain consistent.
  5. Review a specific language issue rather than rewriting the entire text without a plan.

Use the word-order guide, reasons and connectors guide or prepositions guide when the structure is the obstacle. Connectors are useful only when the relationship between the ideas calls for them. The A2 overview explains the surrounding examination.

Questions

How many texts are in A2 Schreiben?

The adult model set has two: a short personal message and an email. Each task specifies three points.

What are the word targets?

The model set asks for 20–30 words in the short message and 30–40 in the email. Follow the exact instructions on your own task.

Is every second email highly formal?

Read who the recipient is and the situation. The examples here practise named contacts with a polite greeting; do not choose a register without reading the prompt.

Can I reuse a model answer?

Reuse a structure after understanding it, but change the reader, details and purpose to match the actual task. Copying a memorised body can miss all the required information.

Do these examples guarantee a mark?

No. They are original learning models, not official assessments of your own writing. Use current provider material and obtain feedback on your actual response.
