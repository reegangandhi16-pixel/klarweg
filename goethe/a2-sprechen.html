Goethe A2 Sprechen: tasks, examples and partner practice

Goethe A2 Sprechen asks you to exchange personal questions, talk about your life and plan something with a partner. This guide shows a method for each task through original examples. Use them to practise adapting your response, not to predict the questions or memorise a guaranteed answer.

Checked against official adult practice material and exam regulations · Last checked 9 November 2026

Quick answer

  • Three speaking parts: exchange personal questions, speak about your life, and plan together.
  • Standard pair test: 15 minutes, with no preparation time. Official A2 administration rules →
  • Build answers that respond to a partner, rather than memorising a long speech.

The three speaking tasks

Goethe A2 adult speaking format
PartWhat you doRehearsal focus
1Ask and answer personal questions using keyword cards.Choose the question meaning and respond directly.
2Tell something about yourself and your life using prompts.Connect relevant points without reading a script.
3Plan an activity with a partner.Suggest, respond, adjust and agree.

The official adult model set provides this format. All practice content below is original and fictional. Its situations are not predictions of your actual cards. Standard timing comes from the regulations; your centre supplies instructions for your booked examination.

A2 is not simply a longer version of a memorised A1 introduction. In rehearsal, give yourself a task that requires listening to another person or connecting several relevant points. If you can only repeat one fixed paragraph, vary the prompt before assuming the skill is secure.

Teil 1: ask a question that fits the keyword

The official model set uses four keyword cards for each candidate’s questions. Read the keyword, decide which personal information it points to and form a question you understand. Listen to the answer before preparing your next sentence.

Original Klarweg personal-question exchange · not an official exam item

A: Wie kommen Sie zum Deutschkurs?
B: Meistens fahre ich mit dem Bus. Die Fahrt dauert zwanzig Minuten.
A: Was machen Sie nach dem Kurs?
B: Ich gehe nach Hause und koche das Abendessen.

A asks about travel to the course and activities afterwards. B answers each question and adds one relevant detail.

Original Klarweg second personal-question exchange · not an official exam item

A: Mit wem wohnen Sie zusammen?
B: Ich wohne mit meiner Schwester zusammen.
A: Was lesen Sie gern?
B: Ich lese gern kurze Geschichten. Am Wochenende habe ich mehr Zeit dafür.

A asks about living arrangements and reading. B keeps the response tied to the question rather than giving an unrelated introduction.

The extra detail in these answers is a learning choice, not a rule that every response must have a particular sentence count. Add information when it makes the answer useful. Avoid a long detour that leaves the original question unanswered.

After an attempt, ask your partner what information they heard. If it differs from what you intended, identify the cause: the question word, an unclear noun, a time expression or a sentence you did not fully control. The question-word guide is a useful reference for that kind of difficulty.

Teil 2: connect your points in a short talk

Treat the prompt as a set of information needs. Think about which point comes first, what example will make it concrete and how to finish without suddenly changing subject. Keep the language within structures you can explain. The fictional example below concerns a person’s usual week; it is not copied from an official topic card.

Original Klarweg short connected talk · not an official exam item

In meiner Woche ist der Deutschkurs wichtig. Ich lerne am Montag und am Donnerstag am Abend. Vor dem Kurs mache ich die Hausaufgaben. Am Wochenende übe ich mit meiner Schwester. Letzte Woche haben wir zusammen einen kurzen Film gesehen. Danach haben wir über die Geschichte gesprochen. In Zukunft möchte ich öfter ohne Untertitel hören.

The speaker describes a weekly study routine, gives a recent example and adds a future intention. The points are connected around one topic.

Notice the time changes: a usual routine, one past event and an intention. These are choices for this practice topic, not a claim that every official answer must contain a past-tense sentence or this exact structure. Existing advice can be useful without becoming a compulsory template for every task.

For a second attempt, replace the topic with something from your own life and make a new set of keywords. Ask a listener to summarise your main point. If they only remember disconnected details, work on the order before adding more vocabulary. Use the Perfekt reference when you choose to talk about a completed event.

Leave room to respond to a follow-up. A shorter answer that you understand can support a conversation; a polished script beyond your current language may be difficult to adapt. In rehearsal, invite one unexpected but relevant question after your talk.

Teil 3: reach an arrangement with your partner

Planning requires both people to contribute. Offer an option, consider the other person’s availability and settle the details that matter. Do not treat the exchange as two unrelated monologues. The following fictional dialogue practises planning a visit to the library.

Original Klarweg planning dialogue · not an official exam item

A: Wollen wir am Samstag zusammen in die Bibliothek gehen?
B: Am Vormittag arbeite ich. Passt dir der Nachmittag?
A: Ja. Treffen wir uns um drei Uhr vor dem Eingang?
B: Um drei habe ich noch einen Termin. Geht es auch um vier?
A: Vier Uhr passt gut. Möchtest du danach einen Kaffee trinken?
B: Gern. Aber zuerst möchte ich ein Buch zurückgeben.
A: Dann treffen wir uns am Samstag um vier vor der Bibliothek.
B: Gut, so machen wir es.

The partners suggest Saturday, adjust the time around a commitment and confirm the final arrangement. They react to one another instead of repeating the first proposal.

Review the decisions in the dialogue: the first time does not work, a later time is proposed, and the final turn confirms the changed arrangement. When practising, make one detail unavailable so that your partner has to listen and adapt. Merely repeating a prepared “yes” does not show whether the exchange was understood.

A disagreement can be brief and constructive. Der Nachmittag passt mir besser. gives a preference. Dann treffen wir uns um vier. confirms a decision. Use expressions that fit the actual detail you are discussing; do not add an unrelated phrase just because it sounds advanced.

Ask a listener to write the final arrangement. If the listener and the two speakers record different times or places, practise the confirmation stage again. The exercise is about communicating a shared plan, not collecting a predetermined number of impressive phrases.

Hindi checks for suggestions and agreement

Passt dir der Nachmittag? asks क्या दोपहर का समय तुम्हारे लिए ठीक है? (kya dopahar ka samay tumhare liye theek hai). Der Nachmittag passt mir besser. means दोपहर का समय मेरे लिए बेहतर है। (dopahar ka samay mere liye behtar hai). These glosses clarify the meaning; the Roman text is only Hindi transliteration.

Use the meaning checks to separate a suggestion, a preference and a final agreement. Then close the Hindi notes and attempt a new plan in German. Translate only where it helps you understand the task; a live conversation does not pause while you construct a complete Hindi sentence first.

Rehearse and review all three parts

Run three separate practice rounds, then join them in an official-format rehearsal using the provider’s material. After each round, note one strength and one specific problem. For a question round, check relevance; for a short talk, check the connection between points; for planning, check whether the partner’s information affected the final decision.

Use the word-order guide for a construction you could not control, rather than turning this page into another grammar syllabus. The A2 exam overview explains the complete exam. A qualified teacher can assess your own responses; this page cannot award an official result.

Questions

How many parts are in A2 Sprechen?

There are three: personal questions, a short talk about yourself and planning with a partner. Use the linked official model set for the complete format.

Is there preparation time?

The standard A2 administration rules provide no preparation time. Practise reacting to new prompts rather than relying on reading a prepared text.

Must I mention a past event in every talk?

Do not turn one model into a universal requirement. Follow the actual prompt; use a past event when it helps answer that prompt accurately.

Can I memorise the planning dialogue?

Learn what the turns do, then change the day, activity and availability. A real partner’s response may require a different suggestion.

Are these official topics and answers?

No. They are original Klarweg examples. They illustrate the task functions rather than predict cards. Official material is linked separately.
