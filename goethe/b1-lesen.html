Goethe B1 Lesen: format and reading strategy

Goethe B1 Lesen rewards reading for evidence. Learn a method for each part with original mini-texts and answer explanations, then apply it to official practice.

The parts at a glance

Goethe B1 Lesen rewards accurate decisions about meaning. You do not need to translate every word. You do need to notice who acts, what is asserted and which conditions apply. The table describes the adult practice format; always read the instructions on your own paper.

A method for each part

Teil 1: A personal account

First identify who is writing, when the events happened and what changed. Read each statement before returning to the relevant lines. Compare the whole claim with the text: the same noun can occur in both while the action is different. Treat a negative statement carefully; underline the verb and the words that limit it. Do not add facts from your own experience of the situation.

Original mini-text · strategy practice

Anfangs wollte ich jeden Abend allein Deutsch lernen. Nach zwei Wochen habe ich aber eine Lerngruppe gefunden. Wir treffen uns jetzt mittwochs online. Am Wochenende wiederhole ich die neuen Wörter allein. Seitdem spreche ich im Kurs häufiger, obwohl ich noch Fehler mache.

  1. Die Gruppe trifft sich jeden Abend. Richtig oder falsch?
  2. Am Wochenende lernt die Person auch allein. Richtig oder falsch?
Answer key and explanation
  1. Falsch. The group meets on Wednesdays, not every evening; the first plan was replaced.
  2. Richtig. The weekend sentence explicitly describes independent revision.

After checking the key, find the exact words that made the wrong answer tempting. Name the difference in English before rereading the German. This turns a small exercise into a reusable checking habit. A correct guess is still worth reviewing if you cannot explain the evidence.

Teil 2: Two press texts

Read the headline for orientation, then read the question without looking for an identical word. Locate a sentence that answers it and check the neighbouring sentence for a restriction. For each option, ask whether it changes the person, reason, time or result. A plausible statement is insufficient. It must answer this question using the information in this article. Leave a difficult item briefly and return after the other questions.

Original mini-text · strategy practice

Die Stadtbibliothek öffnet ab Montag eine Stunde früher. Viele Berufstätige hatten darum gebeten, weil sie vor der Arbeit Bücher abholen möchten. Die Schließzeit bleibt unverändert. Wer nur Bücher zurückgeben will, kann weiterhin den Kasten am Eingang benutzen.

  1. Warum öffnet die Bibliothek früher? a Wegen einer Feier. b Auf Wunsch von Berufstätigen. c Wegen neuer Mitarbeiter.
  2. Was bleibt gleich? a Die Schließzeit. b Die Öffnungszeit am Morgen. c Der Standort des Eingangs wird geändert.
Answer key and explanation
  1. 1: b. Working people requested the change so they could collect books before work.
  2. 2: a. The closing time is explicitly unchanged; no relocation is stated.

For a second attempt, cover the options and answer the question in your own words. Then compare that answer with each option. This prevents familiar vocabulary from making the decision for you. Keep the original meaning, even when an answer uses a different expression.

Teil 3: Matching needs to advertisements

Turn each situation into a short checklist: what is needed, on which day, at what time and under which condition? Read the whole advertisement before matching it. A shared topic is only the first filter. Evening availability cannot satisfy someone who is only free in the morning. In the official task, follow the reuse and no-match instructions precisely; do not force a match simply because an advertisement remains.

Original mini-text · strategy practice

A: Fahrradwerkstatt am Bahnhof. Reparaturen montags bis freitags, 8–17 Uhr. Keine Termine am Wochenende.
B: Reparaturkurs am Samstag, 10–12 Uhr. Sie lernen, einen Reifen selbst zu wechseln. Bitte bringen Sie Ihr Fahrrad mit.

  1. Mira möchte am Samstag lernen, ihr Fahrrad selbst zu reparieren. A oder B?
  2. Jonas möchte sein Fahrrad am Dienstag vor der Arbeit um 8 Uhr abgeben. A oder B?
Answer key and explanation
  1. 1: B. The course teaches self-repair on Saturday.
  2. 2: A. The workshop accepts repair work on Tuesday from 8 a.m.; the course is a different service.

Make a two-column note after practice: requirements on the left, supporting evidence on the right. If one requirement has no evidence, keep looking rather than assuming it. This is especially useful when several texts concern the same activity but differ in availability or conditions.

Teil 4: Recognising a position

Write the exact debate question in your own words. Decide whether each person supports that proposal, rather than whether the comment sounds generally positive. Concessions often introduce a real advantage before the writer rejects the plan. Read through the last sentence. Distinguish criticism of one detail from opposition to the whole idea. Underline the sentence that settles the position and use it to justify your choice.

Original mini-text · strategy practice

Lea: Ein autofreier Sonntag würde die Innenstadt ruhiger machen. Manche Geschäfte hätten vielleicht weniger Kunden, trotzdem sollten wir es ausprobieren.
Ben: Weniger Verkehr ist wichtig. Ein Verbot an Sonntagen überzeugt mich jedoch nicht. Bessere Busverbindungen wären sinnvoller.

  1. Ist Lea für einen autofreien Sonntag? Ja oder nein?
  2. Ist Ben für einen autofreien Sonntag? Ja oder nein?
Answer key and explanation
  1. 1: Ja. Lea acknowledges a drawback but still recommends trying the proposal.
  2. 2: Nein. Ben supports less traffic but rejects this particular ban.

Separate topic agreement from agreement with the proposal. Someone can approve of the goal yet reject the method. In your error log, quote the short phrase that determines the final position, then write what changed your first interpretation.

Teil 5: Rules and conditions

Scan for the section that governs the situation in the question. Circle obligation, permission, prohibition and exceptions. Read time limits literally. If a rule allows something only after registration, an option that removes registration is wrong. Your knowledge of similar rules in India or Germany cannot substitute for the printed rule. Before choosing, check that the option preserves every condition, including who is allowed to act.

Original mini-text · strategy practice

Im Lernraum dürfen Getränke nur in geschlossenen Flaschen mitgebracht werden. Essen ist nicht erlaubt. Gruppen ab vier Personen müssen den Raum vorher reservieren. Einzelne Besucher können ohne Anmeldung kommen, wenn ein Platz frei ist.

  1. Was ist erlaubt? a Ein offener Becher. b Eine geschlossene Wasserflasche. c Ein belegtes Brot.
  2. Eine Gruppe mit fünf Personen möchte kommen. Was muss sie tun? a Vorher reservieren. b Essen bestellen. c Nichts vorbereiten.
Answer key and explanation
  1. 1: b. Only closed bottles satisfy the drinks rule.
  2. 2: a. Five people meet the rule’s threshold of four or more.

For revision, change one condition in the question and decide whether the answer would change. This is a learning exercise, not a new exam item. It checks whether you understood the rule instead of simply remembering the answer letter.

Reading habits Hindi speakers can check

These are possible transfer difficulties, not assumptions about every Hindi speaker. Hindi often places the verb late; German main clauses usually place the finite verb second. Read the subject and finite verb first, then find any final infinitive or participle. Der Kurs findet trotz des Regens statt. means the course takes place despite the rain; the final separable element completes the action.

Track negation and quantity before deciding. Nicht alle Teilnehmer kommen aus Berlin. means not all participants come from Berlin, not that none do. सभी नहीं is a useful reminder of that limited scope. With kein, check the noun it negates; with nicht, identify the phrase being denied.

Connectors signal the direction of an argument. Es regnet, trotzdem gehen wir spazieren. expresses a contrast. Es regnet, deshalb bleiben wir zu Hause. gives a consequence. The Hindi contrast फिर भी and result इसलिए can help you check the relationship without translating the whole paragraph.

Do not let an English-looking word decide an answer. aktuell means current; eventuell means possibly. Read the surrounding sentence before attaching an English meaning. When an option contains a stronger claim such as “always”, “only” or “everyone”, return to the original limitation. A shared topic is not proof that two claims are equivalent.

Build a vocabulary notebook from errors rather than copying a whole article. Save the noun with its article and plural, the verb with its preposition, or a connector with a complete clause. Add a short English explanation of the relationship. Revisit a few entries the next day in a fresh sentence. Your aim is recognising meaning quickly in context, not accumulating an unmanageable list.

Official practice material

Use the Goethe-Institut adult practice PDF for a full-length reading rehearsal and its official answer key. The short exercises here isolate reasoning skills; they do not reproduce the length, difficulty or coverage of a full exam. Keep a fresh official set for a timed attempt instead of treating a remembered score as progress.

After a full paper, classify each error as vocabulary, sentence structure, overlooked condition, wrong speaker or rushed transfer. Choose one category to work on before your next timed attempt. If you understood the text but repeatedly chose a distractor, practise explaining why each wrong option is wrong. If too many sentences were inaccessible, spend more time on level-appropriate reading before repeating exam papers.

Questions

Must I understand every word?

No. Identify the claim and supporting evidence. An unfamiliar adjective may be irrelevant; an unfamiliar negative or condition can change the answer. Decide which kind of gap you have before spending time on it.

Should I read the questions or the text first?

Use the questions to establish what you need, then read enough context to avoid isolated-word matching. For sentence insertion, read the whole text first so that you understand its progression.

Does a course replace official practice?

No. Language study develops the skills; official practice familiarises you with the provider’s instructions and full-length format. Use both, and seek feedback on recurring comprehension errors.
