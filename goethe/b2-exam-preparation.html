GOETHE-ZERTIFIKAT B2 · EXAM GUIDE

Goethe B2 exam: pattern, marks, modules and study plan

Understand the four modules, then prepare each skill with a clear method. This overview connects the task format, language study and a realistic preparation plan.

Checked against Goethe-Institut regulations and official adult practice material · Last checked 8 November 2026

In 30 seconds

  • Four independent modules: Lesen 65 minutes, Hören about 40, Schreiben 75 and Sprechen about 15 in pairs, after 15 minutes of preparation.
  • Each module is scored out of 100 and passed separately with 60. Modules may be taken individually or together.
  • This guide is the overview. Use the linked module guides for task-by-task methods and original practice.

B2 represents independent use of German with more complex texts, detailed opinions and sustained interaction. Whether you need it for Ausbildung, nursing recognition, university study or work depends on the receiving organisation and route. Confirm the exact certificate requirement with that organisation rather than treating B2 as universally sufficient.

Exam pattern and marks

The written modules total approximately 180 minutes without breaks. Reading and listening each convert 30 item results to a score out of 100. Writing and speaking use assessment criteria. Your modules are judged independently: extra points in reading cannot compensate for a speaking result below the pass mark. For booking individual modules and repeat attempts, use the module and retake guide; for centre-specific arrangements, see fees and registration in India.

Lesen: follow the argument and its conditions

Five parts move through matching viewpoints, reconstructing a text, detailed reading, matching headings and locating regulations. Read for relationships as well as facts: a condition, exception or concession often separates the correct answer from a plausible distractor. Keep the full 65-minute limit in view and include answer transfer. The full B2 Lesen guide explains every part, with original mini-texts, answer explanations and a time plan.

Hören: track viewpoint, detail and speaker

Four parts cover short statements, an interview, a multi-person conversation and a lecture. Parts 1 and 3 are played once; parts 2 and 4 twice. Use the reading pauses to identify what changes between options. Do not give an interviewer credit for an opinion expressed by a guest. The full B2 Hören guide covers the playback pattern, speaker tracking and a routine for learners studying from India.

Work on the sentence patterns behind these tasks in B2 Chapter 1, free, or explore the B2 course roadmap.

Schreiben: complete, connected and appropriate

Two texts share 75 minutes: a forum contribution of about 150 words and a message of about 100. The official model suggests 50 and 25 minutes respectively. Each task supplies four content points. Cover them fully, choose the register for the reader and connect paragraphs so the argument develops. The B2 Schreiben guide explains the marking and provides structures, phrases and original model answers.

A dependable writing checklist

  • Underline the points the task asks you to include.
  • Use a suitable greeting and closing for the recipient.
  • Write one clear sentence for each point before adding extra detail.
  • Check verb position: Am Samstag besuche ich meine Freundin. — On Saturday, I’m visiting my friend.
  • Check articles, adjective endings you have learned, noun capitalisation, spelling and punctuation.
  • Leave time to reread the message and confirm that every point is answered.

Sprechen: a presentation and a discussion

After 15 minutes of preparation, the usual pair exam takes about 15 minutes. Part 1 is a short presentation, about four minutes, with follow-up questions. Part 2 is a discussion of about five minutes together. Prepare a clear line of argument rather than a memorised monologue. The B2 Sprechen guide explains both tasks and the assessment criteria.

Conversation moves to warm up

The everyday exchanges below are retained as a warm-up for asking, responding and agreeing. Planning a meeting is not a separate B2 exam task. Move from these short exchanges to B2 practice that compares alternatives, justifies a view and responds to objections.

Ask for information: use question words and polite requests to keep the conversation moving.

Wann beginnt der Film? — When does the film start?
Wie viel kostet die Fahrkarte? — How much does the ticket cost?
Können wir uns um sechs Uhr treffen? — Can we meet at six o’clock?

Talk about your everyday life: prepare flexible points about work or studies, family, free time, home, shopping, travel and routines. Avoid memorising one long speech that may not fit the question.

Plan something together: suggest an option, respond to your partner and reach an agreement.

Wollen wir am Samstag lernen? — Shall we study on Saturday?
Gern. Um wie viel Uhr? — Gladly. At what time?
Passt dir zehn Uhr? — Does ten o’clock work for you?

The grammar B2 really tests

Grammar matters because it makes meaning precise. In reading and listening, identify agency in a passive clause, a hypothetical condition and a reference back to an earlier idea. In writing and speaking, prefer a controlled sentence that says what you mean over an elaborate pattern used inaccurately. Review structures through complete messages, not isolated transformations.

  • Passive voice: recognise what happens when the agent is omitted.
  • Konjunktiv II: qualify suggestions and describe alternatives.
  • Verbs with prepositions: learn complete combinations.
  • Adjective endings: revise agreement in your own writing.
  • N-declension: recognise and use noun forms in context.

Study checks for Hindi-speaking learners

Avoid word-for-word translation. Heute fahre ich mit dem Bus. illustrates finite-verb position in a main clause; Hindi sentence order does not transfer automatically. Learn nouns with articles and plurals, for example der Termin — die Termine. These checks can help Hindi speakers, but individual difficulties vary and should be diagnosed from real errors.

At B2, also check the strength of a claim. Das könnte helfen. is a possibility, not a certainty. संभावना, निश्चितता नहीं is a useful note when an answer option makes the speaker more definite than the original. In reading, finish the sentence after a contrast marker; in listening, wait for a correction before selecting the first familiar detail.

Practise adapting an answer to the exact task. A memorised paragraph about your job may miss a question asking you to compare working arrangements. For every written or spoken response, underline the required points before planning examples. After feedback, keep a short error log separating language errors from missing content and timing problems. This tells you what to practise next.

Official practice material and vocabulary

Use the official B2 adult model test and the Goethe accessible online practice. Use the corresponding audio and key for that set. The official B2 exam information page provides the provider’s current information. Material here supplements those resources and does not reproduce their exam items.

Do not treat a B1 vocabulary list as a complete B2 syllabus. For B2, build a working word list from the texts and recordings you use: nouns with articles and plurals, verbs with their prepositions, and phrases for qualifying or contrasting claims. Record a short original sentence and its meaning. Revisit words in a new context; recognising a list entry alone does not show that you can understand it in speech or use it accurately.

A twelve-week plan after B1

This is a Klarweg study suggestion for someone with a solid B1 foundation, not a promise of reaching B2 in twelve weeks. Your starting skills, feedback and available hours determine the pace. Begin with a diagnostic task in each module. If ordinary B2 texts remain largely inaccessible, extend the language-building phase before booking an exam.

Suggested preparation sequence
WeeksFocusEvidence to collect
1–2Diagnose each module; identify weak structures and vocabulary.An error log with a reason for each difficulty.

5–6Write both task types; practise a structured talk.Feedback on content coverage, register and organisation.
7–8Discuss alternatives with a partner; practise each receptive task type.Answers justified with evidence, not remembered letters.

Keep the four skills connected. After reading a short article, summarise the author’s position aloud and write one paragraph agreeing or disagreeing. After listening, explain the speaker’s qualification in your own words. Reserve some official material for a fresh timed attempt. Repeating one familiar paper can teach the format, but its improved score may reflect memory rather than transferable comprehension.

A practical four-week final preparation plan

Use this shorter schedule only if your B2 language base is already established. It is the final format-and-feedback phase, not a replacement for the twelve-week sequence or earlier study.

Adjust this plan to your current level and available study time. Goethe-Institut provides free sample and practice material to help you become familiar with the exam task types.

Before exam day

  • Know each module’s format and time limit.
  • Rehearse with official sample material and matching audio.
  • Practise responding naturally to a partner.
  • Confirm arrival time, identification and current instructions with your exam centre.

Questions

How long does the exam take?

The written modules total about 180 minutes without breaks: 65 for reading, about 40 for listening and 75 for writing. The usual pair speaking exam is about 15 minutes, after 15 minutes of preparation. Your centre supplies the actual schedule.

Can I take modules separately?

Yes. Modules can be taken individually or together. Check your centre’s available dates and the receiving organisation’s certificate requirements.

Does completing this study plan guarantee a pass?

No. Use fresh practice, independent feedback and consistent task completion to assess progress. Adjust the plan when the underlying language or timing still needs work.

Where can I practise for free?

Start with the official Goethe resources linked above. Use the module guides to understand methods, then work on a complete official practice set.
