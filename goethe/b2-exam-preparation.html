Prepare for the Goethe B2 Exam: Format, Practice and Study Plan

The Goethe-Zertifikat B2 certifies advanced independent language use. You should be able to understand the main ideas of complex texts on concrete and abstract topics, interact with fluency and spontaneity, and express clear, detailed views on a range of subjects, including explaining advantages and disadvantages.

LESEN · READING65 minForum posts, articles, commentaries and regulations
HÖREN · LISTENING40 minInterviews, talks, conversations and radio
SCHREIBEN · WRITING75 minForum opinion and formal professional message
SPRECHEN · SPEAKING15 minPresentation and discussion with a partner

The written modules total approximately 180 minutes: Lesen 65, Hören about 40 and Schreiben 75. Speaking is usually a 15-minute pair exam, with 15 minutes of preparation. Modules can be taken individually or in combination.

Format and pass rules source check: 14 November 2026.

Official format: The module descriptions and durations above follow Goethe-Institut information. The study schedule below is a Klarweg preparation suggestion, not an official Goethe timetable.

1. What each section tests

SectionWhat you doPractise this
LesenRead forum posts, newspaper and magazine articles, commentaries and regulations.Identify main information, details, viewpoints, opinions and rules in varied texts.
HörenUnderstand interviews, talks, conversations and radio contributions.Understand key messages and important details in interviews, talks, conversations and radio.
SchreibenWrite a forum post expressing and justifying your opinion, plus a formal message in a professional context.Cover every task point, choose an appropriate greeting, use clear sentences and check word order.
SprechenGive a short presentation on a given topic, respond to your partner and exchange arguments in a discussion.Give complete answers, ask follow-up questions and make practical suggestions.

2. Speaking: a structured presentation and discussion

Use the official B2 model to rehearse the two speaking tasks. For a short presentation, introduce the topic, compare possible approaches, develop advantages and disadvantages, and state a reasoned preference. After a partner’s presentation, listen for the main position and ask a relevant follow-up question.

In the discussion, respond to the other person’s argument instead of taking turns reciting unrelated speeches. Practise giving a reason, acknowledging a limitation and proposing a conclusion.

Einerseits bietet diese Lösung mehr Flexibilität, andererseits ist die Organisation aufwendiger. — On the one hand, this solution offers more flexibility; on the other, organising it is more demanding.
Ich verstehe Ihren Einwand. Allerdings sollten wir auch die langfristigen Folgen berücksichtigen. — I understand your objection. However, we should also consider the long-term consequences.

These are original Klarweg language examples, not quotations from an official exam or predictions of future questions.

3. Writing: plan the two tasks separately

  • For the forum contribution, identify the issue, your position and every required content point. Support the argument with relevant reasons and examples.
  • For the formal message, establish who receives it, what situation you must address and what action or response you need.
  • Use paragraphs and clear references rather than inserting connectors without a logical relationship.
  • Check that a more complex sentence still says what you intended; complexity is not a substitute for task fulfilment.
  • Reserve time within the 75-minute module to check omissions, register, sentence structure, endings and spelling. Follow the current model’s task-specific length instructions.

4. Preparation mistakes to avoid

  • Practising only beginner arrangements instead of presentation and discussion skills.
  • Using a memorised essay that fails to address the actual task points.
  • Ignoring the partner’s contribution while preparing your next statement.
  • Counting correct grammar alone as proof that writing fulfils the task.

5. A practical 4-week preparation plan

WeekFocusPractice
1Build the baseDiagnose weaknesses with a current official model; read arguments and listen for viewpoints and qualifications.
2Train each modulePractise the two writing tasks, short presentations and responsive discussions alongside reading and listening.
3Timed practiceComplete module practice under time limits; review every error and rewrite weak answers.
4Exam rehearsalComplete official sample material, practise speaking with a partner and revise recurring mistakes.

Adjust this plan to your current level and available study time. Goethe-Institut provides free sample and practice material to help you become familiar with the exam task types.

6. Before exam day

  • Know the format and time limit of each module.
  • Practise with official sample papers—not only textbook exercises.
  • For speaking, practise responding naturally with another person.
  • Confirm the exam centre’s arrival time, identification requirements and current instructions.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Goethe B2 exam?
The written modules take approximately 180 minutes in total: Lesen 65 minutes, Hören about 40 minutes and Schreiben 75 minutes. Sprechen is about 15 minutes, with 15 minutes of preparation. Check your exam centre’s current instructions.

What German level does the exam represent?
It certifies language skills at B2 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR).

Where can I practise for free?
Use the official Goethe-Institut practice materials and sample papers linked below.

Do I need advanced grammar to pass?
The exam assesses independent, advanced communication: understanding complex texts, expressing detailed opinions and interacting fluently. Focus on clear structure, precise vocabulary and supporting your arguments.

Choose and plan your B2 examination

The Goethe-Zertifikat B2 uses four separately assessed modules. This guide concerns the general Goethe examination, not a telc professional-language or medical test.

Check the scoring framework

Each module requires 60/100. Do not use the telc B2 written/oral score split to estimate a Goethe B2 result.

Comparing with telc B2?

Goethe B2 writing has two tasks within 75 minutes; general telc B2 has a 30-minute writing subtest within its written examination. Switching provider requires retraining the task plan, not just changing the cover page.

Preparation checks

  • Plan a forum contribution and a formal message as distinct writing tasks.
  • Practise weighing alternatives in a presentation, then interacting in a discussion.
  • Review why a reading or listening distractor is wrong, not only the correct letter.
