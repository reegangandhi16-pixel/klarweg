Goethe C1 exam: the current format, task by task

The Goethe-Zertifikat C1 tests whether you can work with German the way universities and professional workplaces do: dense articles, expert interviews, a structured forum post, a polite complaint, a short talk and a real discussion. The format changed in 2024, and much material online still describes the old exam. This guide follows the current one.

Checked against the Goethe-Institut's C1 exam regulations (version of 1 September 2025) and its current C1 model test · Last checked 5 November 2026

In 30 seconds

  • Four modules: Lesen 65 min, Hören about 40 min, Schreiben 75 min, Sprechen about 20 min in pairs, after 20 minutes' preparation.
  • Each module is scored out of 100 and passed separately with 60 points. Modules can be taken together or individually.
  • Writing: a forum post of about 230 words and a semi-formal message of about 120 words, each covering four content points.
  • Speaking: a talk of about 5 minutes on a chosen topic, with questions, and a discussion of about 5 minutes with your partner.
  • Planning to study? Goethe C1 is not on the national list of university language proofs. Check first →

Exam pattern

Each module is marked out of 100 points and counts as passed with 60. A module you do not pass can be booked again on its own (module retakes). For fees and booking, see Goethe exams in India.

Lesen: reading (65 minutes)

PartSuggested timeTextTask
110 minA popular-science articleFill 8 word gaps (4 options each)
220 minA dense magazine article7 multiple-choice questions
320 minA commentary or reportPut 8 sentences back into the text
415 minShort contributions by expertsMatch 7 statements to the person who makes them; some match no one

Part 1 is a grammar and collocation test in disguise: the right word often depends on a preposition or a fixed phrase (in Anspruch nehmen, im Vordergrund stehen). Part 3 rewards reading for connectors and references: a sentence starting with Dennoch or Dieser Ansatz has to follow something it can contrast with or refer back to.

Hören: listening (about 40 minutes)

  • Part 1: a podcast reviewing several items (for example, books), heard once; match 6 statements to the right item.
  • Part 2: a radio interview with an expert, heard twice; 9 multiple-choice questions.
  • Part 3: a discussion between three people, heard once in four sections; 8 multiple-choice questions.
  • Part 4: a talk on a current topic, heard twice; 7 multiple-choice questions.

Parts 1 and 3 are heard only once, and at natural speed. Train with German podcasts and radio discussions, not slowed learner audio, and practise noting who said what.

Schreiben: writing (75 minutes)

Two texts. Each task lists four content points, and the marking checks how precisely you handle each one, how correct your German is, and how well your sentences and paragraphs connect.

PartSuggested timeTextLength
150 minA discussion post for an online forum: explain, argue with examples, consider the other side, propose an alternative≈ 230 words
225 minA semi-formal message, for example a complaint to a manager: show understanding, describe a problem, state conditions, propose a compromise≈ 120 words

Part 1: plan four paragraphs

Give each content point its own paragraph, and open with one or two sentences that frame the debate. A strong opening on the topic "Should every company offer working from home?":

Model opening

Kaum ein Thema wird in der Arbeitswelt derzeit so kontrovers diskutiert wie das Homeoffice. Während die einen darin vor allem mehr Flexibilität sehen, befürchten andere, dass Zusammenarbeit und Teamgeist darunter leiden. Meiner Ansicht nach lässt sich die Frage nicht pauschal beantworten, denn entscheidend ist, um welche Tätigkeit es geht.

Hardly any topic in the working world is debated as fiercely right now as working from home. While some see it mainly as more flexibility, others fear that cooperation and team spirit suffer. In my view the question cannot be answered in general terms, because what matters is the kind of work involved.

Useful C1 connectors for the paragraphs that follow: Hinzu kommt, dass … · Dies zeigt sich beispielsweise daran, dass … · Demgegenüber steht … · Nicht zuletzt … · Abschließend lässt sich festhalten, dass …

Part 2: polite, precise, four points

Example task

Your company plans to replace all in-person team meetings with video calls. Write to your manager, Herr Albers: show understanding for the decision, describe the problems it causes, say which conditions would be acceptable, and propose a compromise.

Model answer (about 120 words)

Sehr geehrter Herr Albers,
ich verstehe gut, dass die Firma Reisekosten sparen möchte und Videokonferenzen in vielen Fällen praktisch sind. Dennoch möchte ich auf einige Schwierigkeiten hinweisen. Gerade bei der Planung neuer Projekte fällt es in Online-Meetings deutlich schwerer, Ideen spontan zu diskutieren und Missverständnisse sofort zu klären. Zudem leidet der Kontakt zu neuen Kolleginnen und Kollegen, die das Team noch nicht persönlich kennen.
Für mich wären Videokonferenzen akzeptabel, wenn wir uns zumindest zu wichtigen Terminen weiterhin persönlich treffen könnten. Daher möchte ich folgenden Kompromiss vorschlagen: Die wöchentlichen Besprechungen finden online statt, während wir uns einmal im Monat zu einem gemeinsamen Arbeitstreffen im Büro sehen.
Über eine Rückmeldung würde ich mich sehr freuen.
Mit freundlichen Grüßen
Ananya Iyer

Each of the four points has its own sentence or two, in the given order, and the register stays polite without being stiff. That is what this task rewards.

Sprechen: speaking (about 20 minutes, in pairs)

You prepare alone for 20 minutes, with notes.

Part 1: a short talk (about 7 minutes per person, including questions)

You choose one of two topics and speak for about 5 minutes on four given points, for example: give an example, argue for or against, comment on how others should react, make a suggestion. Then your partner and an examiner ask questions. Structure is marked: announce your structure, signpost each point (Damit komme ich zu …), and finish with a short conclusion.

Part 2: a discussion (about 5 minutes together)

You and your partner discuss a controversial topic from a short text, again with four points, typically: give your opinion, justify it, refer to the situation in your home country or another country, and agree on something together. This is an interaction task. Respond to what your partner actually says:

Da muss ich Ihnen widersprechen, denn … · Das ist ein wichtiger Punkt, allerdings … · Könnten wir uns darauf einigen, dass …?

What makes German "C1"

The step from B2 to C1 is less about new grammar than about precision and range:

  • Nominal style where it fits formal writing: die Entscheidung für ein Studium rather than dass man sich entscheidet zu studieren.
  • Connectors beyond weil and aber: zumal, wohingegen, insofern, folglich.
  • Hedging and nuance: tendenziell, weitgehend, unter Umständen, es lässt sich nicht ausschließen, dass …
  • Collocations: eine Entscheidung treffen, Kritik üben, in Frage kommen, zur Verfügung stehen.
  • Register control: knowing when a forum post can be personal and when a message to a manager must be formal.

Preparation plan

The Goethe-Institut's rough guideline for completing C1 is about 800–1,000 teaching units of 45 minutes, counted from the beginning. If your B2 is solid, a focused final phase could look like this:

WeeksFocus
1–2Do the official model test untimed. Find your weakest module.
3–6Daily: one demanding article, one podcast or radio discussion. Collect collocations in context, not single words.
7–9One forum post and one formal message each week, corrected. One recorded 5-minute talk a week.
10–12Timed modules, discussion practice with a partner, and rework of your error list.

Official practice: the Goethe-Institut's current C1 model test (PDF) and its accessible online version. Older C1 practice sets with a 200-word essay describe the previous format; use them for reading and listening practice only.

Questions

Is Goethe C1 accepted by German universities?

Not automatically. The national framework names DSH, TestDaF, telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule and Goethe C2, among others, but not Goethe C1. Some universities accept it under their own rules. Read German certificates for studying in Germany before booking.

Can I go straight from B1 to C1?

Not realistically. B2 is where you build the argumentation, passive forms and formal register that C1 assumes. See the B2 exam guide.

How is C1 different from C2?

C2 expects near-native precision and adds tasks that C1 does not have. See the C2 exam guide.
