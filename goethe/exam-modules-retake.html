Can you retake one Goethe exam module? The rules, level by level

Failing one part of a Goethe exam does not always mean starting again. From B1 upwards, every module stands on its own. At A1 and A2 the rules are stricter. Here is exactly what the Goethe-Institut's current rules say about retakes, module certificates and combining results, and what to ask your exam centre.

Based on the Goethe-Institut's Exam Rules (Prüfungsordnung, §14 and §15) and the exam regulations for each level, all in the version of 1 September 2025 · Last checked 5 November 2026

In 30 seconds

  • B1, B2, C1, C2: modular. Each module is passed with 60 of 100 points and can be retaken on its own, as often as you like, subject to the exam centre's capacity.
  • You get a certificate for each module you pass, and new module certificates can be combined with earlier ones.
  • A1: the exam can only be retaken as a whole.
  • A2: normally retaken as a whole; in exceptional cases a centre may allow a resit of only the speaking part or only the written part, within one year.

The rules by level

ExamStructureIf you fail
A1: Start Deutsch 1One exam: written part + speakingRetake the whole exam.
A2One exam: written part + speakingNormally retake the whole exam. Exceptionally, if the centre can organise it: resit either the speaking part or the whole written part, within one year, at the same centre. Not guaranteed; a processing fee is possible.
B1, B2, C1, C2Four independent modules: Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, SprechenRetake only the module(s) you did not pass, as often as the centre's capacity allows.

At B1–C2, a module is passed with 60 points (60%), and the result of one module never affects another. A strong reading score cannot make up for a weak speaking score.

Module certificates and the overall certificate

For the modular exams (B1–C2), the Goethe-Institut's Exam Rules set out how results are certified:

  1. You receive a certificate for each module you pass.
  2. If you take and pass all four modules at one venue in one exam session, you can instead receive an overall certificate showing all four.
  3. If you retake a module later, its certificate can be combined with your earlier module certificates.
  4. If the centre's capacity allows, you can request an overall certificate showing your best results achieved in the four modules within one year. Rules on where those modules must have been taken apply (see below). The centre may charge an administration fee of up to 25% of the exam fee for this.

Certificates are issued on paper or digitally, depending on the centre, and their validity can be checked at goethe.de/verify.

What this means in India

India is outside the EU, so for a cross-institute overall certificate the rule is: all four modules passed within one year at Goethe-Instituts in the same country, issued by an institute where you passed at least one module. In India the Goethe-Institut operates as Max Mueller Bhavan. At Goethe-Zentren and exam partners, an overall certificate requires all four modules passed within one year at the same exam centre.

In practice: if you plan to combine modules, take them all at Max Mueller Bhavan institutes, or all at the same Goethe-Zentrum, within twelve months. Confirm with the centre before you book. Fees and booking: Goethe exams in India.

Retake strategy

  • Read your result sheet. It shows points per module. A 55 in one module needs targeted practice, not a full restart.
  • Fix the cause, then rebook. Writing and speaking fail most often on task completion: a missed content point, or a presentation that skipped a slide. The module guides explain the criteria: B1 Schreiben, B1 Sprechen, B2 Schreiben, B2 Sprechen.
  • Mind the clock if you need an overall certificate. All four modules have to be passed within one year; a late retake can push you outside that window.
  • Ask who will read the certificate. Some employers or authorities accept separate module certificates; others want a single overall certificate. Check before you choose your route.

Questions

Is there a waiting period before a retake?

The Exam Rules let each exam centre set time periods for resit dates, and say these are given when your result is communicated. There is no right to a specific date.

Does the paper or digital format change the rules?

No. The modular rules are the same; see paper-based vs digital exams for the format differences.

Do Goethe certificates expire?

The Exam Rules contain no expiry date for certificates. Some institutions and authorities, however, ask for a recent certificate, so check their requirements.
