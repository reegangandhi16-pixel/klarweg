Goethe Exam: Paper-Based vs Digital — What Changes?

Digital Goethe exams are held in selected exam centres on Goethe-Institut laptops. Reading, writing and listening are completed on the laptop; speaking remains in person. Availability varies by exam and location.

Understand the task

Paper format: you read and write on printed materials. Digital format: use the centre’s laptop and German keyboard; listening uses headphones. Both are supervised on site. Choose based on availability, typing comfort, screen reading and handwriting preference.

How to prepare

Before booking, check whether your exact level, date and centre offer digital delivery. Practise typing German characters (ä, ö, ü, ß), navigating on-screen tasks and managing time. Ask the centre about permitted equipment and instructions.
