How Long Does It Take to Learn German A1?

Quick answer

An illustrative 60-hour study plan takes 6 weeks at 10 hours a week, 10 weeks at 6 hours, or 15 weeks at 4 hours. Your calendar depends on what you already know, how much you practise and how independently you can use all four skills.

Estimate from actual study hours

There is no single duration for A1. A useful estimate is your planned study hours divided by the hours you can really do each week. Include listening, speaking and review time alongside lessons. If you miss sessions frequently, use your recent average rather than an ideal calendar.

Sixty hours is a planning example, not a universal requirement or an exam-readiness estimate.

Run a four-skill checkpoint

  1. Listen for a meeting time and place without reading the transcript first.
  2. Read a short invitation and identify what reply is requested.
  3. Say your name, city and one language, then ask the listener a question.
  4. Write a short message to arrange a meeting with a day, time and place.

If you can finish most exercises only with notes, add more retrieval practice before setting an exam date.

Recalculate after two weeks

If you scheduled six hours a week and averaged four, re-estimate with four. Check which skill received the least time and give it the first slot in your next week.

Turn a six-hour week into four kinds of practice

Reserve two hours for new lessons, one hour for listening, one hour for speaking, one hour for reading and writing, and one hour to review mistakes. At the end of the week, write a three-line invitation and say it without looking. If you can read the words but cannot produce them, move part of next week's lesson time into speaking recall.
