The Klarweg method · 01

Every word has a role. Every role has a color.

The four roles below carry every German sentence you'll ever read. Once you see them, word order stops being a puzzle.

Subject

कौन कर रहा है

The one performing the action. Always in Nominativ. The anchor of every sentence.

Der Lehrer erklärt die Grammatik.
Verb

क्या हो रहा है

The action or state. Locked to position 2 in main clauses. The engine of the sentence.

Anna lernt Deutsch.
Object

किसको / क्या

What or who receives the action. Akkusativ for direct, Dativ for indirect — the color tells you which.

Sie gibt mir das Buch.
Time

कब

When something happens. Lives in its own slot in the TeKaMoLo position rule.

Heute ist Montag.
Place

कहाँ

Where something happens — or where it's going. A different concept, a different color.

Wir fahren nach Berlin.
