    Good to know

    Clear terms before you pay.

    Questions? Contact us. Live classes are separate from Klarweg's self-paced courses; see our Terms and Refund Policy.

    What does the course fee include?

    The course fee covers every live class in the course, the teacher’s class notes and worksheets, and written feedback on your homework. Goethe exam fees are not included.

    How do the coupon codes work?

    Use GROUP3000 on a Group course (₹3,000 off) or PERSONAL7000 on a One-to-One course (₹7,000 off). One coupon per course. A coupon cannot be combined with the ₹100 demo fee adjustment; Klarweg's server checks the amount before you pay.

    When do courses start?

    Start and end dates are announced per batch. Until a batch has dates, a schedule and confirmed enrolment conditions, it shows “To be announced” and no payment is taken. Group batches run on a fixed timetable in Indian Standard Time, shown on the course before you enrol. If a class has to move, we tell you at least 24 hours in advance and schedule a replacement class. One-to-one lessons are scheduled directly with your teacher.

    What if I miss a class?

    Group classes are not recorded, and a missed group class is not refunded or repeated individually. Your teacher shares the class notes and homework so you can catch up, and you can bring questions to the next class. One-to-one lessons can be moved free of charge with at least 24 hours’ notice.

    Cancellations and refunds

    If you cancel at least 48 hours before the first class, your course fee is refunded in full. If you cancel during the first two weeks of the course, we refund the classes still to come; after that, course fees are not refunded. If Klarweg cancels or postpones a course and the new dates don’t suit you, we refund everything not yet delivered. The 7-day guarantee in our Refund Policy covers self-paced purchases only, not live classes.

    Can I move my demo?

    Yes, once, free of charge, up to 12 hours before it starts, from this page or My Account. If the teacher has to cancel, you choose a full refund or a new time.
