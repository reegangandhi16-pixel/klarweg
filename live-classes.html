How live classes work

From first conversation to fluent progress.

    See the level, schedule, materials and fee before you commit to anything.

  5. Practise and progress

    Speak, get corrected and practise again, moving through the course one level at a time.

Method

What happens in a live class.

Self-paced study is ideal for steady input on your own time. A live teacher adds what is hard to do alone: real conversation, immediate correction and someone who notices what you keep getting wrong.

01

Grammar, systematically

Each structure explained step by step, with the reasons behind it.

weil ich Deutsch lerne

02

Guided speaking

Build sentences out loud with prompts, then on your own.

Ich möchte einen Termin vereinbaren.

03

Vocabulary

Words learned in context, with gender and plural, and used again in later sessions.

der Termin, die Termine

04

Listening & pronunciation

Hear it, say it, adjust: umlauts, word stress and sentence melody.

schön · Müller · Straße

05

Feedback & correction

Mistakes caught and explained while the sentence is still fresh.

Ich bin gegangen – nicht: Ich habe gegangen.

06

Goethe preparation

Where relevant: exam format, timing and task types, practised module by module.

Lesen · Hören · Schreiben · Sprechen

07

Structured progress through CEFR levels

Each course has a defined level and objectives, so you always know what you are working towards and what comes next.
