ÖSD A1 Exam Preparation: Practice and Checklist

ÖSD A1 has a written module covering reading, listening and writing and an oral module covering speaking. Use the drills on this page to practise each skill, then use ÖSD’s official material when you want to rehearse its exact format.

Build your A1 foundation

Start with the official ÖSD A1 model

ÖSD Zertifikat A1 has a written module covering reading, listening and writing, and an oral module covering speaking. Download the current model set and audio from the official ÖSD A1 page. Use the instructions and answer material for the version you are taking. Ask your centre what it offers and confirm what your receiving institution accepts if the certificate is for an application.

  1. Complete one written task without help. Note which details you missed, such as a date or a negative word.
  2. Use the official speaking example to see how the tasks are framed.
  3. Repeat your weakest task with different names, times or places.

Try a picture description and a short role-play

Imagine a picture of two people at a bakery. One points to bread and the other stands behind the counter. Describe where they are and what each person does. Then role-play buying bread and asking the price. This is original language practice.

Check a short A1 model

Die Personen sind in einer Bäckerei. Eine Frau zeigt auf das Brot. Der Verkäufer steht hinter der Theke.

Ein Brot, bitte. Was kostet das?

Describe what you can see first. In the role-play, state what you want and ask one clear question. Try again with a different shop and item.

Practise the four skills with one bakery visit

Read Heute schließen wir um 14 Uhr: you need to come before 2 p.m. Ask a partner to read Das Brot kostet drei Euro. Write down the price. Write a message to a friend saying you will meet at the bakery at eleven: Wir treffen uns um elf vor der Bäckerei. Then say your order and ask how much it costs. Change the price and time and repeat. The exercise is original and has no official score.
