Ask for the number of lessons, their length and the expected end date before you pay, and divide the fee by the hours to compare offers. Cancellation: a full refund if you cancel at least 48 hours before the first class; during the first two weeks, a refund for the classes still to come. Full terms are on the live classes page.

When one-to-one is worth the extra cost

  • Worth it: you have one specific problem a group cannot fix, such as freezing in speaking or a Goethe writing task that keeps missing points, or your timetable changes every week.
  • Group is enough: you are starting A1 or A2, can attend at a fixed time and want to hear other learners make the same mistakes.
  • Neither yet: you have not tried the method. Do the free A1 Chapter 1 first; self-paced A1 costs ₹1,999.

Use the demo to test one correction

Bring one sentence you need, for example Ich wohne in Delhi und ich arbeite in einem Hotel. Ask the teacher for one correction, then say the improved sentence without reading it. A good demo leaves you with a correction you can use again the same week.

What to write down after the demo

What you were asked to do, the correction you understood, whether you could repeat it without help, the times the teacher can offer, and the number of lessons in the course.

What to send in your first message

“I’m at A2 and want to speak more confidently at work. I’m free on Tuesdays or Thursdays after 7 p.m. IST. How many lessons does the one-to-one A2 course include, and how long is each lesson?”
