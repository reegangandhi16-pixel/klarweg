Online German B2 Classes

Quick answer

B2 practice suits learners who can already explain an opinion and understand the main point of familiar texts. Choose self-paced study for flexible progression or check live B2 options if you need regular feedback on extended answers.

Compare the type of feedback you need

For reading and grammar, try a structured lesson at your own pace. For group discussion, ask how much speaking time each learner gets. For individual coaching, bring one talk or text you can revise after the session. Confirm level, format, total fees and attendance policy before enrolling.

Try a one-minute B2 response

Should employees be allowed to start work at different times? Acknowledge one difficulty, make your recommendation and give a condition for it to work.

Check one possible answer

Flexible Arbeitszeiten erschweren zwar gemeinsame Besprechungen, aber sie können den Arbeitsalltag erleichtern. Ich würde sie einführen, sofern es feste Zeiten für Absprachen gibt.

Give a concrete condition instead of another opinion. In a demo, ask your teacher to probe your condition and explain where your answer became unclear.
