Online German Course and Certificate Fees

Klarweg lists self-paced A1 at ₹1,999 for two years and Lifetime A1–C2 at ₹4,999. These are tuition prices. External exams and certificate fees depend on the issuing provider, exam and centre; check that fee separately before buying.

Check which document you are paying for

A course completion document tracks completion with a provider. A language exam result reports your performance in an exam. Before buying, ask the receiving institution which exam, level and result it accepts, if you need the document for an application. Check the course and exam provider separately for current fees and terms.

Budget itemWhat to ask
TuitionWhich level, support and access period are included?
Course completion documentWho issues it and is there a separate fee?
Exam registrationIs it included, or do you pay an exam centre separately?
Extra costsWhat would a reschedule, retake or trip to a centre cost?

Compare two fictional offers

Offer A is INR 4,000 for tuition and INR 6,000 for a separately booked exam. Offer B is INR 7,000 for tuition and a completion document, plus the same INR 6,000 exam. If the exam is required for your application, the comparable totals are INR 10,000 and INR 13,000 before travel or retakes. These are sample figures.

Copy a question for the course provider

“Please list the full fee, the name of the document you issue, and whether registration for the external exam is included. If it is included, which provider, level and exam date apply?”

Compare what you need before you pay

If your goal is to start learning, compare the free first chapter, A1 access and Lifetime by the levels you need now. If you need proof for an application, ask the receiving institution for the accepting exam, level and deadline first. Then add the quoted exam fee and travel to the tuition figure. Klarweg tuition is separate from external exam registration. See the current plan terms on the A1 pricing section.
