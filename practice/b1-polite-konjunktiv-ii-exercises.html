Understand the rule

Use könnte, würde, hätte and wäre to soften requests or imagine situations: Könnten Sie …? / Ich hätte gern … / Ich würde gern … . These forms are useful in appointments, shops and formal emails.

Common mistake to avoid

Könnten Sie is a polite request, while Können Sie may sound more direct. Both are grammatically possible.

How to practise: read the examples once, cover the English meanings and try explaining the choice aloud. Next, complete all four questions before opening their answers.
