Je ... Desto: German B2 Comparison Practice

Understand the rule

Je + comparative introduces a dependent clause; desto/umso + comparative starts the main clause. Example: Je mehr ich lerne, desto sicherer spreche ich. The verb in the je-clause is final.

Common mistake to avoid

Both halves need a comparison, not a plain adjective: je schneller ... desto besser.

How to practise: read the examples once, cover the English meanings and try explaining the choice aloud. Next, complete all four questions before opening their answers.

Four German examples with English meanings

Pay attention to the exact article, verb position or ending described in the rule.

Worked example 1Je mehr ich übe, desto besser spreche ich.The more I practise, the better I speak.
Worked example 2Je länger wir warten, desto ungeduldiger werden wir.The longer we wait, the more impatient we become.
Worked example 3Je früher du kommst, desto mehr Zeit haben wir.The earlier you come, the more time we have.
Worked example 4Je weniger er schläft, desto müder ist er.The less he sleeps, the more tired he is.

Practice: fill in the gaps

Try each question yourself first. Then open the answer to see both the correct form and the reason. The questions are independent practice, not exam scoring.

Question 1. Je mehr du liest, ___ größer wird dein Wortschatz.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: desto

Start the main-clause comparison.

Question 2. ___ schneller du lernst, desto früher bist du fertig.

Show answer and explanation

Answer: Je

Begin the dependent comparison.

Question 3. Je mehr ich lerne, desto ___ verstehe ich. (viel, comparative)

Show answer and explanation

Answer: mehr

Use the comparative mehr.

Question 4. Je länger wir warten, desto ___ werden wir. (nervös, comparative)

Show answer and explanation

Answer: nervöser

Use the comparative adjective.
