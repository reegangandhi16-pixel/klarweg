For Hindi speakers

The sounds that trip Hindi speakers up.

Hindi has no clean v/w split and no German ch. These pairs are the ones worth drilling first.

Pair 01 · v vs w
Vater
/ˈfaːtɐ/
faatar · father
Wasser
/ˈvasɐ/
vaasar · water

German v sounds like English f; German w sounds like English v.

Pair 02 · ich vs Buch
ich
/ɪç/
ish · soft hiss
Buch
/buːx/
bookh · throat

After i/e → soft ç. After a/o/u → hard x (like Hindi kh).

Pair 03 · ö & ü
schön
/ʃøːn/
round lips, say e
müde
/ˈmyːdə/
round lips, say ee

No Hindi equivalent — shape lips for o/u, but say e/ee inside.
