2. The service

Klarweg provides self-paced German-language learning content: color-coded grammar, stories, vocabulary, listening and speaking exercises, and quizzes, organised into chapters within six CEFR levels (A1–C2). Chapter 1 of every level is free to try without purchase. Klarweg also includes optional supporting features such as Saved Words, spaced-repetition review, and an in-lesson chapter assistant, which may vary in availability as the product evolves.
