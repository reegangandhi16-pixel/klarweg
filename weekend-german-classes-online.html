Quick answer

Klarweg has no weekend batch listed yet. Its 20-minute ₹100 teacher demos run on Friday and Sunday from 4:20 p.m. IST when slots are open, one-to-one lessons are scheduled with the teacher, and the self-paced course works on any day.

Does Klarweg run weekend classes?

Checked against the live classes system on 22 November 2026:

  • Weekend batch: none listed. No live course has dates yet, so there is no Saturday or Sunday batch to join.
  • Demo: 20-minute ₹100 demos run on Friday and Sunday from 4:20 p.m. IST, when the teacher opens slots. A Sunday demo is not a Sunday course.
  • One-to-one: lessons are scheduled with the teacher, so ask whether weekend times are possible. Fee per level: ₹22,000, or ₹15,000 with code PERSONAL7000.
  • Self-paced: available any day. Chapter 1 of every level is free; A1 costs ₹1,999 for two years.

If weekends are your only free time, the option that works today is self-paced study on Saturday and Sunday, with a Sunday demo if you want a teacher’s level check.

What to check in any weekend batch

  • Dates in IST: ask for the first and last class, and any breaks for festivals.
  • Hours: weekend-only courses often run fewer hours a week, so the same level takes more weeks. Ask for the total hours and divide the fee by them.
  • Missed classes: ask whether classes are recorded and what happens if you miss one.

A weekend timetable you can adapt

Example schedule, not a Klarweg batch: Saturday 10:00–11:30 IST for a lesson or one self-paced chapter, Sunday 10:00–10:45 for speaking and corrections, Tuesday and Thursday 15 minutes each to repeat the week’s phrases. That is 2 hours 45 minutes a week.

The weekday 15 minutes matter more than they look: without them, Saturday starts with forgetting last week. If weekdays are impossible, split Sunday into a short audio block, a speaking block and a review block.

A message to send before you book any weekend course

“I’m starting at A1 and can attend on Saturday mornings IST. Which course fits? Could you share all class dates, session length, total hours, the total fee and what happens if I miss a class?”

Try a short speaking task

Tell a partner when you are free: Am Samstag habe ich Zeit. Passt Ihnen zehn Uhr? Then ask them to suggest a different time and agree on it. If the reply Samstag passt mir leider nicht. Geht es am Sonntag um elf? is hard to follow, start with the free A1 Chapter 1.
