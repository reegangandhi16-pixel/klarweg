Weekend German Classes Online

Quick answer

Klarweg offers online German teacher demos for A1–B2. The 20-minute demo costs ₹100 and is advertised for Friday or Sunday after 4 p.m. IST, subject to available slots. Confirm the regular class days with the teacher: a weekend demo does not establish a weekend batch.

What to look for in a weekend batch

Before you compare fees, check which day, time zone and dates the teacher is offering. A Sunday demo and a Sunday course are separate appointments. If you are joining from India, ask for the full schedule in IST, including the last class and any holiday breaks.

  • Fixed weekend: Can you protect the same block through the course?
  • Rotating weekend: Ask how absence and rescheduling work before paying.
  • Weekend-only study: Reserve some of the block for reviewing errors after the live class.

A weekend timetable you can adapt

Illustrative schedule: Saturday 10:00–11:30 IST for a live class, Sunday 10:00–10:45 for speaking and reviewing corrections, Tuesday and Thursday 15 minutes each for recall. This is 2 hours and 45 minutes a week. It is an example schedule, not a listing of Klarweg batches.

If weekdays are unavailable, split Sunday into short audio, speaking and review blocks. At the beginning of the next Saturday session, try recalling last week’s phrases before opening your notes.

Questions to send before you book

“I’m starting at A1 and can attend on Saturday mornings IST. Which current course fits? Could you share all class dates, session length, total fees, and what happens if I miss one class?”

Try a short speaking task before the demo

Tell a partner when you are free. Start with Am Samstag habe ich Zeit. Passt Ihnen zehn Uhr? Then ask them to suggest a different time. Bring the sentence you found hardest to the demo so you can see how the teacher helps you revise it.

Decide whether a weekend course fits

Check three calendar dates now: your first session, a weekend you may miss, and your next review block. If you cannot attend the same time most weeks, choose self-paced A1 for the core lessons and book live speaking only around confirmed availability. Take your level, available times in IST and one speaking goal to the demo.
